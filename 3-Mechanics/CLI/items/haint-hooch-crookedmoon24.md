# Haint Hooch
*Potion, rare*  
![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheGiddyLimit/homebrew-img/refs/heads/main/img/CrookedMoon24/Items/Haint%20Hooch.webp#right)


When you drink from this glass jug, you become insubstantial for 1 minute. During this time, you can move through creatures and objects as if they were <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Difficult Terrain</span>, taking `dice:1d10|noform|noparens|avg` (`1d10`) Force damage if you end your turn inside an object.

The potion is stored in jugs labeled "XxX," with a skeletal smile drawn on it. The ghastly, blue-green liquor within has an overpowering smell and harsh taste.

## Heightened Potency

For the duration, you gain a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Fly Speed</span> equal to your <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Speed</span>.

*Source: The Crooked Moon (2024) p. 614*
