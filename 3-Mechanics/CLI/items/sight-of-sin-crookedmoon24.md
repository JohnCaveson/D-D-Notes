# Sight of Sin
*Potion, rare*  
![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheGiddyLimit/homebrew-img/refs/heads/main/img/CrookedMoon24/Items/Sight%20of%20Sin.webp#right)


When you drink this potion, you gain the effect of a Detect Magic spell (no Concentration required) for 10 minutes, and during that time, you have <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Advantage</span> on Wisdom (Insight) checks to discern hidden motives or deception.

This potion is stored in a round glass flask. An iron band wraps around the bottle, ornamented with a goat's skull.

## Heightened Potency

For the duration, you also gain the effect of the See Invisibility spell.

*Source: The Crooked Moon (2024) p. 616*
