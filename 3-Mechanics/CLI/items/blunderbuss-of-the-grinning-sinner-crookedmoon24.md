# Blunderbuss of the Grinning Sinner
*Weapon (musket) (requires attunement)*  
![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheGiddyLimit/homebrew-img/refs/heads/main/img/CrookedMoon24/Items/Blunderbuss%20of%20the%20Grinning%20Sinner.webp#right)

- **Properties**: [Fabled Heirloom](/3-Mechanics/CLI/item-properties.md#Fabled%20Heirloom)
- **Weight**: 10.0 lbs.

This dark, ornate blunderbuss has a brass barrel and a wooden handle etched with arcane symbols. It was the Grinning Sinner's weapon of choice, who imbued it with the power to shoot jinxes and hexes.

The blunderbuss functions as a Musket that ignores [Loading](/3-Mechanics/CLI/item-properties.md#Loading), and it can make its own ammunition that deals Necrotic damage on a hit. You gain a bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with it determined by the blunderbuss's rarity. While holding the blunderbuss, you can use the blunderbuss as a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Spellcasting Focus</span> for your spells.
