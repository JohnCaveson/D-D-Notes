# Harvestborn
*Source: The Crooked Moon (2024) p. 60*  
![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheGiddyLimit/homebrew-img/refs/heads/main/img/CrookedMoon24/Races/Harvestborn.webp#right)

- **Ability Scores**: None
- **Type**: construct
- **Size**: Medium
- **Speed**: 30 ft.
- **Spellcasting**: Choose one of Intelligence, Wisdom, Charisma

## Traits

### Culling

When you damage a Bloodied creature, you can deal an extra `dice:1d12|noform|noparens|avg` (`1d12`) Necrotic damage to it. You can use this trait a number of times equal to your <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Proficiency Bonus</span>, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Long Rest</span>.

### Gift of the Green

As a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Bonus Action</span>, you can touch the ground and choose a creature also touching the ground within 30 feet of yourself. That creature can expend and roll one <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Hit Points Die</span>, and it regains a number of <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Hit Points</span> equal to the number rolled plus your <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Proficiency Bonus</span>. You can use this trait a number of times equal to your <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Proficiency Bonus</span>. You regain one expended use when you finish a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Short Rest</span>, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Long Rest</span>.

### Jack-O-Lantern

You know the Dancing Lights cantrip. Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma is your spellcasting ability for it (choose the ability when you select this species).

### Scarecrow Nature

You don't require air, food, or drink.

### Watchful Rest

You don't need to sleep, and magic can't put you to sleep. Moreover, you can finish a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Long Rest</span> in 4 hours if you spend those hours in an inactive, motionless state, during which you retain consciousness.

## Description

Harvestborn are a scarecrow-like species native to "Enoch", most often crafted with great care by Methuselah, the lord of the province. Infused with the vital, green magic that surges through the immensely fertile land, they have a strong connection to the cycle of both the harvest and life, death, and rebirth. They feed much of Druskenvald with the crops they grow in a relatively peaceful society that puts a focus on community, family, and fellowship.

As a constructed species resembling any manner of scarecrow, their bodies are comprised of a mixture of wood, metal, straw, and other implements. Their glowing-eyed heads might be made from a burlap sack or a crop such as a pumpkin or turnip. Staying true to the cycle, they generally live 100 years before accepting the next step of death and becoming reborn once more.
