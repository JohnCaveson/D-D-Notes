### Haint - The Sweetheart

![[NPC_The_Sweetheart.png]]
- a [[Wayward Haint]] stares wistfully at a locket of a handsome mustachioed man with the initial "J.B." underneath the portrait. 
- She is lovesick and lonely, only barely registering the characters and bemoaning having to travel the afterlife alone. She isn't complete without her dear love, and she doesn't want to spend and eternity without without him.

```ad-tip
title: Resolutions
- Giving her the letter from Jakob Braun found in the Mail Car makes her realize that her love is already on the other side and is waiting for her in the afterlife.
---
**All checks made against a resolution start at DC 12 and increases by 2 per 
failure**

After 3 success the Haint is free
After 3 failures the Haint becomes hostile and attacks.
- **Calligrapher's Supplies** or **Performance** : Help her compose a poem or song about her love so she has something to soothe her longing for him
- **History** or **Persuasion** : Commiserate with her with personal tales of lost and regained love or recite legends or tales of the same
- **Investigation** or **Religion** : Steer her to remember details of her love's qualities in a real and practical way, suggesting they will be reunited when she reaches her destination.
```
