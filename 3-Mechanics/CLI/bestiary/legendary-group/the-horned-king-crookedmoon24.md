# The Horned King

## Regional Effects
_Source: The Crooked Moon (2024)_

The region containing the Horned King's lair is changed by its presence, creating the following effects:

- **Sweet Temptation.** Within 1 mile of the lair, a creature that acts on a sinful impulse to the detriment of a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Friendly</span> or <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Indifferent</span> creature increases its <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Hit Point</span> maximum and current <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Hit Points</span> by 10 until it finishes a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Long Rest</span>. Creatures are aware of this effect. This can't occur again until the creature finishes a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Long Rest</span>.  
- **Tenacious Curses.** Within 1 mile of the lair, saving throws against curses have <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Disadvantage</span>.  

If the Horned King dies or moves its lair elsewhere, these effects end immediately.
