

> [!danger]+ Happenings
> - The players first encountered the Druskenvalds on the Ghostlight Express where Adela proceeded to give each of the players a Tarot reading. 
> - After a hard-won fight with a Soulflame Stoker and a crashed Ghostlight Express, the players found themselves in a wooded glen in Wickermoor Hollow with the Vagrant and the Druskenvalds
> - After a fight with the Ghostlight Express itself, the Vagrant having left after the events of the night, the players found themselves alone with the Druskenvalds; Phillip knew of Wickermoor Village ~4 hours away where the Druskenvalds had a home waiting on them. 
> - Upon making the trek, the cohort comes upon Wickermoor Village where something of a ritual is taking place. After not interceding, they are introduced to [[Wendel Somerton|the Mayor]] who then informs the party and the Druskenvalds of the town and show each to their respective lodgings for the evening.
> - The Druskenvalds invite the party over for dinner the following night after a nice rest.
