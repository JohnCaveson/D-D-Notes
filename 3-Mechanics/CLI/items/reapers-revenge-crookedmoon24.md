# Reaper's Revenge
*Potion, rare*  
![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheGiddyLimit/homebrew-img/refs/heads/main/img/CrookedMoon24/Items/Reapers%20Revenge.webp#right)


When you drink this potion, you are filled with intense and violent vigor, gaining `dice:3d8|noform|noparens|avg` (`3d8`) <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Temporary Hit Points</span>. For 1 minute, when one of your enemies dies within 30 feet of you, you can take a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Reaction</span> to regain `dice:1d8|noform|noparens|avg` (`1d8`) <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Hit Points</span>.

The base of this glass potion bottle is shaped like a skull, and the images of scythes run up its neck.

## Heightened Potency

For 1 minute after drinking this potion, when you hit a Bloodied creature with an attack roll and deal damage to it, the creature takes an extra `dice:1d8|noform|noparens|avg` (`1d8`) Necrotic damage.

*Source: The Crooked Moon (2024) p. 616*
