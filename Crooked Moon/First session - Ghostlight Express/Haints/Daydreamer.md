### The Daydreamer

![[NPC_The_Daydreamer.png]]

- Sitting in a booth in one of the corners of the Dining Car is a [[Wayward Haint]] of a youthful boy in overalls, hungrily eating all manner of food that he repeatedly conjures forth; his face is wracked with *guilt* as he tries to eat the feeling away.
- Wistful and childlike in responses and often speaking in nonsense. Doesn't dodge questions on purpose, it just kind of happens.
- The one thing he does share openly:
	- **Immense guilt that he feels for stealing a stone painted to look like a toad from his neighbor's garden before passing on Because he lost the rock on the train, he will never be able to return it to her.
