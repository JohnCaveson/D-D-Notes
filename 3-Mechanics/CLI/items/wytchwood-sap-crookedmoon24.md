# Wytchwood Sap
*Potion, rare*  
![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheGiddyLimit/homebrew-img/refs/heads/main/img/CrookedMoon24/Items/Wytchwood%20Sap.webp#right)


As a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Bonus Action</span>, you can coat one weapon or up to three pieces of ammunition with this sticky sap and choose a damage type: Acid, Cold, Fire, Lightning, Poison, or Thunder. A creature that takes damage from the coated weapon or ammunition takes an extra `dice:3d8|noform|noparens|avg` (`3d8`) damage of the chosen type. Once applied, the sap retains its potency for 1 minute or until its damage is dealt, whichever comes first.

Wytchwood Sap is stored in small vessels made of bark with a wooden stopper that never fully contains this oozing, amber-hued tar.

## Heightened Potency

If you roll the same number on two or more of the d8s, another creature of your choice within 30 feet of your target takes `dice:2d8|noform|noparens|avg` (`2d8`) damage of the same type.

*Source: The Crooked Moon (2024) p. 617*
