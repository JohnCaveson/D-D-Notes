# Crooked Moon - Major Arcana Tarot
*Source: The Crooked Moon (2024)*  

Below are all 22 Major Arcana cards that can be pulled in the Fated Tarot Reading, along with Adela Druskenvald's interpretation of the cards, who always pulls the cards in the upright position. You'll also find the Fabled Heirloom or boon that each card corresponds to, as well as where it can be found in The Crooked Moon adventure. For all Fabled Heirlooms, see appendix B.

## Cards

### The Fool
![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheGiddyLimit/homebrew-img/main/img/CrookedMoon24/Adventure/023-02-006.the-fool.webp#card)
>  "Ooooo! The Fool! I love this card! New adventures, new beginnings, new opportunities; who could say no to that? It's almost like it's telling you that a new journey awaits you the moment you step off this train, don't you think?" 

If *The Fool* is pulled, the [Whistle of the Vagrant](/3-Mechanics/CLI/items/whistle-of-the-vagrant-crookedmoon24.md) is left in the grass after the defeat of [The Ghostlight Locomotive](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/npc/the-ghostlight-locomotive-crookedmoon24.md).

### The Moon
![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheGiddyLimit/homebrew-img/main/img/CrookedMoon24/Adventure/041-02-024.the-moon.webp#card)
>  "You wouldn't think the Moon was eerie by the name alone, but I'm tellin' ya, it is! You are surrounded by illusions and trickery, and nothing is what it seems to be. You must seek truth amidst the madness, even when you aren't sure if you can trust yourself." 

If *The Moon* is pulled, the character this card was pulled for receives three Twists of Fate that last until they are expended after defeating [Vessla Browntooth](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/npc/vessla-browntooth-crookedmoon24.md) in the Crooked House. Once per turn, when a creature the character can see makes a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">D20 Test</span>, the character can expend one Twist of Fate to replace the `dice:d20|noform|noparens|avg` (`d20`) roll with a 1 or a 20.
