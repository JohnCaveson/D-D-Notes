# The White Worm

## Regional Effects
_Source: The Crooked Moon (2024)_

The region containing the White Worm's lair is changed by its presence, creating the following effects:

- **Alien Growth.** Strange life grows rampant within 1 mile of the lair. In that region, Grick, Purple Worm, and [Wormhost Surgeons](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/aberration/wormhost-surgeon-crookedmoon24.md) are common.  
- **Psychic Phantoms.** Within 1 mile of the lair, creatures are plagued by horrid nightmares. When a creature sleeps or enters a Trance and finishes a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Long Rest</span> in that area, it must succeed on a DC 15 Wisdom saving throw or gain no benefit from finishing the rest.  

If the White Worm dies or moves its lair elsewhere, these effects end immediately.
