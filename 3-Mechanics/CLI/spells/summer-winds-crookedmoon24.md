# Summer Winds
*2nd-level, Evocation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You unleash a blast of light and wind in a 15-foot <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Cone</span>. Your allies in the <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Cone</span> regain `dice:2d6|noform|noparens|avg` (`2d6`) <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Hit Points</span>. Undead within the <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Cone</span> that are <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Hostile</span> to you must make a Constitution saving throw, taking `dice:2d6|noform|noparens|avg` (`2d6`) Radiant damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** The damage and healing increase by `dice:2d6|noform|noparens|avg` (`2d6`) for each spell slot level above 2.
