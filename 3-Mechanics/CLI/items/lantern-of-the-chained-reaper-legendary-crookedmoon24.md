# Lantern of the Chained Reaper (Legendary)
*Wondrous item, legendary (requires attunement)*  
![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheGiddyLimit/homebrew-img/refs/heads/main/img/CrookedMoon24/Items/Lantern%20of%20the%20Chained%20Reaper.webp#right)

- **Properties**: [Fabled Heirloom](/3-Mechanics/CLI/item-properties.md#Fabled%20Heirloom)

This ornate Hooded Lantern was once a weapon in the hands of the Chained Reaper, filled with ghastly balefire. Able to pierce the veil, the lantern reveals a glimpse at things hidden from mortal eyes.

While you hold the lantern and it is lit, you gain a +3 bonus to Intelligence (Investigation) and Wisdom (Perception) checks determined by the lantern's rarity, and you can use the following properties.

## Ghostly Flame (Rare+)

The lantern doesn't require fuel, and it sheds blue-green light. It casts <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Bright Light</span> in a 30-foot radius and <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Dim Light</span> for an additional 30 feet. You can snuff or ignite its light as a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Bonus Action</span> while you are within 30 feet of it.

## Revealing Flame (Rare+)

Creatures in the Border Ethereal within the area of the lantern's <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Bright Light</span> are visible to creatures on the Material Plane. When a creature within the lantern's <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Bright Light</span> is possessed or affected by the Magic Jar spell, the creature in control appears overlaid on the body in a spectral form.

## Mystic Sight (Very Rare+)

You can cast Detect Magic from the lantern. The spell ends early if you extinguish the light or aren't holding the lantern.

## Summon Spirit (Very Rare+)

As a Magic action, you can summon a Ghost that appears in an unoccupied space you can see within 30 feet of you. The ghost obeys your commands, and it takes its turn immediately after you on your <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Initiative</span> count. The ghost vanishes after 1 minute, or when it dies. You can't use the lantern's properties, except for Ghostly Flame, during this time.

## Balefire (Legendary)

You can cast Scorching Ray (level 5 version, +11 to hit) from the lantern. This property can be used three times, regaining all expended uses daily at dusk.

## Warding Light (Legendary)

The lantern's area of <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Bright Light</span> offers the protection of a Magic Circle spell. This protection can't operate in the reverse direction, and it ends if you aren't holding the lantern or if you have the Incapacitated condition.

*Source: The Crooked Moon (2024) p. 601*
