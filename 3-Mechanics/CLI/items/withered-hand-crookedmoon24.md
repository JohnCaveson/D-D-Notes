# Withered Hand
*Wondrous item, cursed item, rare (requires attunement)*  
![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheGiddyLimit/homebrew-img/refs/heads/main/img/CrookedMoon24/Items/Withered%20Hand.webp#right)

- **Properties**: [Cursed](/3-Mechanics/CLI/item-properties.md#Cursed%20Items), [Cursed Curio](/3-Mechanics/CLI/item-properties.md#Cursed%20Curio)

The skin of this desiccated hand on a looped leather cord is covered with tattoos signifying dreadful hexes. The fingers seem to curl slightly when the hand is at the edge of your vision. While you hold or wear the hand, you gain a +1 bonus to spell attack rolls and spell save DCs. The hand grants the following properties.

## Withering Grasp

When you make an <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Unarmed Strike</span>, you can extend your reach to 30 feet with threads of magenta jinx magic. When you do so, on a hit, you deal Necrotic damage instead of Bludgeoning damage, and if the target is a creature no more than one size larger than you, you can give it the Grappled condition with the threads. The escape DC for this grapple can use your spellcasting ability or Charisma (your choice) in place of your Strength. The Grappled condition ends if you use this property against a different target.

## Withering Curse

When you use the Withering Grasp property, your <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Hit Point</span> maximum decreases by 1. Your <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Hit Point</span> maximum can't decrease again until you finish a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Long Rest</span>. You don't regain the <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Hit Point</span> maximum lost to this curse when you finish a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Long Rest</span>.

*Source: The Crooked Moon (2024) p. 612*
