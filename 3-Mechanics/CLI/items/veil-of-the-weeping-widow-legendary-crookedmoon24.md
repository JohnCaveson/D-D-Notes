# Veil of the Weeping Widow (Legendary)
*Wondrous item, legendary (requires attunement)*  
![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheGiddyLimit/homebrew-img/refs/heads/main/img/CrookedMoon24/Items/Veil%20of%20the%20Weeping%20Widow.webp#right)

- **Properties**: [Fabled Heirloom](/3-Mechanics/CLI/item-properties.md#Fabled%20Heirloom)

This mourning veil is imbued with alien power. The Weeping Widow wore the veil to hide her permanently tear-stained face, and rumors circulate that the widow used it to hide the otherworldly corruption writhing within her eyes.

While wearing the veil, you gain a +3 bonus to Wisdom checks and Wisdom saving throws determined by the veil's rarity, and you can use the following properties.

## Psychic Fortitude (Rare+)

You have <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Resistance</span> to Psychic damage.

## Sense Grief (Rare+)

You can cast Detect Thoughts (save DC = 16 + the veil's bonus) from the veil.

## Psychic Shock (Very Rare+)

When a creature forces you to make a saving throw against Psychic damage, or to avoid or end the Charmed or Frightened conditions, you can take a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Reaction</span> to deal `dice:2d8|noform|noparens|avg` (`2d8`) Psychic damage to it.

## Thought Worm (Very Rare+)

You can use the veil to cast Modify Memory (save DC = 16 + the veil's bonus). When a creature fails its saving throw against the spell, you can implant a parasitic spawn of the White Worm in the target's brain. While you're on the same plane, you know the distance and direction to the target, and you can cast Divination and Enchantment spells on it as if it were in range and in sight. A Greater Restoration or Heal spell kills the parasite and ends this effect. Once this property is used, it can't be used again until the next dusk.

## Psychic Storm (Legendary)

You can take one extra <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Reaction</span> during a round, which you can only use to activate the Psychic Shock property.

## Veiled Countenance (Legendary)

When you are the target of a Divination spell, the caster must succeed on a DC 19 Wisdom saving throw. On a failed save, the caster takes `dice:6d8|noform|noparens|avg` (`6d8`) Psychic damage and has the Incapacitated condition for 1 minute. At the end of each of the caster's turns, it repeats the saving throw, ending the effect on itself on a success.

*Source: The Crooked Moon (2024) p. 606*
