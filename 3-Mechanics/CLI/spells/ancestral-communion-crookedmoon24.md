# Ancestral Communion
*1st-level, Divination*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

You invoke the wisdom of your ancestors to gain insight into your trials. When you cast the spell, and at the start of each of your turns for the duration, you gain <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Heroic Inspiration</span> if you don't already have it.
