### The Jailbird

![[NPC_The_Jailbird.png]]
- Slumped up against a tattered suit case is a [[Wayward Haint]] of a grizzled man in striped clothing with a ball and chain around his left ankle. His stomach grumbles with a loud, rumbling gurgle ashe lets out weary groans, his face scrunched with hunger pangs.
- Talks a lot about foods that sound delicious to eat; when pressed he says that he was executed before getting the last meal of **Steak and Eggs** he was promised, and it's all he's been able to think about since.
