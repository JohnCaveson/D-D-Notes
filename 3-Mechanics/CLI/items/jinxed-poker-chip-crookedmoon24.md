# Jinxed Poker Chip
*Wondrous item, rare (requires attunement)*  
![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheGiddyLimit/homebrew-img/refs/heads/main/img/CrookedMoon24/Items/Jinxed%20Poker%20Chip.webp#right)

- **Properties**: [Cursed Curio](/3-Mechanics/CLI/item-properties.md#Cursed%20Curio)

This ceramic poker chip is painted with a crocodile's skull on one side. Hoarse laughter sounds when it flips. While you hold the chip, it grants the following properties.

## Jinxed Luck

When you fail a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">D20 Test</span>, you can take a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Reaction</span> to flip the chip. Roll `dice:1d6|noform|noparens|avg` (`1d6`). On a 4-6, you succeed on the <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">D20 Test</span> instead. After you use this property, roll `dice:1d20|noform|noparens|avg` (`1d20`). On a 1-5, this property can't be used again until the next dusk.

## Sense Cheating

If you have proficiency with a Gaming Set, you automatically succeed on ability checks to know if someone in a game you are playing is cheating.

## Cursed Coin

When you roll <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Initiative</span>, you must succeed on a DC 0 Charisma saving throw or become cursed until you finish a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Short</span> or <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Long Rest</span>. While cursed, you subtract `dice:1d6|noform|noparens|avg` (`1d6`) from <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">D20 Tests</span>. When you use the Jinxed Luck property, the saving throw DC increases by 1.

*Source: The Crooked Moon (2024) p. 610*
