# Whistle of the Vagrant (Rare)
*Wondrous item, rare (requires attunement)*  
![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheGiddyLimit/homebrew-img/refs/heads/main/img/CrookedMoon24/Items/Whistle%20of%20the%20Vagrant.webp#right)

- **Properties**: [Fabled Heirloom](/3-Mechanics/CLI/item-properties.md#Fabled%20Heirloom)

This tarnished brass whistle belonged to the conductor of the Ghostlight Express, imbuing it with ghastly, mysterious powers. Its metal is cold to the touch, and its sound is an unnerving shriek.

While you hold or wear the whistle, you gain a +1 bonus determined by the whistle's rarity to all saving throws, and you can use the following properties.

## Call the Dead (Rare+)

As a Magic action, you can blow the whistle to cast Gentle Repose on all dead bodies within 30 feet of you. Once this property is used, it can't be used again until the next dusk.

## Sense the Undead (Rare+)

As a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Bonus Action</span>, you can detect the presence and location of Undead within 60 feet of you, even through <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Total Cover</span> of any material other than iron or lead.

## Undaunted (Rare+)

You have <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Advantage</span> on saving throws you make to avoid or end the Frightened condition.

*Source: The Crooked Moon (2024) p. 607*
