# Azureborn
*Source: The Crooked Moon (2024) p. 54*  
![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheGiddyLimit/homebrew-img/refs/heads/main/img/CrookedMoon24/Races/Azureborn.webp#right)

- **Ability Scores**: None
- **Type**: humanoid
- **Size**: Medium
- **Speed**: walk: 30 ft.; fly equal to your walking speed
- **Spellcasting**: Choose one of Intelligence, Wisdom, Charisma

## Traits

### Azure Legacy

You know the Guidance cantrip. Starting at character level 3, you always have the Augury spell prepared and can cast it once without a spell slot or Material components, and you regain the ability to cast it in that way when you finish a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Long Rest</span>. You can also cast it using any spell slots you have of the appropriate level.

Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma is your spellcasting ability for the spells you cast with this trait (choose the ability when you select this species).

### Darkvision

You have Darkvision with a range of 60 feet.

### Glimpse Fate

When you roll <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Initiative</span>, if you aren't surprised, you can choose a number of creatures that you can see equal to your <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Proficiency Bonus</span>. The targets have <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Advantage</span> on their <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Initiative</span> rolls. Once you use this trait, you can't do so again until you finish a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Short</span> or <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Long Rest</span>.

### Winds of Magic

As a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Bonus Action</span>, you can take the Dash action and increase your <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Fly Speed</span> by 10 feet until the end of the turn. You can use this trait a number of times equal to your <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Proficiency Bonus</span>, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Long Rest</span>.

## Description

Azureborn (*A‑zher‑born*) are a harpy-like species native to "Astramar", often materializing from  the bright blue night sky filled with twinkling stars  and winds of arcane power. Bearing a connection to  the aither that courses through the air and land of the province, they are an inherently prescient folk that can divine the future by looking to the cosmos. They have built a society of magical wonder through grand vision, endless curiosity, and dedication to study.

Sprouting feathers across their entire body and wings attached to each of their arms, this diverse folk can resemble all manner of avian creatures, from raptors with earthy tones to tropical birds of vibrant and colorful plumage. They typically live 100 years, although many of the wisest among them have lived up to 400.
