# Shovel of Yorgrim (Legendary)
*Weapon (greatclub), legendary (requires attunement)*  
![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheGiddyLimit/homebrew-img/refs/heads/main/img/CrookedMoon24/Items/Shovel%20of%20Yorgrim.webp#right)

- **Damage**: 1d8 bludgeoning
- **Properties**: [Fabled Heirloom](/3-Mechanics/CLI/item-properties.md#Fabled%20Heirloom), [Two-Handed](/3-Mechanics/CLI/item-properties.md#Two-Handed)
- **Weight**: 10.0 lbs.

This shovel features a steel blade and collar at the end of a makeshift walking stick, appearing old and well-used. It was once the shovel of Yorgrim of the Shadestone Clan, who could commune with the spirits of the dead. It occasionally emits a faint whispering sound when handled.

You gain a +3 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls with this magic weapon determined by its rarity. While holding the shovel, you can use the following properties.

## Death-Kenning (Rare+)

When you hit an Undead with the shovel, you can take a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Bonus Action</span> immediately after the hit to learn its <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Resistances</span>, <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Immunities</span>, and <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Vulnerabilities</span>, if any.

## Deathspeaker (Rare+)

You can cast Gentle Repose and Speak with Dead from the shovel.

## Deny Death's Touch (Very Rare+)

You and your allies within a 10-foot <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Emanation</span> originating from you have <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Resistance</span> to Necrotic damage.

## Bind Deathly Servant (Legendary)

When a Humanoid dies within 30 feet of you, you can take a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Reaction</span> to turn its soul into a Specter that obeys your commands. If the Humanoid was <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Friendly</span> to you when it died, you can turn the soul into a Wraith instead. You can control one creature at a time with this property. The Undead disappears after 8 hours, when you create a new creature with this property, or when it dies.

## Psychopomp (Legendary)

As a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Magic</span> action, you and one willing creature that isn't Undead that you touch become wraithlike until the end of your next turn. While wraithlike, you can move through other creatures and objects as if they were <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Difficult Terrain</span>. If you end your turn inside an object, you are shunted to the nearest unoccupied space and take `dice:1d10|noform|noparens|avg` (`1d10`) Force damage. If the creature you touch is Undead, it must succeed on a DC 19 Charisma saving throw or have <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Vulnerability</span> to all damage until the start of your next turn. This property can be used three times, regaining all expended uses daily at dusk.

*Source: The Crooked Moon (2024) p. 604*
