# Jinxed Rum
*Potion, uncommon*  
![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheGiddyLimit/homebrew-img/refs/heads/main/img/CrookedMoon24/Items/Jinxed%20Rum.webp#right)


When you drink this brightly colored rum, your luck takes a strange turn for 1 hour. For the duration, when you make a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">D20 Test</span>, roll `dice:1d4|noform|noparens|avg` (`1d4`). On a 2-4, add the number to your roll. On a 1, roll an extra `dice:d20|noform|noparens|avg` (`d20`) and use the lowest roll for the test.

This potion is stored in a glass rum bottle marked with crossbones.

## Heightened Potency

Roll `dice:1d6|noform|noparens|avg` (`1d6`) instead of `dice:1d4|noform|noparens|avg` (`1d4`).

*Source: The Crooked Moon (2024) p. 615*
