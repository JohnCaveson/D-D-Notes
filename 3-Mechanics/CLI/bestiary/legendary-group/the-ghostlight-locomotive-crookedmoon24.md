# The Ghostlight Locomotive

## Regional Effects
_Source: The Crooked Moon (2024)_

The region containing the Ghostlight Locomotive's lair is changed by its presence, creating the following effects:

- **Ghastly Shroud.** When a creature that isn't Undead finishes a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Short</span> or <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Long Rest</span> within 1 mile of the locomotive, the creature must succeed on a DC 15 Constitution saving throw or any time it regains <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Hit Points</span>, it only regains half the amount. This effect ends when the creature next finishes a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Long Rest</span> or receives a Lesser Restoration spell or similar magic.  
- **Spirit Calling.** Within 1 mile of the locomotive, <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Hostile</span> [Shrouded](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/undead/shrouded-crookedmoon24.md) and Specters are abundant and drawn to any creature that attacks the Ghostlight  

    Locomotive or that tries to keep pace with, climb, or enter the *Ghostlight Express*.  

If the Ghostlight Locomotive dies, these effects end immediately.
