# The Vermintoll Abomination

## Regional Effects
_Source: The Crooked Moon (2024)_

The region containing the Vermintoll Abomination's lair is changed by its presence, creating the following effects:

- **Endless Vermin.** Within 1 mile of the lair, Giant Centipedes, Giant Weasels, and pigeons (use the Raven stat block) are common nuisances. They steal Tiny objects and befoul food.  
- **Loyal Dwelling.** The lair itself, including any greater structure or complex it resides within, rejects intruders. Ability checks to pick locks or find secret doors have <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Disadvantage</span>.  

If the Vermintoll Abomination dies or moves its lair elsewhere, these effects end immediately.
