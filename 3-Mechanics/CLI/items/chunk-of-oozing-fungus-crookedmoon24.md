# Chunk of Oozing Fungus
*Wondrous item, rare (requires attunement)*  
![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheGiddyLimit/homebrew-img/refs/heads/main/img/CrookedMoon24/Items/Chunk%20of%20Oozing%20Fungus.webp#right)

- **Properties**: [Cursed Curio](/3-Mechanics/CLI/item-properties.md#Cursed%20Curio)

This fist-sized fungal growth is a pallid color, is oozy and lumpy, and frequently fills the air with spores. The fungus has the following properties.

## Spore Cloud

As a Magic action, you can throw the fungus up to 30 feet. The fungus is destroyed and explodes into a cloud of spores in a 30-foot <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Sphere</span> centered on that point. Each creature other than you in the area must make a DC 14 Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, a target takes `dice:3d8|noform|noparens|avg` (`3d8`) Poison damage and has the Poisoned condition until the start of your next turn. A creature that fails this save by 5 or more also has the Incapacitated condition. On a successful save, a target takes half damage only.

## Regrow

You can grow another Chunk of Oozing Fungus by coughing green-black spores into your hand as a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Bonus Action</span>. When you do so, you must succeed on a DC 10 Constitution saving throw or your <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Hit Point</span> maximum decreases by 5.

## Fungal Curse

You have a -1 penalty to Constitution saving throws. This penalty increases by 1 immediately after you succeed or fail on the saving throw from the Regrow property.

*Source: The Crooked Moon (2024) p. 609*
