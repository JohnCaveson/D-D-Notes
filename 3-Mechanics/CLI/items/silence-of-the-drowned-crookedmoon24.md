# Silence of the Drowned
*Potion, uncommon*  
![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheGiddyLimit/homebrew-img/refs/heads/main/img/CrookedMoon24/Items/Silence%20of%20the%20Drowned.webp#right)


When you drink this potion, you gain the effect of the Silence spell (no Concentration required) as a 20-foot <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Emanation</span> originating from you for 10 minutes, and for the duration, squid tentacles emerge from your mouth.

This sculpted clay jar is painted in a deep blue and adorned with squid tentacles. Uncorking it reduces all noise in the area as if you are underwater, and the liquid within is a coppery blue-green.

## Heightened Potency

For the duration, you can spit ink from your mouth as a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Bonus Action</span>. One creature within 30 feet of you makes a DC 13 Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, the target gains the Blinded condition until the start of your next turn. On a successful save, the target is immune to this property for the next 24 hours.

*Source: The Crooked Moon (2024) p. 617*
