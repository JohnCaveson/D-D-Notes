# Tincture of Mourning
*Potion, rare*  
![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheGiddyLimit/homebrew-img/refs/heads/main/img/CrookedMoon24/Items/Tincture%20of%20Mourning.webp#right)


When you drink this potion, you weep with inexpressible sorrow for 1 hour. During that time, you have <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Immunity</span> to the Charmed condition, but your continued wailing prevents you from being silent. If you are Charmed when you drink the potion, the condition is suppressed for the duration. This glass bottle is topped with a small crypt angel carved from stone. The fluid within has the taste and consistency of tears and the colors of a rain cloud.

## Heightened Potency

Creatures within 10 feet of you that are <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Friendly</span> to you also gain <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Immunity</span> to the Charmed condition.

*Source: The Crooked Moon (2024) p. 617*
