# Skitterdeep Mine
*Source: The Crooked Moon Part Two: Monsters & Adventure Campaign, p. 305* 

![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheGiddyLimit/homebrew-img/refs/heads/main/img/CrookedMoon24/Adventure/199-09-001.skitterdeep-mine-opener.webp#center)

> [!quote] A quote from Mrs. Oakum  
> 
> "I can't go no further. It's the mines. The hills 'round here are snaked full of tunnels, and she's down in there. I hear her. She was never 'round these parts until the big cave-in 'bout seventy years back. My pa used to tell me about it.
> 
> Fifty men got trapped down there, and from the twilight hour to the witching hour, the hills were full of her centipedes, big as hounds, all skittering down into that mine. Pa said there was some screaming down there for a while, then nothing. She's a special kind of evil."

A skittering infestation scrapes beneath the soil, burrowing ever deeper into the heart of Wickermoor Hollow and its people. From the depths of what was once Glitterdeep Mine, rich with veins of gold, silver, and iron, the contamination spreads. Seventy years ago, a suspicious cave-in occurred, and the few rescuers who returned spoke of terrors before they condemned the mine, leaving it abandoned.

Now called Skitterdeep Mine after its many-legged inhabitants, it is mentioned only in hushed tones, with wary glances over shoulders in fear of the crawling minions and the hag rumored to have brought ruin upon its darkened tunnels.

## Chapter Background

A trio of ancient hags known as The Vermintoll Coven has long wrought their wicked magic upon the fields, forests, and villages of Wickermoor Hollow. Seventy years ago, the [centipede hag](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/fey/centipede-hag-crookedmoon24.md) Stonoga Blackstinger corrupted Skitterdeep Mine—a once thriving source of precious ores.

Using illusion, hearty food, and strong drink, she wormed her way into the miners' trust. Then, she poisoned and twisted them all into grotesque abominations and collapsed the tunnels, trapping the wretched souls within. A cadre of knights attempted a rescue, but they too fell to Stonoga's influence as she warped their flesh to create new minions.

Over the years, fear and superstition shrouded the Skitterdeep Mine. Travelers still vanish near its entrance, taken as material for her pestilent experiments. The infestation has now spread beyond the mine, skittering throughout the walls, beneath the earth, and within the bodies of Wickermoor Village.

## Chapter Summary

Wickermoor Hollow is infested with countless centipedes that devour foodstuffs, spread disease, and even burrow into the bodies of the townsfolk. The source of the infestation is the [centipede hag](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/fey/centipede-hag-crookedmoon24.md) Stonoga Blackstinger, who seeks a new supply of human flesh for her twisted experiments deep within her abandoned mine. With each passing night, the vermin that have taken over town claim more victims and devour more food stores, threatening widespread death and famine. Characters drawn to the mine by the Quest Hooks later in this chapter have these goals:

- Discover and trace the source of the tunneling vermin to Skitterdeep Mine, and face the horrors within.  
- Destroy Stonoga Blackstinger and end the Skitter Plague.  

Before running this chapter, familiarize yourself with the centipede hag Stonoga Blackstinger, the key NPC. Review the Quest Hooks to provide a compelling reason for the characters to venture to the abandoned mine. Finally, read through the locations of Skitterdeep Mine so you are ready to improvise encounters as the characters explore.

This chapter is designed for four level 7 characters.

## The Centipede Hag

Stonoga Blackstinger *(stoh-NOH-guh)* commands countless swarms of large, squirming centipedes that skitter throughout the mine. Hideously misshapen miners, knights, and other victims of her fleshcrafting also wander the tunnels and serve their maker without question.

### Stonoga Blackstinger

![Stonoga Blackstinger and Slippery Oswald](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheGiddyLimit/homebrew-img/refs/heads/main/img/CrookedMoon24/Adventure/200-09-002.stonoga-blackstinger-and-slippery-oswald.webp#center)

Stonoga Blackstinger ([Centipede Hag](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/fey/centipede-hag-crookedmoon24.md)) is the middle sister of the Vermintoll Coven. Her unnaturally tall and lanky body has morphed overthe years to more closely resemble the centipedes she favors, losing her nose and most of her toes and stretching her neck out by several feet.

Just as it was her sister Vessla's role in the coven to twist souls in preparation for sacrifice to the Crooked Queen, Stonoga is tasked with corrupting bodies. She cares not for the matters of mind and spirit, as all life to her is simply meat to be molded beneath her jagged fingers. But despite this, she has a jovial disposition, taking glee in savoring the suffering of her victims.

Unlike her sisters, she prefers to hide away in the deepest, darkest holes in the earth where she can be embraced by the squirming swarms of her beloved centipedes. It is in these places that never see the light of the moon where she conducts her alchemy, witchcraft, and fleshcrafting.

Stonoga has been waiting for the night that her younger sister Vessla is slain, a moment foreseen and deemed a necessary sacrifice by her older sister Golub. The two older hags conspired to allow the weasel hag to be butchered in the Crooked House, but Stonoga has no idea that she herself was deceived. Golub Graygullet believed the middle sister needed to die too, as whispered by the Crooked Queen with the promise of being elevated within the inevitable crooked court of Kehlenn. Golub will aid the characters in hunting down Stonoga if she can.

### Slippery Oswald

[Slippery Oswald](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/npc/slippery-oswald-crookedmoon24.md) ([Vermin Familiar (Centipede)](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/fey/vermin-familiar-centipede-crookedmoon24.md)) is a rough approximation of a centipede the size of a hound, with grotesquely human-like hands at the end of each appendage and an unsettling human grin. The familiar of Stonoga Blackstinger, he shares his mistress's glee in the horrid reshaping of flesh into new forms. He can command the squirming hordes of centipedes that crawl throughout Wickermoor Hollow.

Rather than being a collector of body parts like his weasel counterpart Filthy Jasper, Oswald prefers to create grotesque homes out of his master's victims. He burrows deep into Humanoid carcasses and rearranges the rotting innards to his liking, often lurking within for weeks at a time. Sometimes, he even digs into a healthy person to corrupt and prime their body for flesh shaping while they still live.

## Quest Hooks

Wickermoor Village is crawling with centipedes as a sudden illness rips through its people. The sick only have a few nights to live before succumbing to the affliction. A solution must be found before it's too late.

### Infestation

The village is infested with thousands of centipedes sent by Stonoga Blackstinger. These vermin consume food stores, burrow into the flesh of the living, and spread a magical contagion that afflicts those bitten by the creatures (see "Skitter Plague" below).

After the characters finish a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Long Rest</span> in Wickermoor Village, four [Skitterswarms](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/fey/skitterswarm-crookedmoon24.md) emerge from the walls and floor of wherever they are staying and attack. Creatures hit by the swarms' attacks must succeed on a DC 15 Constitution saving throw or be afflicted with Skitter Plague. Dozens of small mundane centipedes crawl throughout the floorboards as well.

When the characters step out into the streets of Wickermoor Village after dispatching the skitterswarms, many people are gathered here having fled their homes from centipede infestations. When asked about what's going on, the plight is consistent across the townsfolk's stories:

- They awoke with dozens of centipedes crawling in their beds, many having been bitten.  
- Their stored food is infested with centipedes, and much of it has been eaten.  
- Some larger, more grotesque, and especially vicious centipedes have dug into certain people's skin.  
- People quickly started falling ill, coming down with fever, breaking out into mauve-colored pustules, and vomiting black bile.  

Over the next few hours, ten more villagers succumb to the unknown malady, all with similar stories. If nothing is done, the Skitter Plague will become an epidemic.

#### Skitter Plague

*Magical Contagion*

Skitter Plague was created by the hag Stonoga Blackstinger, and it spreads through the bites of her grotesque centipedes. A creature bitten by any kind of centipede under Stonoga's control must succeed on a DC 12 Constitution saving throw or suffer the following effects `dice:1d4|noform|noparens|avg` (`1d4`) hours after infection:

##### Fever

The creature gains 1 Exhaustion level that can't be removed until the contagion ends on the creature.

##### Crawling Blisters

Blisters that feel like crawling centipedes form on the creature's skin. When the creature finishes a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Long Rest</span>, it takes `dice:1d4|noform|noparens|avg|text(2)` (`1d4`) Necrotic damage, and its Hit Point maximum is reduced by an amount equal to the damage taken. The creature dies if this effect reduces its Hit Point maximum to 0. The reduction can't be removed until the contagion ends on the creature. A Humanoid that dies from this contagion explodes into a [Skitterswarm](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/fey/skitterswarm-crookedmoon24.md) at the next Midnight Hour, the corpse's flesh and bones turning into a malleable putty-like substance that can be split apart and carried by centipedes back to Skitterdeep Mine.

##### Fighting the Contagion

A Lesser Restoration spell suppresses the effect of the contagion for 24 hours. Nightly at the Twilight Hour, an infected creature makes a DC 15 Constitution saving throw. After the creature succeeds on three of these saves, the contagion ends for it.

#### Trail of Corruption

The characters can investigate the town to find where the centipedes are coming from by succeeding on a group DC 15 Intelligence (Investigation) or Wisdom (Survival) check, discovering a sprawling network of tiny tunnels in the earth that seem to be coming from the northwest of Wickermoor Village.

After deducing the direction from which the centipedes are burrowing into town, the characters are approached by Mrs. Oakum (see chapter Wickermoor Village), who works for Siv Harga at the butcher shop. She tells the characters that the centipede infestation perfectly matched stories that her father used to tell her about the old abandoned mine in the wooded hills northwest of Wickermoor Village. Use the details below to guide the conversation with her:

- Her family used to live in a small community of hill-folk that worked in mining, logging, and trapping.  
- Decades ago, the old Glitterdeep Mine had a terrible cave-in that trapped dozens of miners within.  
- Thousands of huge, monstrous centipedes were seen skittering in the hills and into the mine.  
- A large rescue party was organized, but those who went into the mine never returned.  
- The rumor among the community was that it was a hag that caused the tragedy.  
- Swearing vengeance, a posse of riders was rallied to kill the hag and her minions, but they were never heard from again.  
- Fear of vile magic and the loss of work in the mine caused the community to dwindle.  
- Folks who know the old story have called the abandoned mine Skitterdeep for years.  

Mrs. Oakum offers to guide the characters through the hills but says she won't get too close to the mine, fearing the hag that lurks in the depths. If the characters accept her help, the journey to the mine is the ideal opportunity to have her true identity as a Werewolf be revealed through an inadvertent transformation, whether it be through danger that the characters experience or hunger triggered by a forest creature.

If the characters don't take it upon themselves to investigate the mine, Morgan the apothecary (see chapter Wickermoor Village) requests their assistance. They stress that if nothing is done, the sickness will overwhelm the town, and there will not be enough healers to tend to the ill—made worse by the indifference of the de facto surgeon Randall Graham, who believes the situation will resolve itself without taking drastic action. If necessary, Morgan offers the characters a Potion of Healing (greater) each to find the cause of the sickness and eliminate it.

Characters who monitor the health of afflicted villagers over the course of the night, or Morgan the apothecary if the characters don't think to, realize that the illness is likely fatal over the course of several nights.

### Sisterly "Love"

If the characters are not in Wickermoor Village when you wish to initiate the events of this chapter, a carrier pigeon finds them wherever they are, delivering a letter from postmaster Theodora Mayville. She informs them of the town's infestation and her own suspicion that the centipedes were coming from Skitterdeep Mine.

She claims that she sent out a rider, who normally delivers large parcels, to investigate the mine, but he never returned. Theodora implores the characters to head to the mine themselves and put an end to the infestation, offering 50 GP to each character once the plague is halted.

This is a ruse by [Golub Graygullet](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/npc/golub-graygullet-crookedmoon24.md) (see "The Crooked Moon") to lure the characters into finding and killing her sister Stonoga as a sacrifice in honor of the Crooked Queen. If the characters follow this quest hook, they are confronted by a lone [Fleshdobbin](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/monstrosity/fleshdobbin-crookedmoon24.md) when they are three miles out from the mine—the flesh-crafted parcel rider that Theodora had sent to his doom.

## Skitterdeep Mine

Skitterdeep Mine is about 8 hours of travel away from the village at a Fast pace through the wooded hills northwest of town. It once provided gold, silver, and iron for the people of Wickermoor Village, but has become a place of death and decay. When the characters arrive at the mine, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud] 
> 
> Old, weathered buildings sit beside a large hole dug into the earth, the darkness within an inky black. A wooden beam extends from one of the structures, which holds a heavy chain lowered into the abyss. Not far from the shaft, broken iron minecart rails lead to a lumber-reinforced tunnel entrance built into a wooden ridge. The stench of rotting meat and sweat hangs in the air.

### Skitterdeep Mine Features

Unless otherwise noted, Skitterdeep Mine has the following features:

#### Ceilings

Ceilings are 10 feet high in mine shafts and tunnels, and 15 feet high in rooms and chambers.

#### Lighting

The mine's interior locations are unlit unless otherwise noted.

#### Hag Mark

Stonoga has marked all the denizens of the mine (the mark is a cluster of blisters that resembles a centipede), which makes them immune to the Oppressive Gloom feature as well as the Putrid Miasma in area S6.

#### Oppressive Gloom

Thanks to Stonoga's magical tampering, all light sources have their radius halved. Furthermore, Darkvision's range is reduced to 15 feet. Stonoga and creatures she has marked can see normally.

#### Skitter Plague

When a creature in the mine is hit by an attack from a Giant Centipede or [Skitterswarm](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/fey/skitterswarm-crookedmoon24.md), it must succeed on a DC 12 Constitution saving throw or be afflicted with Skitter Plague (see "Skitter Plague" above).

### Skitterdeep Mine Locations

The following locations are keyed to the Skitterdeep Mine (Side) map through the Deep Drift map.

#### S1: Warehouse

> [!readaloud] 
> 
> A broken sign hangs on the door to the warehouse. The faded letters once read "Glitterdeep Mine," but the first two letters have been painted over by a vandal, so it now reads "Skitterdeep Mine" in weathered writing. The door hangs mostly open in the frame, revealing a long aisle filled with damaged shelves and scattered rubble.
> 
> The room stinks of sweat and sewage, and a thin, greenish haze hangs in the air. You hear the sound of guttural snoring and wet lip smacking and see their source. Four humanoid figures sleep on sheets of rusted metal, horribly mutated and warped with distended bellies, mole-like claws, huge bulging eyes, and stringy wispy hair.
^3ed

Broken shelves line the warehouse interior, and debris clutters nearly every possible space.

##### Debris

The warehouse is <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Difficult Terrain</span> due to the great piles of detritus.

##### Creatures

There are four [Deepdrecks](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/monstrosity/deepdreck-crookedmoon24.md) asleep amidst the broken items here at any given time. These flesh-crafted abominations are what remain of the lost miners. Resting deepdrecks have no interest in fighting and will flee to warn Stonoga of intruders if awoken—though they will defend themselves if cornered.

##### Treasure

A character who spends 10 minutes searching the debris finds two trinkets lost in the rubble, and with a successful DC 14 Wisdom (Perception) check, also finds a metal lockbox. The lock is rusted through and can be opened with light effort. The box contains three citrines (50 GP each) and a Potion of Healing (greater) labeled "Emergency."

#### S2: Machine Shop

The chain runs from the Winch House (area S2c) and into the Main Shaft (area S3).

##### Handprints

If the characters have already seen a [fleshdobbin](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/monstrosity/fleshdobbin-crookedmoon24.md), they can easily deduce that more such creatures lurk in the next room.

The south door is locked (DC 15).

##### Treasure

As a Search action, a character who looks through the debris and succeeds on a DC 20 Wisdom (Perception) check finds a Maul, as well as a Dungeoneer's Pack with a flask of Alchemist's Fire and a flask of Oil strapped to the side. A character who spends 5 minutes thoroughly searching succeeds on the check automatically.

#### S2b: Fleshdobbin Lair

> [!readaloud] 
> 
> This filthy room stinks of mold and stale sweat. Four piles of rubbish are draped in rotten blankets and old cloth. You hear a squelching whinny as two large abominable creatures stir. They have the silhouette of horses but seem to have the flesh and loose anatomy of humans. They hobble about the room on four limbs, drooling large drops of putrid saliva onto the stained floor.
^3fa

The piles are fleshdobbin beds, covered with the horse blankets and rotten wine-red surcoats of the horses and knights these creatures once were.

##### Creatures

Two [Fleshdobbins](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/monstrosity/fleshdobbin-crookedmoon24.md) are presently inside the lair. They are <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Hostile</span> to intruders and attack without hesitation.

##### Doors

Both doors here are locked (DC 15).

#### S2c: Winch House

The door leading into this room is locked (DC 15).

> [!readaloud] 
> 
> A heavy chain winds around a massive winch and runs out through a port in the wall. A control box with a lever stands next to the machinery.
^3fe

A Small creature can wriggle through the chain port with a successful DC 15 Dexterity (Acrobatics) check, and a Tiny creature can squeeze through the port without a check.

##### Winch

The controls for the winch are simple enough to operate, but doing so causes it to emit a loud groan and series of shrieks that alert all other creatures in the mine to the presence of intruders. For the next 2 hours, denizens of the mine are alert and watching. Stonoga, Slippery Oswald, and the [deepdrecks](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/monstrosity/deepdreck-crookedmoon24.md) attempt to hide and set ambushes during that time. [Fleshdobbins](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/monstrosity/fleshdobbin-crookedmoon24.md) can't be bothered to hide, as they delight in brashness.

The winch can be disabled using Thieves' Tools with a successful DC 15 Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) check, and it has AC 17, HP 10, and <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Immunity</span> to Poison and Psychic damage.

If the platform is brought up to the ground level, the creatures on the lift of the Main Shaft (see area S3) ride the platform up and investigate, attacking any intruders on sight.

As a Study action, a character who examines the winch and succeeds on a DC 14 Intelligence (Investigation) check realizes the danger of operating it, and that a flask of Oil poured on the mechanism will prevent it from making noise.

#### S3: Main Shaft

> [!readaloud] 
> 
> A rusted chain extends from a ramshackle building to a scaffold over a square mineshaft, then descends into the darkness. The hole in the earth belches a foul-smelling gas of a greenish hue.
^401

The chain runs from the winch mechanism (area S2c) and suspends a lift platform. The shaft is a 15-foot square tunnel cut straight down into the rock. It drops 100 feet from the surface to the rough bottom just below area S6.

##### Chain

A creature can climb the chain with relative ease (no ability check required).

##### Creatures

One [Fleshdobbin](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/monstrosity/fleshdobbin-crookedmoon24.md), two [Deepdrecks](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/monstrosity/deepdreck-crookedmoon24.md), and ten Giant Centipedes lurk on or around the lift.

##### Lift

The lift is 30 feet below the surface, at area S5. It's a platform composed of a series of wooden planks bolted to the top of three iron beams, and it is controlled from the winch house (area S2c).

If more than 2,500 pounds of weight is placed on the lift and chain, the winch breaks and spins free, sending the lift, any creatures on it, and the chain hurtling to the bottom of the shaft. If the centipedes are on the lift, they scuttle off onto the walls of the shaft. If the lift falls, it is smashed into splinters and alerts the denizens of the mine as described in area S2c. The winch is destroyed and useless.

Any creatures on the lift when it falls also fall and take damage from the fall if they hit the bottom of the shaft.

##### Rotting Stench

Characters smell a noxious odor from below, radiating from area S6, where a Putrid Miasma has entirely consumed the tunnels.

##### Treasure

The fleshdobbin wears an iron key to the doors in the Machine Shop (area S2) on a leather cord around its neck.

#### S4: Old Drift

If the characters enter the Old Drift from the surface, read the following:

> [!readaloud] 
> 
> Overturned minecarts litter the entrance of this tunnel bored into the ridge. The timber scaffolding built around the opening is discolored, rotten, and termite-eaten. Vegetation has grown overmuch of the start of the drift that has been unused for some time. An especially foul stink wafts up from the tunnel.
^408

This was once the main working drift of the mine long ago, before the terrible cave-in that doomed so many miners. An exit from the drift was cut as a secondary air source and access for minecarts. Stonoga and her minions haven't bothered concealing the old entrance further.

##### Rotting Stench

An odor of decay lingers faintly throughout and wafts from the far entrance, radiating up from area S6 where Stonoga's miasma has consumed the tunnels.

##### Creatures

Five [Deepdrecks](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/monstrosity/deepdreck-crookedmoon24.md) wander these tunnels, grinding through the stone and dirt, searching for precious metal nuggets to curry favor with Stonoga. Unless they were alerted by the lift moving in area S3, they are utterly immersed in their work and have <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Disadvantage</span> on Wisdom (Perception) checks made to notice intruders.

##### Patrolling Spy

Every `dice:1d6|noform|noparens|avg|text(3)` (`1d6`) minutes, a Giant Centipede—squeezed into a Ring of Evasion it wears around its middle—skitters through the tunnel. If the centipede spots intruders, it scurries off to report their presence to Stonoga in her laboratory (area S7).

##### Treasure

Removing the centipede's Ring of Evasion requires killing it. Scattered along the drift are enough serviceable mining implements to assemble a set of Mason's Tools.

#### S5: Crosscut

> [!readaloud] 
> 
> Boxes of large gems and sacks full of glittering nuggets of precious metals line the available space—a massive treasure hoard. Despite the shiny trove of precious stones and metals, this chamber stinks with the smell of rotten meat.
^40e

Stonoga stores some of her valuables that have no immediate practical use to her in this tunnel, along with a different type of treasure: rotten flesh and bones she may be able to use in her fleshcraft experiments. To safeguard these "riches," she set her familiar and a magical trap to guard them.

##### Illusion

The gems and precious metals here are false, save for one box (see "Treasure" below), and are protected by an intangible illusion that makes the eyeballs, tongues, knucklebones, and various organs appear as gemstones, fine silks, coins, gilded chalices, and delicate necklaces. As a Study action, a creature that examines the false treasure and succeeds on a DC 15 Intelligence (Investigation) check can see through the uncanny illusion for what it really is, leaving it as a ghostly apparition to them.

##### Creature

When he's not at Stonoga's side or tormenting [deepdrecks](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/monstrosity/deepdreck-crookedmoon24.md), Slippery Oswald ([Vermin Familiar (Centipede)](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/fey/vermin-familiar-centipede-crookedmoon24.md)) lurks here, burrowed in a lumpy pile of intestines. He knows that he can hide deep within the illusory treasures and remain undisturbed by the deepdrecks and [fleshdobbins](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/monstrosity/fleshdobbin-crookedmoon24.md), who have been trained to avoid this space. While within the hoard, he has the Invisible condition against a creature that has not seen through the illusion. He ambushes the characters if they disturb him and fights without fear, knowing that if he dies, Stonoga can summon him again.

##### Magical Trap

The entrance to the Crosscut has a single eyeball hanging by its optic nerve above it, noticeable by creatures that take the Search action and succeed on a DC 15 Wisdom (Perception) check. If anyone other than Stonoga or Slippery Oswald takes treasure from the Crosscut past the eyeball, the Main Shaft (area S3) fills with a Hypnotic Pattern spell (save DC 15) for 10 minutes, and a mental Alarm spell alerts Stonoga, Slippery Oswald, and any remaining fleshdobbins to the presence of intruders.

Stonoga can end or reset the trap's effects at any time (no action required) from anywhere in the mine. The eyeball trap can be disabled with the Utilize action and a successful DC15 Intelligence (Arcana) check or a successful Dispel Magic spell (DC 15), or it can be broken (AC 13, HP 5, <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Immunity</span> to Poison and Psychic damage).

##### Treasure

Amidst the boxes and sacks full of parts is a single real box of gold and silver nuggets worth 450 GP.

#### S6: Deep Drift

> [!readaloud] 
> 
> The shaft ends at a rough floor a few feet below a long tunnel filled with a chartreuse haze. A heavy bronze door nearby is cast in dim, pulsing greenish-purple light that illuminates the mist. The sound of trickling water echoes from farther down the dark tunnel. The stench here is oppressive, stinking like a combination of rotting flesh, sweat, and sewage gas. The walls themselves are slick and seemingly soft to the touch, squirming before your eyes.
^414

The deepest level of the mine, 100 feet below the surface, is the hag Stonoga Blackstinger's lair and laboratory.

##### Putrid Miasma

When a creature first enters the area, and again every 10 minutes thereafter until it leaves the miasma, it must succeed on a DC 16 Constitution saving throw or have the Poisoned condition. While Poisoned in this way, the creature repeats the saving throw every hour. On a failure, it gains 1 Exhaustion level. On a success, the Poisoned condition ends. Denizens of the mine are immune to the miasma (see "Hag Mark" above).

##### Bronze Door

The unlocked bronze door is 3 inches thick and leads to Stonoga's Laboratory (area S7). It's remarkably difficult to hear sounds through the door, so only impacts on the door itself alert Stonoga to a commotion outside.

##### Creatures

A [Fleshdobbin](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/monstrosity/fleshdobbin-crookedmoon24.md) and a [Skitterswarm](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/fey/skitterswarm-crookedmoon24.md) keep guard outside the bronze door, and two [Deepdrecks](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/monstrosity/deepdreck-crookedmoon24.md) dwell within the walls near the glowing sigils (see below). If combat breaks out, the deepdrecks roll <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Initiative</span> and join the fight on the second round. If the fleshdobbin dies, any remaining deepdrecks try to pound on the bronze door to warn Stonoga.

##### Gruesome Sigils

The tunnel leads to a chamber where connective tissues and entrails have been formed into a rough knotted pattern on the ground. These sigils writhe as if alive, releasing clouds of foul gas that condense like dew on the walls and seeps through the soil. Stonoga crafted the sigils, which are the source of the Putrid Miasma choking the mine and fueling the magical contagion of Skitter Plague.

If the sigils are destroyed (AC 17; HP 30; <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Immunity</span> to Poison and Psychic damage; <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Resistance</span> to all other damage except Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Force), the toxic fume dissipates. They can also be destroyed with a successful Dispel Magic spell (DC 15) or disabled as a Utilize action with a successful DC 20 Intelligence (Arcana) check. If she's alive, Stonoga knows when the sigils are destroyed or deactivated.

The lab is lit with <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Dim Light</span>.

##### Putrid Miasma

The miasma filling the Deep Drift (area S6) persists here.

##### Creatures

If she hasn't been alerted to the presence of the characters, the [Centipede Hag](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/fey/centipede-hag-crookedmoon24.md) Stonoga Blackstinger is deep in ritual research for her horrid experiments. If she is alerted, or when battle breaks out, three [Skitterswarms](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/fey/skitterswarm-crookedmoon24.md) writhe from the walls and floor to join their mistress. If [Slippery Oswald](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/npc/slippery-oswald-crookedmoon24.md) ([Vermin Familiar (Centipede)](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/fey/vermin-familiar-centipede-crookedmoon24.md)) is still alive, Stonoga calls to him telepathically, and he joins the battle as well.

She's a putrid creature, delighting in the corruption of living flesh, blighting it with disease and poison, and twisting it into freakish new forms through her foul magic. Despite her and morbid appetites, she is unnervingly jovial, savoring lively conversation before choosing to massacre the intruders so that their flesh may be crafted into horrid new designs.

When Stonoga is defeated, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud] 
> 
> With a sickening pop, Stonoga Blackstinger's pitch black eyes pop out of her head, and her grotesquely long neck snaps and curls up like a dead centipede as she collapses to the floor. Her corpse immediately begins losing form as her own flesh turns to a malleable sludge that oozes across the ground.
^41e

##### Treasure

Stonoga wears a Necklace of Adaptation in the form of a gold centipede on a cord around her neck. Scattered on her workbench are four Spell Scrolls: Alarm, Inflict Wounds, Invisibility, and Sleep. Her scrawled notes contain useful information summarized in "Conclusion: Exterminated" below.

The apparatus scattered across the shelves is a set of Alchemist's Supplies and a large glass jar contains a [fleshdobbin](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/monstrosity/fleshdobbin-crookedmoon24.md) head preserved in a translucent, cloudy gel. The gel is 4 doses of Restorative Ointment.

##### Fated Tarot Reading

If The Star was pulled during the "Fated Tarot Reading" in Running the Adventure, the character this card was pulled for gains a feat of their choice for which they qualify after defeating Stonoga Blackstinger.

## Conclusion: Exterminated

Once Stonoga Blackstinger is slain, Slippery Oswald dies alongside her if he was not killed previously. The poisonous mist (see "Putrid Miasma" in area S6) lingers if they haven't already stopped it, though Stonoga's frantic notes and the noxious miasma rising from the symbols reveal that the sigils in the Deep Drift (area S6) are the source of the magical contagion.

Once the sigils are destroyed or deactivated, the Putrid Miasma dissipates, and the spreading Skitter Plague fades from Wickermoor Village. With the death of Stonoga, the thousands of centipedes skitter off into the wilds, ending the infestation. Those afflicted with Skitter Plague begin to recover over the course of the next hour.

Among Stonoga's notes, the characters discover that she gleefully awaits the death of the younger Vessla Browntooth and delights in the conspiracy she has with the older Golub Graygullet, who she claims hides in disguise among the humans of Wickermoor Village. This leads the characters to their next clue on the final member of The Vermintoll Coven.

### Back in Wickermoor Village

The townsfolk that had fallen ill recover miraculously and are grateful to the characters for saving their lives. Morgan the apothecary is relieved the townsfolk didn't perish and offers the characters potions from their shop that are appropriate for the needs and requests of the characters (see "Wickermoor Village").

Phillip Druskenvald, meanwhile, rolls up his sleeves, determined to better help Wickermoor Village now that the hag and her minions have been scoured from the mine. Alongside Mayor Somerton, he eagerly lays plans to re-open Glitterdeep Mine, envisioning a rebirth of prosperity for the town. He sends out word, seeking laborers with iron resolve, skilled artisans, and guards unafraid of the shadowed depths. His promise of generous wages from his own coffers stirs the townsfolk, and soon, hammers and saws ring out in preparation.
