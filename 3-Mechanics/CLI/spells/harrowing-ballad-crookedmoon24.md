# Harrowing Ballad
*2nd-level, Enchantment*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Bonus Action
- **Range:** 120 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

You inflict a mind-eroding song on a creature that you can see within range. If the creature has an Intelligence of 4 or higher, it must succeed on an Intelligence saving throw or have the Charmed condition for the duration.

While Charmed in this way, the target is struck with a rapidly repeating ballad that only it can hear; it takes `dice:1d6|noform|noparens|avg` (`1d6`) Psychic damage at the start of each of its turns, and it has <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Disadvantage</span> on Wisdom (Perception) checks and on Constitution saving throws made to maintain its Concentration. The target repeats the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the spell on a success.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** The damage increases by `dice:1d6|noform|noparens|avg` (`1d6`) for each spell slot level above 2.
