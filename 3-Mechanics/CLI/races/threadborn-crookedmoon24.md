# Threadborn
*Source: The Crooked Moon (2024) p. 65*  
![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheGiddyLimit/homebrew-img/refs/heads/main/img/CrookedMoon24/Races/Threadborn.webp#right)

- **Ability Scores**: None
- **Type**: construct
- **Size**: Small or Medium
- **Speed**: 30 ft.
- **Spellcasting**: Choose one of Intelligence, Wisdom, Charisma

## Traits

### Ball-Jointed

You can move through a space as narrow as 1 foot wide as if it were <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Difficult Terrain</span>.

### Innocent Mind

You have <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Resistance</span> to Psychic damage.

### Sewn Nature

You don't require air, food, or drink.

### Soothing Heart

You know the Guidance cantrip. Starting at character level 3, you always have the Sanctuary spell prepared, and starting at character level 5, you always have the Calm Emotions spell prepared. You can cast each spell once without a spell slot. When you cast Calm Emotions in this way, it can target creatures of any type. You regain the ability to cast them using this trait when you finish a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Long Rest</span>. You can also cast either spell using any spell slots you have of the appropriate level. Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma is your spellcasting ability for the spells you cast with this trait (choose the ability when you select this species).

### You've Got a Friend

When a creature you can see within 30 feet of yourself fails an ability check using a skill, you can take a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Reaction</span> to cast Guidance on the creature, ignoring the spell's normal range. The creature can add the spell's `dice:1d4|noform|noparens|avg` (`1d4`) to its ability check, potentially causing it to succeed, then the spell ends. You can use this trait a number of times equal to your <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Proficiency Bonus</span>, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Long Rest</span>.

## Description

Threadborn are a doll-like species native to "Picco", most often built with love by Blinski II, the master toymaker and lord of the province. In a land that is mostly fabricated and constructed to maximize the fun of all who live there, the essence of joy, thrill, and friendship are woven into a threadborn's very nature. They cherish the bonds made between loved ones and always maintain a childlike wonder and whimsy, no matter one's age.

As living dolls, these folk are constructed with a variety of materials, including cloth, wood, metal, porcelain, stuffing, yarn, and anything else a toy might be made of. With the most varied body shapes of all Druskenvald's species, each threadborn represents a unique representation of fun. They usually live for 100 years before falling apart and needing to be rebuilt.
