# Draught of the Red Hunger
*Potion, legendary*  
![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheGiddyLimit/homebrew-img/refs/heads/main/img/CrookedMoon24/Items/Draught%20of%20the%20Red%20Hunger.webp#right)


When you drink this potion, you gain a ravenous thirst for blood and gain the effect of the Vampiric Touch spell (level 6 version, no Concentration required) for 1 minute. You can repeat the attack on subsequent turns as a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Bonus Action</span> instead of a Magic action.

This liquid is a shimmering red deeper and more intense than natural blood.

## Heightened Potency

For the duration, as a Magic action, you can gain the effects of the Gaseous Form spell (no Concentration required) while you aren't in sunlight. You can also revert to your normal form as a Magic action.

*Source: The Crooked Moon (2024) p. 614*
