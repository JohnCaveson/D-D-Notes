# Lucky Charm
*5th-level, Transmutation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S, M (a four-leaf clover and a shard of broken mirror)
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 hour

You touch a Tiny, nonmagical object, infusing it with a portion of your own luck for the duration. While another creature carries the object, that creature has <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Advantage</span> on <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">D20 Tests</span>, and you have <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Disadvantage</span> on <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">D20 Tests</span>.
