# Ashborn
*Source: The Crooked Moon (2024) p. 53*  
![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheGiddyLimit/homebrew-img/refs/heads/main/img/CrookedMoon24/Races/Ashborn.webp#right)

- **Ability Scores**: None
- **Type**: fiend
- **Size**: Small
- **Speed**: 30 ft.
- **Spellcasting**: Choose one of Intelligence, Wisdom, Charisma

## Traits

### Ashen Legacy

You know the Minor Illusion cantrip. Additionally, starting at character level 3 you always have the Charm Person spell prepared, and starting at character level 5 you always have the Invisibility spell prepared. You can cast them each once without a spell slot, and you regain the ability to cast them in that way when you finish a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Long Rest</span>. You can also cast them using any spell slots you have of the appropriate level.

Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma is your spellcasting ability for the spells you cast with this trait (choose the ability when you select this species).

### Darkvision

You have Darkvision with a range of 60 feet.

### Fiendish Fortune

When you're hit by an attack roll that isn't a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Critical Hit</span>, you can take a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Reaction</span> to magically cause the attack to miss you, and a creature of your choice within 5 feet of you takes Force damage equal to your <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Proficiency Bonus</span>. Once you use this trait, you can't do so again until you finish a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Short</span> or <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Long Rest</span>.

### Scorpion Sting

You have a tail stinger you can use to make <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Unarmed Strikes</span> using your Strength or Dexterity (your choice) for attack and damage rolls. On a hit, you deal `dice:1d4|noform|noparens|avg` (`1d4`) Piercing damage, and you can deal an extra `dice:1d6|noform|noparens|avg` (`1d6`) Poison damage. You can deal this extra damage a number of times equal to your <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Proficiency Bonus</span>. You regain one expended use when you finish a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Short Rest</span>, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Long Rest</span>.

When you reach character level 10, this extra damage increases to `dice:2d6|noform|noparens|avg` (`2d6`).

## Description

Ashborn are an imp-like species native to "Chernabos", typically coalescing from the sulfuric ash of the province's hellfire and brimstone volcanoes. Infused with the fiendish power of the Hells, this folk has managed to survive and even prosper in the barrens and badlands of the province through guile, boldness, and industriousness. Said to be favored by fortune itself, ashborn count on luck to twist the balance in the most dire of situations.

Their short stature ranges from slight to stout, but this makes them no less dangerous, for each ashborn has a venomous tail resembling a scorpion's, always ready to strike. They sport horns and skin tones in varying shades of red and gray that reflect their devilish origin, and they typically live 100 years.
