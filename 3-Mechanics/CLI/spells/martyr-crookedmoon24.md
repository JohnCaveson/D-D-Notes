# Martyr
*3rd-level, Necromancy*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S, M (a Dagger worth at least 1 CP)
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You touch a dead creature that died within the last minute and transfer your life essence into it. The target returns to life with <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Hit Points</span> equal to your current <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Hit Point</span> total. You immediately drop to 0 <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Hit Points</span> (which nothing can prevent) with two failed <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Death Saving Throws</span>, and you must immediately make a Death <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Save</span>. You can't regain <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Hit Points</span> or become <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Stable</span> until after you make this save.
