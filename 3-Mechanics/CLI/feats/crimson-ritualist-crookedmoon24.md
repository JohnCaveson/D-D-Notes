# Crimson Ritualist
*Source: The Crooked Moon (2024) p. 113*  

Your study of blood magic and life force has borne fruit, granting you the following benefits.

**Blood Lash.** You learn the [Blood Bolt](/3-Mechanics/CLI/spells/blood-bolt-crookedmoon24.md) cantrip. Your spellcasting ability for it is Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma (choose the ability when you select this feat).

**Bottled Life.** When you finish a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Long Rest</span>, you can perform a 1-minute ritual to transfer some of your life essence into a vessel for later use. Expend a number of <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Hit Point Dice</span> up to your <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Proficiency Bonus</span>, and bleed into a vial of water or other liquid. As a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Bonus Action</span>, a creature can drink the vial to roll the <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Hit Dice</span> you expended. The creature regains a number of <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Hit Points</span> equal to the total rolled plus its Constitution modifier. The vial remains potent until you finish a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Long Rest</span>.
