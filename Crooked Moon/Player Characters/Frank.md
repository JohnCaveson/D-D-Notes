```ad-note
title: Lore
collapse: open

Oh, Emb. The tallest in his class, certainly, but height was arguably the best thing he had going for him. Ember Thatch III(Emb) was the classic loser-boy—a slacker who would often skip his father’s dark sermons to sneak out and smoke "spices" near the family castle.

  
His father, Ember Thatch II, was a highly ranked member of their cult, having done much to spread their dark, pestilent haze. One would assume this lineage meant Emb was set for life, but the spice had taken hold. In minor amounts, it merely chilled him out. However, after a particularly fat, rolled blunt, something magical would occur (a side effect tied to a smoking roll table). Emb spent most of his days in the basement, either alone or with friends, playing darts and smoking.

  
After months of this frustrating behavior, his father finally issued an ultimatum: Emb must find his purpose in the cult and contribute to spreading the pestilent haze. Specifically, he must return with at least five new members who would join the flock. Fail this, and he would be immediately evicted and cut off.

  
Reluctantly, Emb gathered the few items he thought would help him spread the word of Methuselah’s pestilent haze and boarded the train to begin his search.
