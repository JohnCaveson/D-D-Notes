# [Walter Jenkin](3-Mechanics\CLI\bestiary\npc/walter-jenkin-crookedmoon24.md)
*Source: The Crooked Moon (2024) p. 219*  

A handsome and affable sort, Walter Jenkin, lost his voice within weeks of arriving. No healer could explain the sudden silence. Now, he communicates through writing and sign language, which his family also knows and can translate. Entering the manor makes him nervous, sensing its malice, though he never speaks of his fears.

Known for his loyalty and dutifulness, Walter has an almost reverent respect for [Mayor Wendel Somerton](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/npc/wendel-somerton-crookedmoon24.md) and his other former employers. And in this new position, he has already become devoted to the Druskenvalds, despite having only just met them.
