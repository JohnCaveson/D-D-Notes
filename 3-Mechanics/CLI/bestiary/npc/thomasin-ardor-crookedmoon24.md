# [Thomasin Ardor](3-Mechanics\CLI\bestiary\npc/thomasin-ardor-crookedmoon24.md)
*Source: The Crooked Moon (2024) p. 205*  

Thomasin Ardor is the village's most proficient tailor. As a child, she fell through a large pane of glass, but her life was saved with a critical medical procedure. This trauma, in conjunction with her quiet and cool disposition, leaves her the brunt of unfounded rumors and gossip.

She often keeps folks at a distance, and the only person in town she is close with is [Randall Graham](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/npc/randall-graham-crookedmoon24.md) (see area V7), who stitched her up following her childhood accident.

While Thomasin is a benevolent member of the Followers of the Old Ways and reveres the Green Queen, [Randall Graham](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/npc/randall-graham-crookedmoon24.md) is attempting to leverage their relationship to subtly guide her into joining the cult of the Crooked Queen. Whether she falls to wickedness or discovers Randall's plot and rebuffs him is up to you.
