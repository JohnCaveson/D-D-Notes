# Dread Speech
*Source: The Crooked Moon (2024) p. 114*  

You have articulated a few words of forbidden truth that resonate with—and fray—the fabric of creation, granting you the following benefits.

**Eldritch Word.** As a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Bonus Action</span>, you can speak a magical word aloud to force a creature within 60 feet of yourself to make a Wisdom saving throw (DC = 8 plus your <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Proficiency Bonus</span> and Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma modifier; choose the ability when you select this feat). On a failed save, the target has the Frightened condition until the start of your next turn. You can use this benefit safely once, and you regain the ability to do so when you finish a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Short</span> or <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Long Rest</span>. You can use it again before resting, but you take `dice:1d6|noform|noparens|avg` (`1d6`) Necrotic damage, which can't be reduced or prevented in any way.

**Shred Fate.** Your dread secrets can unravel the destiny of your foes. You always have the Bane spell prepared, and your spellcasting ability for it is the ability you chose for Eldritch Word. You can cast it once without Material components or a spell slot, and you regain the ability to cast it in that way when you finish a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Long Rest</span>. You can also cast the spell using any spell slots you have.
