# Shield of Marius Renathyr (Very Rare)
*Armor ([shield](/3-Mechanics/CLI/items/shield.md)), very rare (requires attunement)*  
![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheGiddyLimit/homebrew-img/refs/heads/main/img/CrookedMoon24/Items/Shield%20of%20Marius%20Renathyr.webp#right)

- **Armor Class**: 2
- **Properties**: [Fabled Heirloom](/3-Mechanics/CLI/item-properties.md#Fabled%20Heirloom)
- **Weight**: 6.0 lbs.

Crafted from polished gold and white enamel and adorned with an intricate design of a rose encircled by thorny vines, this Shield was once owned by Marius Renathyr, a knight-errant destined for a terrible fate.

While holding this Shield, you gain a +2 bonus to AC determined by the Shield's rarity, in addition to the Shield's normal bonus to AC, and you can use the following properties.

## Valorous Guard (Rare+)

When a creature within 5 feet of you is hit by an attack roll, you can take a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Reaction</span> to add 2 + the Shield's bonus to the creature's AC, potentially causing the attack to miss. When a creature within 5 feet of you fails a Dexterity saving throw, you can take a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Reaction</span> to add 2 + the Shield's bonus to the creature's total, potentially causing it to succeed.

## Honor of the Vanguard (Very Rare+)

When you roll <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Initiative</span> and an ally you can see is within 30 feet of an enemy, you can teleport to an unoccupied space within 5 feet of that ally and gain <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Temporary Hit Points</span> equal to 5 times the Shield's bonus.

## Righteous Wrath (Very Rare+)

When you use the Valorous Guard property to cause an attack to miss, the next time you hit that attacker with a weapon or <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Unarmed Strike</span> before the end of your next turn, it takes an extra `dice:1d8|noform|noparens|avg` (`1d8`) (Legendary: `dice:3d8|noform|noparens|avg` (`3d8`)) Radiant damage.

*Source: The Crooked Moon (2024) p. 604*
