# Cane of Phillip Druskenvald (Very Rare)
*Rod, very rare (requires attunement)*  
![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheGiddyLimit/homebrew-img/refs/heads/main/img/CrookedMoon24/Items/Cane%20of%20Phillip%20Druskenvald.webp#right)

- **Properties**: [Fabled Heirloom](/3-Mechanics/CLI/item-properties.md#Fabled%20Heirloom)

This ornate cane is made from polished black wood and is topped with a silver goat-skull ornament. Even a glance at it sparks greed and envy in all but the truly humble.

While holding the cane, you can use it as a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Spellcasting Focus</span> for your spells, you gain a +2 bonus to spell attack rolls and your spell save DC determined by the cane's rarity, and you can use the following properties.

## Eldritch Glutton (Rare+)

When you succeed on a saving throw against a spell that targets only you, you can take a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Reaction</span> to consume the spell and store its energy in the cane. The spell has no effect on you. You can take a Magic action to absorb the stored energy to either regain an expended spell slot that is at least one level lower than the triggering spell or gain <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Temporary Hit Points</span> equal to three times the spell's level. If you absorb a new spell while the cane holds spell energy, the previous energy is lost. Once the cane absorbs a spell, this property can't be used again until the next dusk.

## Allure of Sin (Very Rare+)

You can cast Dominate Person (save DC = 16 + the cane's bonus) from the cane. Once a creature is Charmed by the spell, this property can't be used again until the next dusk.

*Source: The Crooked Moon (2024) p. 597*
