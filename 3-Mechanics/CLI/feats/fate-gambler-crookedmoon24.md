# Fate Gambler
*Source: The Crooked Moon (2024) p. 114*  

Coming out ahead, whether in games of chance or in life-or-death contention, fills you with resolve and vigor. You gain the following benefits.

**Bet Big.** If you don't already have it, you gain <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Heroic Inspiration</span> whenever you succeed on an ability check made against another creature, when you reduce a creature to 0 <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Hit Points</span>, or when a creature fails a saving throw you forced it to make. If, in any of these cases, the creature has a CR of 0 or lacks a CR (such as another player character), you don't gain <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Heroic Inspiration</span>.

**Win Big.** As a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Bonus Action</span>, you can expend your <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Heroic Inspiration</span> to gain a number of <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Temporary Hit Points</span> equal to your level.
