# Banjo of Ol' Jericho Sticks (Rare)
*Wondrous item, rare (requires attunement)*  
![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheGiddyLimit/homebrew-img/refs/heads/main/img/CrookedMoon24/Items/Banjo%20of%20Ol%20Jericho%20Sticks.webp#right)

- **Properties**: [Fabled Heirloom](/3-Mechanics/CLI/item-properties.md#Fabled%20Heirloom)

An old, weathered banjo inscribed with runes, this instrument once belonged to Ol' Jericho Sticks, a scarecrow bard created by a hag coven.

While holding the banjo, you can use it as a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Spellcasting Focus</span> for your spells; you gain a +1 bonus determined by its rarity to spell attack rolls, your spell save DC, and Charisma (Performance) checks; and you can use the following properties.

## Fiendish Lure (Rare+)

As a Magic action, choose one Fiend or Humanoid that you can see within 60 feet. The target must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw (DC = 16 + the banjo's bonus) or have the Charmed condition (ignoring a demon's <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Immunity</span>) for 1 minute. The Charmed condition ends if you or your allies deal damage to the target or force it to make a saving throw. Once a creature is Charmed in this way, this property can't be used again until the next dusk.

## Scarecrow's Dance (Rare+)

You can take a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Bonus Action</span> to strum the banjo and dance wildly to take the Disengage action. The music is audible within 120 feet.

*Source: The Crooked Moon (2024) p. 595*
