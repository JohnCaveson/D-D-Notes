# Crimson Harvest
*3rd-level, Necromancy*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Self (15-foot Emanation)
- **Components:** V, S, M (a vial of blood)
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

A wave of blood sweeps out from you in a 15-foot <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Emanation</span>. Each creature of your choice in the wave must make a Dexterity saving throw, taking `dice:3d10|noform|noparens|avg` (`3d10`) Necrotic damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.

If you damage at least one Small or larger creature, one creature of your choice that you can see within the <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Emanation</span> regains a number of <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Hit Points</span> equal to half the damage roll.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** The damage increases by `dice:1d10|noform|noparens|avg` (`1d10`) for each spell slot level above 3.
