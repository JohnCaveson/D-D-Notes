# Tooth of the Jinxed Leviathan
*Weapon (dagger) (requires attunement)*  
![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheGiddyLimit/homebrew-img/refs/heads/main/img/CrookedMoon24/Items/Tooth%20of%20the%20Jinxed%20Leviathan.webp#right)

- **Properties**: [Fabled Heirloom](/3-Mechanics/CLI/item-properties.md#Fabled%20Heirloom)
- **Weight**: 1.0 lbs.

This tooth is razor sharp and can be wielded as a Dagger. Its "hilt" is wrapped in alligator hide, and the flat of its blade flickers with magenta hex magic in carved glyphs.

You gain a bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon determined by its rarity. While holding the tooth, you can use it as a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Spellcasting Focus</span> for your spells.
