# Hook of the Tall Man (Legendary)
*Weapon (war pick), legendary (requires attunement)*  
![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheGiddyLimit/homebrew-img/refs/heads/main/img/CrookedMoon24/Items/Hook%20of%20the%20Tall%20Man.webp#right)

- **Damage**:
  - One-handed: 1d8 piercing
  - Two-handed: 1d10 piercing
- **Properties**: [Fabled Heirloom](/3-Mechanics/CLI/item-properties.md#Fabled%20Heirloom), [Versatile](/3-Mechanics/CLI/item-properties.md#Versatile)
- **Weight**: 2.0 lbs.

This hook is made from a single huge claw attached to a large bleached fingerbone mounted on a haft of ash. The tip of the claw gives off an eerie glow. The finger and claw were cut from the hand of the Tall Man.

You gain a +3 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon determined by the hook's rarity. While holding the hook, you can use the following properties.

## Snare (Rare+)

When you hit a creature with the hook, you can activate the Topple mastery property in addition to a different mastery property you're using with it.

## Fey Haunt's Stride (Rare+)

You can cast Misty Step from the hook, and when you do so, you have <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Advantage</span> on your next Dexterity (Stealth) check made before the end of your next turn. This property can be used twice, regaining all expended uses when you roll <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Initiative</span>.

## Strike from Shadows (Very Rare+)

When you start your turn with the Invisible condition, you have <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Advantage</span> on the first attack you make on your turn, even if the condition ends before you make the attack. If that attack hits, it deals an extra `dice:2d6|noform|noparens|avg` (`2d6`) Piercing damage.

## Hunter's Hook (Legendary)

When you hit with an Opportunity Attack made with the hook, the target takes an extra `dice:2d6|noform|noparens|avg` (`2d6`) Psychic damage, and its <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Speed</span> is 0 until the end of the turn.

## Terrifying Stride (Legendary)

Immediately after you use the Fey Haunt's Stride property, you can choose a creature within 20 feet of you. It must succeed on a DC 19 Wisdom saving throw or have the Frightened condition until the end of your next turn. While Frightened in this way, the creature can't take the Disengage action.

*Source: The Crooked Moon (2024) p. 601*
