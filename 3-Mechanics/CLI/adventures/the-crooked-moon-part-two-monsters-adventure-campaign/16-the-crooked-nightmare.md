# The Crooked Nightmare
*Source: The Crooked Moon Part Two: Monsters & Adventure Campaign, p. 417* 

![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheGiddyLimit/homebrew-img/refs/heads/main/img/CrookedMoon24/Adventure/295-15-001.the-crooked-nightmare-opener.webp#center)

> [!quote] A quote from Adela Druskenvald  
> 
> Oh Phillip, I had such a terrible dream! I saw this shining, beautiful world we've built turned to ash and smoke. I've had so many nightmares lately. It's this awful house. I just know it!

Nightmares stir anew in the Crooked House. Though slain one by one, the Vermintoll Coven has returned, reborn with the malignant power of Kehlenn, the Crooked Queen. Despite their hatred for one another, the three vile hags have been fused together as one horrific abomination. They now twist the potent dreams of the clairvoyant Adela Druskenvald to fulfill her foretold fate. The house cannot be cleansed and must be destroyed along with the coven to bring the terrors to an end.

## Chapter Background

With the destruction of the Fallen and the sacrifice of the Lord of Fools, the next step of the prophesized ritual to turn The Horned King to flesh and free the Crooked Queen from her prison is the sacrifice of Adela Druskenvald. However, her inevitable burning in the Wicker Man atop Rowan's Rise can only occur once her body, mind, and soul have been prepared.

These preparations were the wicked purpose of the Vermintoll Coven before they were slaughtered. However, their malefic spirits lingered, and Kehlenn returned them to abominable life to utilize their twisted magic one last time. Now, they have pulled Adela Druskenvald's prophetic dreams into the Crooked House, weaving them with Kehlenn's primeval power deep in the earth below to turn the entire home into a waking nightmare that has shattered reality itself.

After watching the characters throughout their trials and tribulations in Wickermoor Hollow, the Crooked Man waits for them inside his old home in hopes of adding more sacrifices to the pyre. He knows he will be given a new body just like his Horned King. The night has come for him to incarnate as the Wicker Man, and he only need delay the would-be heroes a little longer.

## Chapter Summary

The Crooked House has reawakened with the rebirth of the Vermintoll Coven in their new amalgamative form. The [Crooked Man](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/undead/crooked-man-crookedmoon24.md) has trapped the Druskenvalds—once again—and the Jenkins within the house. Characters who enter the nightmare have these goals:

- Approach Druskenvald Manor and ensure the safety of Dani Jenkin.  
- Enter the Crooked House, confront the Crooked Man, and witness it shatter into the Crooked Nightmare.  
- Venture through the horrific dreamscapes, rescuing Walter Jenkin, Gilly Jenkin, and Phillip Druskenvald along the way.  
- Confront the hags of the Vermintoll Coven in the attic of the Crooked House and destroy the Coven Abomination.  

Before running this chapter, refresh yourself on the major NPCs, all of which the characters have previously encountered: the Druskenvalds, the Jenkin family, the Vermintoll Coven, and the Crooked Man. Read through the locations and dreamscapes of the Crooked Nightmare to better improvise each encounter.

This chapter is designed for four level 11 characters.

## Quest Hooks

After the characters deal with the chaos of the Festival of Fools, they may choose to check in on Phillip and Adela at Druskenvald Manor. Otherwise, follow the Quest Hook below.

The characters are approached by Old Rusty while they're in Wickermoor Village, hoping to catch them on the edge of town or on the Founder's Round. Read or paraphrase the following:

^6c4

Old Rusty is not fully lucid, but it is clear he is telling the truth. If the characters have not figured out that he is Francis, the son of Joseph and Gail Patrini, a successful DC 15 Intelligence (History or Investigation) check allows them to connect the dots.

The characters then feel a warmth within and hear a distant harpsichord as they fulfill the final request of Joseph Patrini, gaining the effects of the Bless spell for `dice:1d4|noform|noparens|avg` (`1d4`) hours (see "H3: Parlor").

## Into the Nightmare

The characters leave Dani and Shuck behind and enter the horrifying Crooked House to put an end to its evil once and for all.

### Crooked Nightmare Features

Unless otherwise stated, the areas of the Crooked Nightmare have the following general features:

**Infinite Dreams**. The dreamscapes within the Crooked Nightmare defy reality, regardless of the location they seem to take place in. If a creature attempts to leave a location, it finds itself moving back into the location, no matter how far or in what direction it travels.

- **Crooked Doors.** Crooked doors appear throughout the nightmare generally in impossible places. These doors invitingly open to a swirling reddish glow, which a character can step into and disappear, waiting in limbo. Once all characters have stepped through, they arrive at the next area together.  
- **No Escape.** The Crooked Nightmare is a demiplane. Spells cast for the purpose of leaving it fail, as do effects that banish a creature to another plane of existence.  
- **Vermin.** The Crooked Nightmare is infested with the vermin favored by the Vermintoll Coven. Whenever a character fails a Wisdom (Perception) or Intelligence (Investigation) check, rather than spotting or finding nothing, they discover a weasel, centipede, or pigeon.  
- **Prophecy.** Spells that contact creatures or beings on other planes of existence instead return a feverish prophecy from Adela Druskenvald.

The little girl (Neutral, human noncombatant Commoner) is fearful of what her imaginary friend Frederick has been telling her. She explains that Frederick, her doll, is a toy version of a one-eyed beast that looks like a mix between a turkey and dinosaur.

She becomes increasingly nervous if the characters approach, telling them that Frederick is very hungry and wants to devour their flesh. She whispers that Frederick will devour her flesh if she lets go of him. She is scared and doesn't know what to do. Use the Dark Wood map for combat in this area.

##### Little Adela

The mole on the girl's right cheek seems familiar. A character who succeeds on a DC 15 Intelligence (Investigation) check deduces this must be Adela Druskenvald as a child.

##### Frederick

When a character moves within 5 feet of Adela's stuffed creature, it instantly transforms into a [Gobblegeist](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/monstrosity/gobblegeist-crookedmoon24.md) and attacks the characters. When the gobblegeist is reduced to 0 <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Hit Points</span>, it reverts to a stuffed toy.

After Frederick is defeated, little Adela apologizes for her imaginary friend and blames the "Bad Bug Lady." At this mention, the cackling voice of Stonoga Blackstinger fills the clearing. Little Adela's eyes go wide, and in an instant, she is dragged screaming by an Invisible force into the woods.

##### Crooked Door

When the characters follow the direction little Adela is dragged, they find a strange, crooked door standing on the forest floor, covered in child-sized white handprints. Any attempt to leave the area around the door triggers the Crooked Nightmare's Infinite Dreams feature. The door opens, beckoning to the characters; it lead{@s }s to area N3.

##### Treasure

When little Adela is whisked away, she drops her stuffed Frederick. The toy acts as a [Corn Dolly](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/construct/corn-dolly-crookedmoon24.md) familiar.

##### Saving Gilly

Gilly Jenkin is secured to the spit by three heavy, locked chains. Even if the characters don't attack Stonoga, roll Initiative! All three chains must be removed within 10 rounds or Gilly succumbs to fatal burns.

The chains have AC 17, HP 1, a damage threshold of 40, and <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Immunity</span> to Bludgeoning, Piercing, Poison, Psychic, and Slashing damage. While trapped by the chains, Gilly can't be targeted by spells or other magical effects.

A chain can be unlocked, broken, slipped, or otherwise escaped with any of the following DC 20 ability checks made as a Utilize action:

- Strength (Athletics) check to break a chain  
- Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) check using Thieves' Tools to pick a lock  
- Intelligence (Investigation) check to find a weak link in a chain that can be easily pried or broken  
- Other appropriate checks at your discretion  

Each round on <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Initiative</span> counts 20 and 10 (losing <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Initiative</span> ties), the oven's inferno sends forth a burst of fire that randomly targets a character or Gilly. The target must succeed on a DC 17 Dexterity saving throw or take `dice:2d10|noform|noparens|avg|text(11)` (`2d10`) Fire damage. If Gilly is targeted, the number of rounds she can survive decreases by 1. These bursts continue until all characters have left the area or died.

When all three chains are removed, Gilly falls free from the spit. If she is alive, she is terrified and in pain. She reacts as if hearing a voice that the characters can't. She calls out to her daughter Dani, who pulls her out of the dream in a swirl of blue mist.

##### Roast Gobblegeist

The roasting beast on the spit is the monstrous form of the creature that little Adela had called Frederick. Dealing 10 Slashing damage to the roast slices off a portion of delectable meat. As a Utilize action, a character can eat a piece of meat and regain `dice:2d8|noform|noparens|avg|text(9)` (`2d8`) <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Hit Points</span>. Only the first three portions of meat carved off have healing properties.

##### Crooked Door

After Gilly Jenkin escapes or dies, a crooked door appears in the flames of the oven, leading to area N4. Creatures that pass through the door must succeed on a DC 17 Constitution saving throw or gain 1 Exhaustion level from the heat. A creature with <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Resistance</span> or <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Immunity</span> to Fire damage automatically succeeds on the save.

##### Teenage Adela

The voice clearly belongs to Adela Druskenvald (Neutral, human noncombatant Commoner), though a decade older than her form in the forest. When she arrives at the pulsing meteorite, she stares at it with awe and beckons the characters to join her so they can all travel beyond the stars together. A successful DC 15 Wisdom (Insight) check makes it clear that she is enchanted by the fallen star. She is currently Charmed by the Whispers from Beyond.

##### Whispers from Beyond

When a creature steps over the ridge and into the sloping crater, they hear gibbering, incomprehensible whispers and must make a DC 17 Intelligence saving throw. On a failure, the creature has the Charmed condition, and while Charmed in this way, it must use as much of its movement as possible each round to move toward the meteorite. On a success, the creature is immune to this effect for 24 hours. A Charmed creature can repeat the saving throw whenever it takes damage, or at the start of its turns while it can't see the meteorite, ending the effect on a success.

Characters who speak Deep Speech can puzzle out that the whispers are inviting them on a trip through the stars to realms beyond comprehension, but such a voyage requires leaving their bodies behind. Only their minds can make the trip.

##### Space Invaders

The first time a character moves within 5 feet of the meteorite, it cracks and an [Invader Out of Space](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/aberration/invader-out-of-space-crookedmoon24.md) rises from within, attacking the characters until it's destroyed. Any Disrupting Touch attacks that hit creatures Charmed by Whispers From Beyond are <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Critical Hits</span>. Creatures killed from damage in this way are turned to dust except for their brain or equivalent organ responsible for thought.

##### Fallen Star

On <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Initiative</span> count 20 (losing <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Initiative</span> ties), the cracked meteorite pulses with its strange, alien colors. Creatures within 60 feet of the fallen star that can see the colors must succeed on a DC 15 Intelligence saving throw or roll on the Fallen Star table to determine the alien experience and its effect. Aberrations succeed on the save automatically.

##### Decanter

The glass decanter has <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Immunity</span> to all damage and can't be moved. Its stopper can't be removed by any means from outside, but it can easily be pressed up from within if Walter chooses to escape. However, the mental torture that he is suffering makes him unaware of this solution and unable to do anything but impotently bang on the glass in agonized desperation.

##### Wisp

Even if the characters previously killed the malicious ketgrinn during the events of the Crooked House, this nightmare version of Wisp has stolen Walter's voice once more. Now colossal, Wisp grins and speaks with not just the voice of Walter, but also Gilly, Dani, Mayor Somerton, and other townsfolk as you see fit. He spews vicious words to keep the man paralyzed in self-loathing.

Wisp looks into the bookshelf with only his head and paws visible, attacking the characters as he wraps Walter in despair. He uses the Sphinx of Lore stat block with the following changes: his alignment is Chaotic Evil, his size is Gargantuan, his creature type is Fey, and his Claw attacks deal Psychic damage. Wisp vanishes with a yowl if reduced to 0 <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Hit Points</span>.

##### Saving Walter

The liquor level continually rises in the great decanter imprisoning Walter. He is terrified and clutches his head in agony. On <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Initiative</span> count 0 (losing <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Initiative</span> ties) on the 10th round, Walter drowns in the liquor.

Only Walter can save himself, but he can't do it alone. He can be snapped out of his nightmare-induced powerlessness by appealing to the most defining aspect of his personality: his sense of loyalty. This can be accomplished through encouragement or by him seeing those he cares about in grave danger. He starts with loyalty 0, and he gains loyalty when any of the following occur:

- **Rally (Gain 1 Loyalty).** An attack made against Wisp scores a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Critical Hit</span>.  
- **Resolve (Gain 1 Loyalty).** An attack against a character scores a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Critical Hit</span>, or a character fails a saving throw Wisp forced.  
- **Victory (Gain 3 Loyalty).** Wisp is reduced to 0 <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Hit Points</span>.  
- **Encourage (Gain 1 Loyalty).** As an Influence action, a character succeeds on a DC 20 ability check using one of these approaches, or any other that you deem appropriate:  

- Charisma (Deception) to distract Wisp from unleashing more mental torture.  
- Charisma (Persuasion or Intimidation) to inspire him to block out the verbal abuse or to pull himself together.  
- Intelligence (History or Nature) to use logic to help Walter realize that the nightmare is full of lies.  

When his loyalty reaches 6, Walter snaps himself out of his tortured inaction. He pushes the glass stopper out of the decanter and climbs out, horrifically shaken and scarred but alive. He reacts as if hearing a voice only he can hear and calls out to his daughter Dani, who pulls him from the nightmare in a burst of blue mist.

##### Crooked Door

After Walter Jenkin escapes or dies, a crooked door appears in an image of Wisp's enormous grin of sharp teeth. To pass through, characters must succeed on a DC 17 Dexterity saving throw or take `dice:2d10|noform|noparens|avg|text(11)` (`2d10`) Slashing damage. This door leads to area N6.

- Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) check to slip the root free  
- Intelligence (Investigation) check to find the right knots in the roots to easily undo one

### Back In Wickermoor Village

Wickermoor Village is in chaos. The scheming cultists among the Followers of the Old Ways have made their move and initiated the ritual to sacrifice Adela Druskenvald and countless more innocents atop Rowan's Rise, the highest hill in the moors north of Wickermoor Village.

Once the construction of the Wicker Man was complete, cult members from across the valley donned uncanny animal masks and descended upon Wickermoor Village, kidnapping victims from the streets and their homes and dragging them out into the moors. Which NPCs have turned evil and joined the cultists and which are at risk of being sacrificed are up to you to escalate the dramatic tension of forthcoming events (see Wicker's Vigil).
