# Swift Witchcraft
*Source: The Crooked Moon (2024) p. 115*  

**Prerequisite**: 4th; Spellcasting or Pact Magic feature

You have honed your skills in the rituals of witchcraft and can quickly weave your magic in times of need. You gain the following benefits.

**Ability Score Increase.** Increase your Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma by 1, to a maximum of 20.

**Swift Invocation.** You can use an action to cast a spell that has a casting time of 1 minute. Once you use this benefit, you can't do so again until you finish a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Long Rest</span>.
