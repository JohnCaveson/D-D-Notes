# The Beast of Blight

## Regional Effects
_Source: The Crooked Moon (2024)_

The region containing the Beast of Blight's lair ischanged by its presence, creating the following effects:

- **Fouling Blight.** Within 6 miles of the lair, ability checks made to forage for food or water have <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Disadvantage</span>, and magic that creates food or water only produces half the normal amount.  
- **Rapid Decay.** Within 1 mile of the lair, objects that aren't worn or carried have <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Vulnerability</span> to Necrotic damage, and the length of time in which a dead creature can be brought back to life is halved.  

If the Beast of Blight dies or moves its lair elsewhere, these effects end immediately.
