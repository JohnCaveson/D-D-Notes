# Appendix D: Music
*Source: The Crooked Moon Part Two: Monsters & Adventure Campaign, p. 624* 

Alongside this book are three music albums by The Blasting Company that serve as companion pieces to the adventure of *The Crooked Moon*, all available on most streaming platforms. However, they can be used to set the tone for any Folk Horror adventure you wish to run. Below is suggested guidance for how to use all three albums to maximize the atmosphere of your dreadful folktales.

## The Crooked Moon Soundtrack

The first album acts as a soundtrack for this book's adventure campaign. In most cases, these songs aren't literally occurring in the "canon" of the world (non-diegetic music) and can be utilized for conveying the tone of each character or encounter. Alternatively, if you wish to run a more musical campaign, you can find creative ways to incorporate each song into the context of the adventure (diegetic music). The list below overviews the characters and scenarios that have inspired each song.

**Crooked Moon**. A cultist of the Old Ways tells of the folklore and superstitions of Wickermoor Hollow.

### When It's Time

The Vagrant welcomes wayward souls aboard the *Ghostlight Express* for their final journey.

### Impossible

Phillip and Adela Druskenvald share a tender moment and celebrate what they've built.

### The Echoes

The spirit of Joseph Patrini laments the grisly fate of the inhabitants of the Crooked House.

### Coal Eyed Birds

Ol' Jericho Sticks is manipulated by his inner demon Virgil to join together as Raum.

### Under Your Shadow

Father Renathyr leads worship of the devil goddess Viraxys, still wounded by lost love.

### By the Lagoon

Mister Crossroads makes a deal with a sinner and takes them to the Dead Man's Hand.

### Lullaby of the Sea

Matron Lethica sings a lullaby to Hugo, still consumed by the grief of her lost love.

### Dead Trees

Gorthos dwells on who she once was, whom she once loved, and what she has become.

### Cold Iron

Yorgrim tends to his penitent duties as the twisted souls of the Shroud corrupt him.

### No Fun to Die

Chuckles wakes up to lead a parade of revelry and usher in rebirth in Wickermoor Hollow.

### Sing Ye Vermin

The Vermintoll Coven meets to brew a magic concoction and commune with their vermin.

### Light of My Life

Adela reflects on her life and love before meeting a fiery fate in the Wicker Man.

### Born King

Kehlenn takes her revenge against Phillip and transforms him into the Horned King.

### Nightwind

The Vagrant returns at the end of the journey to take the survivors to their next destination.
