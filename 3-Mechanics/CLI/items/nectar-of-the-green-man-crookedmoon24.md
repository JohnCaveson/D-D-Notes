# Nectar of the Green Man
*Potion, very rare*  
![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheGiddyLimit/homebrew-img/refs/heads/main/img/CrookedMoon24/Items/Nectar%20of%20the%20Green%20Man.webp#right)


When you drink this potion, you gain the effect of the Commune with Nature spell, but your experience is filled with terror and dread. You must make a DC 15 Wisdom saving throw. On a failure, for the next 8 hours, you have <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Disadvantage</span> on attack rolls and ability checks while you can see a forest. You automatically succeed on the save if you have <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Immunity</span> to the Frightened condition.

This bottle is carved from wood, with a green, leafy face adorning its surface. The liquid is a deep green streaked with shades of umber.

## Heightened Potency

You can learn all five facts from Commune with Nature.

*Source: The Crooked Moon (2024) p. 616*
