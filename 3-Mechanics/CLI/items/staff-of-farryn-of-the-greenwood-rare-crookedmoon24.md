# Staff of Farryn of the Greenwood (Rare)
*Staff, weapon, rare (requires attunement)*  
![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheGiddyLimit/homebrew-img/refs/heads/main/img/CrookedMoon24/Items/Staff%20of%20Farryn%20of%20the%20Greenwood.webp#right)

- **Damage**:
  - One-handed: 1d6 bludgeoning
  - Two-handed: 1d8 bludgeoning
- **Properties**: [Fabled Heirloom](/3-Mechanics/CLI/item-properties.md#Fabled%20Heirloom), [Versatile](/3-Mechanics/CLI/item-properties.md#Versatile)
- **Weight**: 4.0 lbs.

This twisted wooden staff is crowned by a trinket-adorned deer's skull. It was fashioned by Farryn before she delved into the dark lore of Gorthos. Soft, fragrant moss and earthy mushrooms grow along the staff's length.

While holding the staff, you can use it as a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Spellcasting Focus</span> for your spells, you gain a +1 bonus to spell attack rolls and your spell save DC determined by the staff's rarity, and you can use the following properties.

## Living Ward (Rare+)

You have <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Resistance</span> to Necrotic damage.

## Servant of the Cycle (Rare+)

You can cast Cure Wounds (level 3 version) from the staff. When you do so, the next time you hit with an attack before the end of your next turn, the target takes an extra `dice:1d10|noform|noparens|avg` (`1d10`) Necrotic damage. This property can be used three times, regaining all expended uses daily at dusk.

*Source: The Crooked Moon (2024) p. 605*
