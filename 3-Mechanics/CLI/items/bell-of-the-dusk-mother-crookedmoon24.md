# Bell of the Dusk Mother
*Wondrous item (requires attunement)*  
![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheGiddyLimit/homebrew-img/refs/heads/main/img/CrookedMoon24/Items/Bell%20of%20the%20Dusk%20Mother.webp#right)

- **Properties**: [Fabled Heirloom](/3-Mechanics/CLI/item-properties.md#Fabled%20Heirloom)

This heavy iron handbell is elaborately decorated with filigree. Its handle is a large ring, and its clapper hangs from a chain within. Its only sound is ever-mournful tolling.

While holding the bell, you can use it as a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Spellcasting Focus</span> for your spells, you gain a bonus to spell attack rolls and your spell save DC determined by its rarity.
