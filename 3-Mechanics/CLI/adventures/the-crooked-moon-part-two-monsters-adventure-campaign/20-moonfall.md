# Moonfall
*Source: The Crooked Moon Part Two: Monsters & Adventure Campaign, p. 469* 

![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheGiddyLimit/homebrew-img/refs/heads/main/img/CrookedMoon24/Adventure/335-19-001.moonfall-opener.webp#center)

> [!quote] A quote from Rowan  
> 
> The moon knows you, stranger. It has been waiting since the first stone was laid, since the first bone was buried. She comes to claim what was promised. And if you meet her mad gaze, it's too late-they've already chosen you.

The horrors the characters have overcome were merely a prelude to the nightmare that now looms over Wickermoor Hollow. Though Kehlenn's influence has been severed from the land, her imprisonment within the Crooked Moon above did not put an end to her primeval power. The defeated archfey seeks her final revenge: the total destruction of the realm she once ruled. Her leering gaze settles on Wickermoor Hollow for the last time, awakening eight ancient beings of immense power to bring the moon crashing down upon Druskenvald, destroying everyone and everything within.

With Kehlenn's defeat at the Crooked Tree, a fragile peace has settled over Wickermoor Hollow, but it is a peace born of fear and uncertainty. Even in the face of victory, the people of the valley have been forever changed by the horrors that have occurred over the past weeks and months. While many inhabitants of the valley may not know the events that occurred in the Wytchwood and how close The Horned King came to annihilating them, they all soon learn of the new doom descending from above.

The moon's grin has curled into a sneer as it wakes eight eons-old powers still tethered to the land. With the promise of a transformation of the domain into their own image, these mythic beings call the moon down from the night sky to eventually crash into Wickermoor Hollow. The resulting cataclysm would destroy the whole of Druskenvald and remake it as Kehlenn and her champions see fit. The characters must act quickly, or all they have fought to save will be lost with the moon's apocalyptic embrace.

## Endgame

This optional chapter offers guidance for leading level 13 characters to the pinnacle of level 20, providing the inspiration to craft challenging and harrowing encounters that will test their might and resolve. This chapter is aimed at tables that favor epic tiers of play and want to experience grandiose stakes of high fantasy.

If you wish to continue the campaign after the main events of *The Crooked Moon*, this chapter presents a flexible framework that challenges players while maintaining the unsettling dread central to folk horror. If you plan on following this path, the characters should not reach level 14 after defeating The Horned King. Instead, each character increases one ability score of their choice by 2. This increase can't raise a score above 24.

Characters who seek to prevent Kehlenn's final vengeance have the following goals:

- Seek out each of the eight awakened powers of Wickermoor Hollow.  
- Destroy them and sever their tether to the Crooked Moon.  
- Banish Kehlenn to the Crooked Moon for all time.  

Many of the specifics of how the characters embark on this final adventure will be up to you to decide. If the adventure incorporated Fateweaving, you must decide how the various climaxes and catharses of the characters would affect the events presented in this chapter.

## The Moon is Falling

One terrible night, the Crooked Moon lets out a horrific shriek and speaks with Kehlenn's voice, delivering a message that echoes across Wickermoor Hollow: the moon shall fall, and doom will come in thirteen nights. Slowly but surely, the moon begins to grow larger in the sky as it descends toward the valley.

The inhabitants of Wickermoor Hollow react in a variety of ways—some turn to panic, others to prayer, and surprisingly, many resign themselves to the end. However, most are too preoccupied with the destructive effects of the eight evils that have risen and the horrors they've unleashed upon the land. This development will stress or empower the relationships that the characters have nurtured during their time in Wickermoor Hollow.

### Impending Doom

The characters must now hunt down these emerging evils, racing against time as each victory delays the moon's fall—but not its inevitable approach. Further, every moment wasted allows the once-dormant threats to grow in power and cause mass destruction ahead of the impending apocalypse.

### Countdown to Extinction

As soon as the Crooked Moon begins falling, it will crash into Wickermoor Hollow in exactly thirteen nights. If this occurs, the resulting collision and explosion of primordial energy annihilates the entirety of Druskenvald, including the characters if they are still within the realm when the moon makes impact. This ends the campaign.

#### Achieving Victory

The only way to prevent Druskenvald's downfall is to kill all eight of the ancient evils bound to Wickermoor Hollow and reject Kehlenn's final bargain. This returns the Crooked Moon to its rightful place in the sky and ensures that the Crooked Queen is powerless forever.

#### Tracking Time

As each of the thirteen nights progresses, keep track of how many nights are left before the inevitable collision. Clearly describe to the players that the moon is growing larger in the sky and its face is growing more menacing. Also, consider how the people of the valley act in the face of a seemingly inevitable death.

#### Delaying the Inevitable

Each time one of the ancient evils is slain, the descent of the moon stops for 24 hours. This means that if the characters continually kill the powerful enemies before them, they have a full twenty nights to stop the doom.

#### Turn Back Time

If you wish to give the characters additional time or believe they have earned help, the Vagrant arrives right before the collision and aids them. He plays a powerful song on his fiddle that turns back time 72 hours, buying the characters three additional nights. Due to the timeless nature of the ancient evils, any of them killed before or during this reverted time remain dead.

### The Old Fears

Eight fearsome beings bound to the land of Wickermoor Hollow stir and rise to power, having felt the gaze of the Crooked Moon upon them. Imbued with Kehlenn's primordial energies, they have exchanged their own ties to Wickermoor Hollow so that the moon can continue to plummet toward a cataclysm with the promise to remake the realm in their own image. They are as follows:

[The Galloping Headsman](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/npc/the-galloping-headsman-crookedmoon24.md). A headless phantom of fiery wrath driven by revenge.

[The White Worm](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/npc/the-white-worm-crookedmoon24.md). An eldritch being from beyond the stars that feasts on memories.

[The Tall Man](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/npc/the-tall-man-crookedmoon24.md). A twisted and corrupted archfey that stalks the woods for victims.

[The Wild Titan](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/npc/the-wild-titan-crookedmoon24.md). A gigantic manifestation of the wilds that seeks to unleash savagery.

[The Barrow King](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/npc/the-barrow-king-crookedmoon24.md). A long-dead king seeking glory who commands Undead legions.

[The Jinxed Leviathan](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/npc/the-jinxed-leviathan-crookedmoon24.md). An enormous swamp beast imbued with the essence of bad luck.

[The Dusk Mother](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/npc/the-dusk-mother-crookedmoon24.md). A tormented spirit of loss that spreads her misery to all.

[The Brimstone Behemoth](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/npc/the-brimstone-behemoth-crookedmoon24.md). A volcanic colossus of hellfire that ushers in armageddon.

The characters must defeat these legendary creatures in order, each one more powerful than the last, if they hope to save Druskenvald. The subsequent sections of this chapter feature the eight ancient evils, including the following tools to craft an exhilarating adventure for each:

- A quest hook that points the characters in the direction of the ancient evil.  
- The effects of the legendary creature's presence on the land, which are in addition to its lair features.  
- How the locals are tormented, affected, and corrupted by it.  
- What types of monsters and minions that might serve it.  
- The circumstances of the final showdown with the powerful being.  
- How the afflicted people react to the destruction of the creature.  

## The Galloping Headsman

![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheGiddyLimit/homebrew-img/refs/heads/main/img/CrookedMoon24/Adventure/336-19-003.the-galloping-headsman.webp#center)

Centuries ago, a centaur served as an executioner to the people who first founded Foxwillow. Due to corruption at the highest levels of the town's leadership, this headsman was falsely accused of murder, dragged through an unjust trial, and beheaded before the whole town. While its townsfolk exclaimed justice, the blood that spilled on the soil bound the centaur's spirit to the fields. With the power of the Crooked Moon, [The Galloping Headsman](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/npc/the-galloping-headsman-crookedmoon24.md) thunders through the farmland, seeking justice against his killers' descendants, whipping them up in a frenzy of paranoia that spelled his doom so many years ago.

### Hysteria in Foxwillow

The distrust sown by the secrets revealed during the Harvest Terror's attack has boiled into all-out conflict, plunging the farming community into mass hysteria. A farmer from the town seeks out the characters and tells them that neighbor has turned against neighbor, and folks are being condemned to death by beheading. The executioner is rumored to be a spirit of fiery wrath that rides to meet each of the condemned, lops off their heads, and adds them to its collection.

#### Fields of Vengeance

The fertile farmland around Foxwillow—so easily corrupted by the demonic influence of Raum—craves vengeance and has come to life under the ghastly influence of The Galloping Headsman.

##### Stone Pressing

The earth itself opens up, attempting to swallow the accused and crush them with the stones within. For every mile traveled, characters must succeed on a DC 17 Strength saving throw or fall into a sinkhole and have the Restrained condition. While Restrained in this way, a character takes `dice:1d8|noform|noparens|avg|text(4)` (`1d8`) Bludgeoning damage at the start of each of their turns. A Restrained character repeats the save at the end of each of their turns, freeing themself on a success.

##### Choking Crops

The crops attempt to choke the life out of the wicked. The first time a character enters any field that bears crops, its roots, stalks, or vines attempt to strangle the character. They must succeed on a DC 17 Strength saving throw or gain the Grappled condition for 1 minute (escape DC 15). While Grappled in this way, the character gains 1 Exhaustion level at the end of each of their turns. A character who can't be Charmed succeeds automatically. The crops have AC 5; HP 10; <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Vulnerability</span> to Fire damage; <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Immunity</span> to Bludgeoning and Psychic damage.

#### Mob Injustice

When the characters arrive at the Foxwillow Farmland, they find its people are <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Hostile</span> to them, accusing them of witchcraft, murder, sabotage, and of causing the incoming apocalypse. Some cunning townsfolk even attempt to act as friends to earn their trust before turning them over to frenzied mobs.

##### Vengeful Wrath

The wrathful emotion of The Galloping Headsman spreads like a virulent plague. Whenever a character comes within 20 feet of a Foxwillow villager, the character must succeed on a DC 17 Charisma saving throw or lose the ability to distinguish friend from foe, regarding all creatures it can see as enemies. On a success, the character is immune to this effect for 24 hours.

##### Wandering Mob

Roving gangs of vigilantes hunt the characters in the hopes of capturing, imprisoning, trying, and executing them. Every 1 hour that the characters spend within 1 mile of the town center of Foxwillow, they must succeed on a group DC 17 Dexterity (Stealth) check or be detected and attacked by 20 Toughs.

#### All Hallows' Evil

The emergence of [The Galloping Headsman](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/npc/the-galloping-headsman-crookedmoon24.md) has twisted the Foxwillow farmland's flora, fauna, and people, and it has awakened monstrous creatures with a burning vengeance:

##### Vengeful Spirits

The Headsman has riled up the spirits of the dead that faced wrongful deaths and seek retribution. These take the form of Centaurs (Undead), [Kackles](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/undead/kackle-crookedmoon24.md), [Phantom Hearses](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/undead/phantom-hearse-crookedmoon24.md), [Pumpkin Heads](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/fiend/pumpkin-head-crookedmoon24.md), [Wayward Haints](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/undead/wayward-haint-crookedmoon24.md), Will-o-Wisps, and Wraiths.

##### Monsters of the Fields

Livestock and scavenger birds have been twisted by the Headsman's vengeful fury. These take the form of Cockatrices, [Corvodaemons](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/fiend/corvodaemon-crookedmoon24.md), [Crowstorms](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/fiend/crowstorm-crookedmoon24.md), [Drakkenhobs](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/dragon/drakkenhob-crookedmoon24.md), [Gobblegeists](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/monstrosity/gobblegeist-crookedmoon24.md), Gorgons, Minotaurs of Baphomet, and Nightmares.

### Vengeance's Reckoning

Some townsfolk whisper of a shadowy figure that looks like a rider on horseback but joined as one. They revere this being as an angel of judgment, punishing their town for the wickedness of their neighbors. The characters must learn where they can find this spirit to put an end to the mass hysteria.

Tarnished History. The characters can investigate Foxwillow to learn about the village's dark past. If the characters succeed on a group DC 17 Intelligence (History or Investigation) check, they learn the headsman's story and that he was beheaded before the covered bridge to the east of town toward the edge of Wickermoor Hollow.

#### Bloody Trail

The characters can tail a mob as it dispenses its "justice" or even infiltrate its ranks with a successful group DC 17 Dexterity (Stealth) or Charisma (Deception) check to witness the attempted execution of a falsely accused villager.

#### The Headless Horseman

Characters who arrive at the covered bridge can lure out the vengeful spirit with the offering of the execution of a Foxwillow local or by inciting its warped sense of justice. [The Galloping Headsman](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/npc/the-galloping-headsman-crookedmoon24.md) rides out from the darkness of the bridge's cover and charges the characters, seeking to behead them all and slake its never-ending thirst for vengeance.

##### Reckoning's End

With The Galloping Headsman's destruction, the town is freed from his oppressive judgment. The people of Foxwillow gain clarity and realize that they must aid their neighbors in these final nights. Grateful to the characters, they decide to spend their final hours feasting and celebrating instead of falling to despair. The Crooked Moon shifts, its gaze turning toward Moonsong Lake.

##### Level Up

The characters reach level 14, if they are still level 13, when they destroy The Galloping Headsman.

##### Fabled Heirloom

The [Axe of The Galloping Headsman](/3-Mechanics/CLI/items/axe-of-the-galloping-headsman-crookedmoon24.md) is left behind after the vengeful spirit is destroyed.

## The White Worm

A once unfathomable horror from a forgotten reality beyond the stars, the eldritch entity known as [The White Worm](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/npc/the-white-worm-crookedmoon24.md) was pulled through the veil by the rituals of Matron Lethica. Imprisoned for many years beneath Memory's Rest Sanatorium, it feasted upon the memories of the hospital's patients before escaping into the depths of Moonsong Lake. After recovering from its long torment and being fed countless ancient memories by the Crooked Moon, it has undergone a metamorphosis into its true form as a cosmic horror. It now ravenously hungers for the memories of Druskenvald until the land itself is forgotten.

### A Feast for Worms

Moonsong Lake is infested with worms. These parasites slither through the water supply and infect the people who live upon its shores. Memories are devoured, personalities are reshaped, and bodies are contorted into horrific abominations of alien biology. A member of a small fishing village approaches the characters and begs them to end the infestation and restore the identities of the mindless victims.

#### Lake of The White Worm

The waters of Moonsong Lake and its estuary are tainted with alien parasites that sap memories, feeding them to The White Worm and spawning more thralls for the hivemind.

##### Water of Forgetfulness

Any character who drinks from the water of Moonsong Lake, or any well, river, or stream within 5 miles of it, must succeed on a DC 17 Wisdom saving throw or have the Poisoned condition for 8 hours. While Poisoned in this way, the character loses all memories from before the moment they drank the water. A character who succeeds on this save is immune to this effect for 24 hours.

##### Parasite Found

Any character immersed in the water of Moonsong Lake must succeed on a DC 17 Constitution saving throw or be infected with a spawn of The White Worm. Unless the character receives a Lesser Restoration spell or similar magic, they transform into a [Wormhost Surgeon](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/aberration/wormhost-surgeon-crookedmoon24.md) in 7 days.

#### A Strange Folk

When the characters arrive on the shores of Moonsong Lake, they find the fishing villages filled with folk who are oddly at peace with the coming doom. Some sit catatonically with no recollection of who they are or who they once were. Those who have not forgotten their memories are secretly <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Hostile</span> to the characters and seek to turn them into thralls of The White Worm.

##### Cult of the Worm

White Worm cultists cast Dominate Person (DC 17) on any character who is alone with them.

##### Shattered Psyche

The minds of those who have forgotten themselves are filled with oblivion. Any character who uses magic to attempt to read a villager's thoughts or look into their mind must succeed on a DC 17 Intelligence saving throw or be affected by the Befuddlement spell.

#### Things From Beyond

The reawakening of [The White Worm](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/npc/the-white-worm-crookedmoon24.md) has twisted the minds of the lake's denizens to her alien will, and a tear to its home reality has opened at the bottom of the lake, unleashing unspeakable things into the dark water.

##### Deep Dwellers

The creatures that lurk in the depths of Moonsong Lake and are influenced by the great memory devourer include Giant Crabs, Giant Toads, Giant Octopi, Hydras, [Lake Dredgers](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/undead/lake-dredger-crookedmoon24.md), Plesiosauruses, Swarm of Piranhas, [Wellwyrms](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/dragon/wellwyrm-crookedmoon24.md), and worm swarms (use the Swarm of Venomous Snakes stat block).

##### Alien Invaders

Entities from beyond the stars drift through the veil into Druskenvald. These take the form of Aboleths, Chuul, Cloakers, Gibbering Mouthers, [Invaders Out of Space](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/aberration/invader-out-of-space-crookedmoon24.md), [Mutant Nurses](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/aberration/mutant-nurse-crookedmoon24.md), and [Wormhost Surgeons](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/aberration/wormhost-surgeon-crookedmoon24.md).

### The Hunger Below

The people of the lakeside babble and sometimes shriek of the "thing in the lake" and its unending hunger that seeks to consume all memory of Druskenvald. Rumors of ancient ruins built by the original denizens of the lake hold the key to the creature's origin and goal. The characters must find the tear in reality at the bottom of the lake to restore memory to the people.

#### Black Obelisks

The characters can search for ancient ruins of the original lake dwellers. After locating the ruins, they can study the carvings. With a successful DC 17 Intelligence (Arcana or Investigation) check, the characters learn of The White Worm's origin and the signs to find it.

#### Into the Cult

The characters can feign interest in willfully joining the esoteric order that worships The White Worm with a successful group DC 17 Charisma (Deception or Persuasion) check, learning its secrets.

#### The Eldritch Horror

Characters who find a way to brave the water and survive its crushing depths find reality and their minds warp the deeper they delve. Once they discover the tear into the long-forgotten reality, [The White Worm](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/npc/the-white-worm-crookedmoon24.md) emerges into the black water and attempts to devour the characters in both body and mind.

##### Remembering Once More

With the destruction of The White Worm, its tether to Druskenvald is broken, and its essence is ripped into the tear in reality as the rift seals forever. The parasitic worms wither and die, and the water becomes clean once more. The people around the lake regain their memories, though many wish they hadn't, for now they are gripped with the terror of their inevitable doom. The Crooked Moon, ever closer, looks to Hartsblight Forest.

##### Level Up

The characters reach level 15, if they are still level 14, when they destroy The White Worm.

##### Fabled Heirloom

The [Eye of The White Worm](/3-Mechanics/CLI/items/eye-of-the-white-worm-crookedmoon24.md) can be claimed as a grotesque trophy from the withered corpse.

## The Tall Man

Once a slender and agile archfey of the Greenwood who danced to the music of the forest, [The Tall Man](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/npc/the-tall-man-crookedmoon24.md) was twisted and rotted into an empty husk of his former self. Now, he silently lurks within the shadows between the trees. Emboldened by the Crooked Moon, he seeks to lure unsuspecting travelers to the woods so he may stalk his prey in a wicked game of cat and mouse. Once his prey is immobilized by terror, he uses his huge, gnarled fingers to snatch them up and dine on their flesh, made all the more delectable by their fear.

### Terror Between the Trees

![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheGiddyLimit/homebrew-img/refs/heads/main/img/CrookedMoon24/Adventure/338-19-005.the-tall-man.webp#center)

Wicked things lurk in the recovering woods of Hartsblight Forest. The shadows grow long as the moon draws nearer, and the people nearest to the forest's edge are consumed by waking nightmares and entranced by dancing shadows that lure them into the thicket. Members of a small logging settlement seek out the characters and ask for help finding those who are missing.

#### Where Shadows Reign

With the destruction of Gorthos and the empowerment of the Crooked Moon, The Tall Man has made Hartsblight Forest his domain of terror, creating the ideal hunting grounds for the horrifying archfey and his corrupted court.

##### Fear of the Dark

For every hour of travel through Hartsblight Forest, each character must succeed on a DC 18 Wisdom saving throw or have <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Disadvantage</span> on ability checks and attack rolls for 8 hours. A creature that can't be Frightened succeeds automatically.

##### Trick of Shadows

Throughout Hartsblight Forest, shadowy silhouettes of The Tall Man linger between the trees. Characters who move within 10 feet of these silhouettes are attacked by four [Lurkers in the Dark](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/fey/lurker-in-the-dark-crookedmoon24.md).

#### Easy Prey

When the characters arrive at the settlements near the forest's edge, they are greeted by horrified shrieks and find the communities mostly empty. Those that remain are <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Indifferent</span> toward the characters and can't rip themselves from their waking nightmares. Any who suffer these nightmares wander into the woods and become lost.

##### Fear and Panic

Whenever the characters find a missing villager in the forest, the villager soon begins to scream in terror, drawing the attention of The Tall Man or his minions, unless the characters calm them with a successful group DC 18 Charisma (Deception or Persuasion) check.

##### Lost and Alone

Voices of the lost cry out to the characters, but the forest distorts their location. For every hour of travel through Hartsblight Forest, the characters must succeed on a group DC 18 Wisdom (Perception or Survival) check or spend the next 3 hours wandering through the woods, making no progress, and not finding the lost villager.

#### Court of Fear and Shadow

With newfound dominion over the forest, all of the dark wood serve [The Tall Man](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/npc/the-tall-man-crookedmoon24.md), whether they be the denizens of the forest once blighted by Gorthos or the vicious members of his fey court.

##### Rotten Hearts

The denizens of Hartsblight Forest now dance with The Tall Man, including Death Dogs, [Festerhulks](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/undead/festerhulk-crookedmoon24.md), Giant Elks (Undead), [Gloomwoods](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/plant/gloomwood-crookedmoon24.md), [Hypnovulfen](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/monstrosity/hypnovulfen-crookedmoon24.md), [Keepers of the Blight](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/fey/keeper-of-the-blight-crookedmoon24.md), Owlbears, [Rotweavers](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/fey/rotweaver-crookedmoon24.md), and Treants.

##### Bumps in the Night

The corrupted members of the fallen fey court serve their lord's twisted appetites, taking the form of Dryads, Green Hags, [Heralds of Fools](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/fiend/herald-of-fools-crookedmoon24.md) (Fey), [Ketgrinn](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/fey/ketgrinn-crookedmoon24.md), [Lurkers in the Dark](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/fey/lurker-in-the-dark-crookedmoon24.md), [Revelers of Song](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/fiend/reveler-of-song-crookedmoon24.md) (Fey), [Revelers of Stories](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/fiend/reveler-of-stories-crookedmoon24.md) (Fey), and Sprites.

### Stalker in Darkness

The screeching villagers consumed by waking nightmares rave about the man between the trees, who dwells in his ruined palace of murk and misery. Rumors of the forgotten fey folk of the Greenwood are whispered by a few who know of the lithe dancing lord before he fell to rot. The characters must find his palace or lure him out to put an end to his reign of terror.

#### Fear in Folktales

The characters may seek out the folklore of these settlements, finding an old crone who knows of The Tall Man's story and his palace if they succeed on a group DC 18 Intelligence (Arcana) or Charisma (Persuasion) check.

#### Hunting the Hunter

The characters can track and lure out The Tall Man with a successful group DC 18 Wisdom (Survival) check if they use fear to draw his attention.

#### The Slender One

Whether the characters discover the ruined palace or attempt to lure the withered archfey to them, [The Tall Man](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/npc/the-tall-man-crookedmoon24.md) knows of their coming and stalks them for hours before finally striking when the creeping dread is at its most intense.

##### Face Your Fears

When The Tall Man is slain, his body breaks and crumbles like dry bark, and the looming moon's light pierces the canopy of the woodland once more as the terror lifts. Having been consumed by fear for so long, the denizens of the forest's edge can't find it in them to dread their coming deaths, and they go about their business as if nothing happened. The Crooked Moon turns to the Howlers, its light growing colder as it seeks its next champion.

##### Level Up

The characters reach level 16, if they are still level 15, when they destroy The Tall Man.

##### Fabled Heirloom

The [Hook of The Tall Man](/3-Mechanics/CLI/items/hook-of-the-tall-man-crookedmoon24.md) can be claimed by removing a finger from the butchered archfey.

## The Wild Titan

Created long ago by some unknown will for a purpose now lost to time, [The Wild Titan](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/npc/the-wild-titan-crookedmoon24.md) is the wilderness made flesh. It once wandered the remote peaks of the Howlers, the mountain chain that makes up the western border of Wickermoor Hollow, following the rune stones of its creator after the creature lost all contact. As the centuries passed, its long, heavy strides became more of a struggle, and it grew weary from its aimlessness. And so, it slumbered until the Crooked Moon instilled the beast with primal fury, the very essence of its creation. Now, it seeks to bring the mountains down upon civilization and unleash the bestial savagery within all mortal beings.

### Primal Rage

Savage howls and frigid wind echo through the mountains as colossal boulders crash upon the settlements below. A feral violence has erupted among the communities nestled in the foothills of the Howlers. Some have even lost their humanity as they transform into monsters. A miner from Dawn's Gate who lost his family to these transformations pleads for the characters to bring an end to the wrath of the wilds.

#### The Rumbling

The looming, frigid mountain range of the Howlers is treacherous, but now it stands as a monument to unbound wilderness, as if the mountains themselves seek the destruction of civilized life.

##### Rocks Fall

Whether cast down by The Wild Titan or the mountains themselves, crashing stone tumbles from icy peaks. For every mile traveled within 1 mile of the Howlers, the characters must succeed on a DC 18 Dexterity saving throw or take `dice:6d6|noform|noparens|avg|text(21)` (`6d6`) Bludgeoning damage and `dice:4d6|noform|noparens|avg|text(14)` (`4d6`) Cold damage.

##### Make No Fire

The harsh elements make it impossible for travelers to benefit from the warmth of fire. Within 5 miles of the Howlers, open flames, including torches and bonfires, are snuffed out instantly, and all Fire damage is halved.

#### The Beasts Within

When the characters arrive at Dawn's Gate or any of the mining settlements at the base of the Howlers, they find rampant destruction and few buildings standing. Grunting townsfolk incapable of speech attack on sight and try to devour flesh. Even more dreadful are the hairy monsters that still linger on the fringes of the communities and drag screaming victims into the wilderness.

##### Going Berserk

Whenever a character comes within 10 feet of a villager afflicted with primal wrath or starts a turn there, they must succeed on a DC 18 Wisdom saving throw or go berserk until the start of their next turn, perceiving all creatures as enemies. While berserk, on each of their turns, a character must move to the nearest creature and take the Attack action against it. Characters who succeed on the saving throw are immune to this effect for 24 hours.

##### Titan's Roar

Strange roars bellow from the mountains. Every hour while within 1 mile of a settlement, each character must succeed on a DC 18 Charisma saving throw or gain one Mark of The Wild Titan. When a character reaches three of these marks, they instantly transform into an [Alpengrendel](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/giant/alpengrendel-crookedmoon24.md). A Remove Curse spell removes one or two accumulated marks.

#### Where the Wild Things Are

The untamed wilderness where [The Wild Titan](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/npc/the-wild-titan-crookedmoon24.md) roams is filled with ferocious predators incensed by the howling of the hairy colossus. These include ravenous beasts and primeval entities drawn by the runestones that dot the peaks.

##### Menacing Menagerie

The creatures of the Howlers have gone berserk with the urge to destroy anything civilized, including [Alpengrendel](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/giant/alpengrendel-crookedmoon24.md), Behir, [Boneflayers](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/monstrosity/boneflayer-crookedmoon24.md), Dire Wolves, Giant Apes, Mammoths, Polar Bears, Ogres, Remorhazes, Rocs, Trolls, and Winter Wolves.

##### Spirits of the Wild

The ancient runestones of the Howlers call forth beings of the wild. These take the form of Earth Elementals, Frost Giants (Elemental), Gargoyles, Ice Mephits, Stone Giants (Elemental), Stone Golems, [Wildpyres](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/undead/wildpyre-crookedmoon24.md), [Woodwarped](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/plant/woodwarped-crookedmoon24.md), and Xorn.

### Attack on the Titan

The constant rumbling of the mountain crashing into the valley below and the howling of savage monsters cause the survivors and the villagers who remain to believe that the spirit of the mountain itself wishes to destroy them before the Crooked Moon does. Some, however, remember the tales of the titan that walks the peaks and the stones it uses as a guide, and the most cunning or desperate trackers are willing to sacrifice everything to bring an end to the titan's assault.

#### In the Runes

The characters can follow the clues left in the runestones along the mountainside. A successful group DC 18 Intelligence (History or Nature) check allows the characters to discover The Wild Titan's story and the path that it treads through the Howlers.

#### Dangerous Game

The characters encounter a grizzled hunter with a relentless dedication to kill The Wild Titan as a final triumph. A successful group DC 18 Wisdom (Survival) check aids him in finding his quarry through wilderness tracking.

#### The Abominable Wildman

When the characters make the ascent to the peaks of the Howlers and find the path of the wandering giant, [The Wild Titan](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/npc/the-wild-titan-crookedmoon24.md) makes the approach deadly with an onslaught of rockslides and avalanches. It is prepared for a brutal battle for survival, issuing a bellowing challenge and seeking to crush all civilized beings beneath its enormous feet.

##### Harder the Fall

When The Wild Titan is slain, it lets out a mournful roar, having never discovered its purpose or the reason for its creation, before tumbling down the mountain like a boulder. The settlements among the foothills, especially Dawn's Gate, regain their senses. Despite the looming apocalypse, they immediately start rebuilding their crumbled communities. The Crooked Moon shifts once again, its baleful stare focusing now on Maidenmist Cemetery.

##### Level Up

The characters reach level 17, if they are still level 16, when they destroy The Wild Titan.

##### Fabled Heirloom

The [Runestone of The Wild Titan](/3-Mechanics/CLI/items/runestone-of-the-wild-titan-crookedmoon24.md) can be found near the site of the battle once the beast has been cast off the mountain.

## The Barrow King

None living know the origin of the long-dead monarch entombed in the grand barrow of Maidenmist Cemetery, but legends of his undying army of wights, immense necromantic might, and unmatched combat prowess are told in hushed tones around campfires or the corners of taverns. Even the unrelenting primordial element of death condensed in the Shroud seeks to corrupt this ruler, but his tomb proved too impenetrable. Now, with the Crooked Moon casting its sight upon the endless graves, the one known as [The Barrow King](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/npc/the-barrow-king-crookedmoon24.md) has risen to conquer a new kingdom of the dead.

### Death Reigns Here

The graves of Maidenmist Cemetery burst open, and the shambling dead rise to join the undying legions of The Barrow King as he prepares for a bloody conquest of the living, many of whom submit to the awesome necromantic power. Every night, the army of death grows and pledges sword, bow, and axe to their monarch. An inhabitant of Wickermoor Village rushes to the characters, claiming they are the last line of defense against the march of death.

#### All the World's a Grave

The land itself floods with necromantic power as the graveyard of Maidenmist spreads beyond its cold iron fence. The earth churns, graves open up, decay of living matter quickens, and a dense, shroud-infused fog rolls across the valley.

##### Death and Decay

Any healing within 10 miles of Maidenmist Cemetery is halved.

##### Draining Shroud

The dense fog saps the lifeforce from the living. For every hour spent within 10 miles of Maidenmist Cemetery, the characters must succeed on a DC 19 Constitution saving throw or gain 1 Exhaustion level. A Death Ward, Hallow, Magic Circle, or Protection from Evil and Good spell prevents this Exhaustion.

#### The Scourge of the Damned

The Barrow King's influence spreads across the land, converting enthralled townsfolk into loyal cultists that advance his aims, Further, a plague rips through settlements that leaves its victims as mindless walking corpses.

##### Death Cults

Any villager encountered by the characters after The Barrow King rises has a 50 percent chance of being a member of a necrotic cult, which can be deduced with a successful DC 19 Wisdom (Insight) check. These cultists attempt to guide the characters to their death or infect them with necrotic plague.

##### Necrotic Pestilence

A magical contagion spreads through the population of Wickermoor Hollow. Whenever a character ends their turn within 5 feet of an infected creature, they must succeed on a DC 19 Constitution saving throw or have the Poisoned condition. While Poisoned in this way, the character takes `dice:2d12|noform|noparens|avg|text(13)` (`2d12`) Necrotic damage at the end of every hour, which decreases their Hit Point maximum by the same amount. If a character is killed by the plague, their corpse rises as a Wight 8 hours later.

#### Call the Banners

The Barrow King commands endless legions of Undead that gather in masses of corpse and spirit, preparing for their inevitable march upon the land. They are joined by twisted monsters bound to the monarch's will.

##### Army of the Dead

The living dead form most of The Barrow King's forces, including Ghasts, [Kackles](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/undead/kackle-crookedmoon24.md), Mummies, [Night Creatures](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/fiend/night-creature-crookedmoon24.md), [Phantom Hearses](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/undead/phantom-hearse-crookedmoon24.md), [Shrouded](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/undead/shrouded-crookedmoon24.md), Specters, Vampire Spawn, Wights, and Wraiths.

##### Horde of Domination

Monstrous creatures of the night also serve The Barrow King, taking the form of [Bat Hounds](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/monstrosity/bat-hound-crookedmoon24.md), [Boneflayers](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/monstrosity/boneflayer-crookedmoon24.md), Death Dogs, [Fleshdobbins](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/monstrosity/fleshdobbin-crookedmoon24.md), Hell Hounds, [Necrolisks](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/dragon/necrolisk-crookedmoon24.md), Night Hags, Phase Spiders, [Rotweavers](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/fey/rotweaver-crookedmoon24.md) (Monstrosity), and Vrocks.

### Wrath of The Barrow King

As a plague tears through the valley and the sinister cult grows more influential, the people begin to resign themselves to a fate of undeath. The only way to destroy the monarch is by infiltrating his tomb, and knowledge of such a thing is nearly lost to Druskenvald. Only the dead hold such secrets.

Grim History. By exhuming graves and tombs throughout Maidenmist Cemetery, characters can discover how to breach the royal barrow with a successful group DC 19 Intelligence (Arcana or History) check.

#### Ghostly Communion

Spirits of the tortured dead still linger, some not entirely under the dominion of The Barrow King. A successful group DC 19 Charisma (Persuasion) check when communing with such a spirit convinces a long-dead ghost to reveal their secrets.

#### Kill the King

Entering the royal tomb, the characters find that the stone has given way, and a network of catacombs and crypts winds deep into the earth, surging with the living dead. In the bowels of this subterranean fortress, [The Barrow King](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/npc/the-barrow-king-crookedmoon24.md) awaits the interlopers on a grand throne of skulls. He rises to meet the battle head-on, fighting with overwhelming necromantic power and grave honor.

##### End of an Empire

When The Barrow King is defeated, his army of the dead collapses into piles of desiccated flesh and bone. The royal barrow and the catacombs beneath it collapse, and the spread of death comes to an end. The people of Wickermoor Village recover from the plague and turn to the Old Ways, performing numerous strange rituals to prevent the coming doom. The Crooked Moon looks northward to the Drowned Crossroads.

##### Level Up

The characters reach level 18, if they are still level 17, when they destroy The Barrow King.

##### Fabled Heirloom

The crumbled remains of the monarch wear the [Crown of The Barrow King](/3-Mechanics/CLI/items/crown-of-the-barrow-king-crookedmoon24.md).

## The Jinxed Leviathan

Long ago, a simple alligator was captured by a cabal of jinxcraft-practicing magicians that poured hexes into it, creating an enormous swamp beast fused with bad luck itself, all in hopes of defeating the powerful death spirit known as Snake Eye Jack. However, this misfortune fell upon [The Jinxed Leviathan's](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/npc/the-jinxed-leviathan-crookedmoon24.md) captors when it broke free from their spell and devoured every last one of them, disappearing into the darkest tangled reaches of the Drowned Crossroads. Now, with the gaze of the Crooked Moon upon it, this reptilian colossus has risen to the surface once more, bringing misfortune and death to all those in its path.

### Get Jinxed

A terrible hex afflicts the Drowned Crossroads, bringing nothing but misfortune to its people. Horrible accidents, sudden sicknesses, and shocking disappearances occur by the hour, and even the simplest tasks can bring about a gruesome death. A resident of Murkwater Bend approaches the party, claiming that things are far worse than the jinxcraft of Mister Crossroads and begging for salvation before the swamp community's luck runs out.

#### Mire of Mishaps

With the terrible jinx upon the Drowned Crossroads, every inch of the swamp is treacherous, with the bayou itself animated and twisted into a strange, glowing mockery of what it once was.

##### The Worst Luck

A jinx afflicts the swamp. When a character makes a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">D20 Test</span>, it must subtract `dice:1d4|noform|noparens|avg` (`1d4`) from the total.

##### Swamplights

Eerie, jinxed hues radiate from patches of water, plants, and mud throughout the swamp. For every hour spent in the bayou, the characters must succeed on a DC 19 Wisdom saving throw or be affected by the Confusion spell for 10 minutes. If one or more characters fail the save, a [Bayou Beast](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/elemental/bayou-beast-crookedmoon24.md) rises from the swamp and attacks.

#### Poor Unfortunate Souls

The people of the Drowned Crossroads react to their turmoil with two different extremes: some party and live life to the fullest because they know their luck will soon run out, while others desperately try to appease the beast by any means necessary.

##### Last Dance

Many of the locals of the Drowned Crossroads are celebrating the last party before the apocalypse. They will use jinxcraft to convince others to party with them. Any character who starts their turn within 15 feet of one of these locals must succeed on a DC 19 Charisma saving throw or have the Charmed condition for 24 hours. While Charmed in this way, the character parties without rest and gains 1 Exhaustion level at the end of that time. Characters who fail can repeat this saving throw every hour, and characters who succeed are immune for 24 hours.

##### Appeasing Meal

Many panicked swampfolk wish to calm the leviathan with sacrificial meals. Groups wander the swamps looking for victims. Every 8 hours spent in the Drowned Crossroads, the characters must succeed on a group DC 19 Dexterity (Stealth) check or be found and attacked by 20 Bandit Captains seeking a new offering.

#### Swamp Things

The bayou has come to life with malevolent monsters that search for new meals for [The Jinxed Leviathan](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/npc/the-jinxed-leviathan-crookedmoon24.md), from hexed flora and fauna to the living dead and thralls risen from the unstable jinxcraft.

##### Bog Beasts

The swamp itself rises up at the command of The Jinxed Leviathan, including [Bayou Beasts](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/elemental/bayou-beast-crookedmoon24.md), [Dream Eaters](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/monstrosity/dream-eater-crookedmoon24.md), Giant Constrictor Snakes, Giant Crocodiles, Giant Toads, [Gloomwoods](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/plant/gloomwood-crookedmoon24.md), [Gobblegeists](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/monstrosity/gobblegeist-crookedmoon24.md), Shambling Mounds, Spirit Nagas, Water Elementals, and whippoorwill flocks (use [Crowstorm](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/fiend/crowstorm-crookedmoon24.md) stat block).

##### Jinxed Servants

Risen dead and bewitched thralls serve the beast, taking the form of Barbed Devils, [Deepdrecks](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/monstrosity/deepdreck-crookedmoon24.md), [Ferrymen](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/undead/ferryman-crookedmoon24.md), [Festerhulks](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/undead/festerhulk-crookedmoon24.md), Horned Devils, [Kackles](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/undead/kackle-crookedmoon24.md), [Lake Dredgers](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/undead/lake-dredger-crookedmoon24.md), Trolls, Ogre Zombies, [Wayward Haints](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/undead/wayward-haint-crookedmoon24.md), [Wildpyres](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/undead/wildpyre-crookedmoon24.md), and Will-o'-Wisps.

### The Leviathan Smiles

Every night that goes by sees more folk succumb to the worsening misfortune, and the survivors increasingly resign themselves to hedonism as the raging revelry reaches a fever pitch. Only by baiting the swamp beast or finding its cursed bayou can the characters slay it and finally end the madness.

#### Bait the Beast

Although [The Jinxed Leviathan](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/npc/the-jinxed-leviathan-crookedmoon24.md) will eat most living things, its favorite meal is a giant serpent sacred to the followers of Snake Eye Jack. One of these creatures can be found and used to bait the giant swamp monster into a trap with a successful group DC 19 Wisdom (Animal Handling or Survival) check.

#### Accursed Murk

Although the story has been lost for generations, some survivors familiar with the folklore of the swamp tell the story of the cabal that sought to depose Snake Eye Jack. The location where The Jinxed Leviathan was first transformed can be learned with a successful group DC 19 Charisma (Intimidation or Persuasion) check.

#### The Monster of Misfortune

No matter how the characters approach the behemoth, [The Jinxed Leviathan](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/npc/the-jinxed-leviathan-crookedmoon24.md) erupts from the depths of the swamp, turning the black water of the bayou into raging waves. Glowing jinxcraft glyphs appear on every surface around it as its eternal hex spreads far and wide. Its jaws gnash and its tail thrashes in an effort to turn the characters into its finest meal yet.

##### Turn of Luck

When The Jinxed Leviathan is slain, it lets out a bellow and collapses into the murk. Immediately, the swamp grows dark and quiet as the powerful jinx that has cursed the Drowned Crossroads is broken and lifted. With the misfortune finally banished from the bayou, the people of Murkwater Bend and other settlements celebrate even more wildly than before, hoping to go out with a bang. The Crooked Moon turns its gaze southward to the Webwoods.

##### Level Up

The characters reach level 19, if they are still level 18, when they destroy The Jinxed Leviathan.

##### Fabled Heirloom

The [Tooth of The Jinxed Leviathan](/3-Mechanics/CLI/items/tooth-of-the-jinxed-leviathan-crookedmoon24.md) can be pulled from the maw of the slain beast.

## The Dusk Mother

Once a human seamstress who practiced witchcraft, the mournful shade known only as [The Dusk Mother](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/npc/the-dusk-mother-crookedmoon24.md) was driven to inconsolable sorrow after she slew her children in a feverish ritual while possessed by a demonic entity she let into her home. In death, she has lingered in Wickermoor Hollow as a spirit clad in white, filled with immeasurable grief and hate. With the gaze of the Crooked Moon upon the Webwoods, a remote forest south of Wickermoor Village, her tether to the world of the living is at its strongest, and she seeks to ensure that all the valley shares in her misery.

### The Threads that Bind

There is a ghostly witch in the Webwoods, and the humble farming communities near the forest's edge are under her spell. The crops wither and die, the livestock turn up mutilated, the children disappear, and adults awaken with their eyes and mouths sewn shut—catatonic husks robbed of their souls. A teenage girl from a homestead, barely escaping the wrath of her parents after her siblings went missing, finds the characters and begs them to bring salvation to the forest's edge.

#### The Webs They Weave

The Dusk Mother's hut lies in the Webwoods, a forest normally overrun with enormous spiders but is now a network of webbing filled with haunted, spectral energies and agonized crying.

##### Tangled Webs

The Webwoods are choked with webs that seem to have a mind of their own. For every mile traveled through the wood, the characters must succeed on a DC 20 Dexterity saving throw or have the Restrained condition (escape DC 18) and the Poisoned condition for 8 hours. Additionally, each character who fails is immediately attacked by eight Ettercaps.

##### Ghostly Wails

Mournful wails of The Dusk Mother can be heard all around. For every hour spent within 5 miles of the tree line of the Webwoods, the characters must succeed on a DC 20 Wisdom saving throw or have the Incapacitated condition for 10 minutes as they break down into tears.

#### Come Little Children

The communities and homesteads near the Webwoods are nearly abandoned. The children have disappeared into the woods, and the adults that remain are bewitched and succumb to fits of convulsion and catatonic spells, barely responding to external stimuli.

##### Severed Soul

The eyes and mouths of the adults who remain are sewn shut. They have no souls, and their bodies seek new ones to inhabit them. Any character who cuts the threads and opens someone's eyes and mouth must succeed on a DC 20 Charisma saving throw or have their soul trapped within the Commoner's body as if by the Magic Jar spell for 8 hours.

##### Babes in the Wood

Numerous children have been bewitched and lured into the woods by The Dusk Mother, crying and whimpering for their parents or siblings. When a character moves within 5 feet of a child, the child's face turns wicked and they scream. Any character within 15 feet of the screaming child must succeed on a DC 20 Wisdom saving throw or have the Charmed condition from The Dusk Mother as if by the Dominate Monster spell for 1 hour.

#### Puppets on Strings

The occult magic of The Dusk Mother is immensely powerful, utilizing ancient witchcraft to master soul weaving. This has allowed her to bind spirits and other monsters of the valley to her mournful and malicious will.

##### Here Be Haunts

Otherworldly entities conjured and dominated by [The Dusk Mother](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/npc/the-dusk-mother-crookedmoon24.md) take the form of [Corvodaemons](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/fiend/corvodaemon-crookedmoon24.md), Doppelgangers, Erinyes, Ghosts, Green Hags, Incubi, Invisible Stalkers, [Ketgrinn](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/fey/ketgrinn-crookedmoon24.md), Mariliths, Night Hags, Oni, [Phantom Hearses](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/undead/phantom-hearse-crookedmoon24.md), [Pumpkin Heads](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/fiend/pumpkin-head-crookedmoon24.md), [Revelers of Song](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/fiend/reveler-of-song-crookedmoon24.md), [Revelers of Stories](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/fiend/reveler-of-stories-crookedmoon24.md), [Shrouded](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/undead/shrouded-crookedmoon24.md), Succubi, and Wraiths.

##### Dolled Up

Puppeted monsters and strange dolls crafted by The Dusk Mother and given life stalk the woods, taking the form of [Alpengrendel](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/giant/alpengrendel-crookedmoon24.md), Clay Golems, [Drakkenhobs](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/dragon/drakkenhob-crookedmoon24.md), Driders, Ettercaps, [Fleshdobbins](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/monstrosity/fleshdobbin-crookedmoon24.md), Flesh Golems, [Gobblegeists](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/monstrosity/gobblegeist-crookedmoon24.md), Harpies, [Houses of Horror](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/construct/house-of-horror-crookedmoon24.md), [Hypnovulfen](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/monstrosity/hypnovulfen-crookedmoon24.md), [Lurkers in the Dark](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/fey/lurker-in-the-dark-crookedmoon24.md), [Maidenwoods](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/plant/maidenwood-crookedmoon24.md), Owlbears, Phase Spiders, and Werewolves.

### Wicked Woman of the Woods

The people near the Webwoods have nearly been wiped out, and the children become more lost in the woods with every night that passes. The increasing quiet is more unsettling than the sniffling cries. Only by finding The Dusk Mother's hut, to which her spirit is bound, can the characters purge her spirit and cleanse the land of her sorrow and hate.

#### Nursery Rhymes

Although the missing children are bewitched by The Dusk Mother, they have seen her hut. When the characters encounter a child who sings haunting rhymes about the shade in white, they can piece together the location of the hut with a successful group DC 20 Intelligence (Arcana or History) check.

The arachnids of the Webwoods serve The Dusk Mother and view her as their spider queen. Characters who observe the skittering patterns of these local beasts and succeed on a group DC 20 Wisdom (Animal Handling or Survival) check can follow them to the spirit's haunted hut.

[The Dusk Mother](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/npc/the-dusk-mother-crookedmoon24.md) materializes with a mournful wail and the tolling of her bell, seeking to sever the souls of the characters and make them her puppets in death.

##### Put to Bed

With the destruction of The Dusk Mother, the shade is finally freed from her lingering torment and fades with a wail. Many of the missing children wander out of the forest and find their parents and older siblings, all of whom have their souls restored. ##### Level Up

The [Bell of The Dusk Mother](/3-Mechanics/CLI/items/bell-of-the-dusk-mother-crookedmoon24.md) is left behind once the spirit is banished from Druskenvald.

## The Brimstone Behemoth

Throughout countless worlds, realms, and planes of existence, there is a single shared tale told across every culture. In every community, a legend is born from the universal fear of the end of all things. This collective belief manifested a Fiend of titanic proportions made from hellfire and dying earth that possesses a malevolent will and a single desire: armageddon. As the Crooked Moon makes its final descent, its baleful gaze calls down its first sacrifice the maiden once known as Rowan, who has now ushered in [The Brimstone Behemoth](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/npc/the-brimstone-behemoth-crookedmoon24.md) to Druskenvald.

### The End of All Hope

Kehlenn's sneering moon is low in the sky, with surging primordial energies trailing its incessant descent. A rumbling felt throughout Wickermoor Hollow serves as a prelude to an enormous eruption from Rowan's Rise, destroying the hill and turning the surrounding moors into a volcanic hellscape that stinks of brimstone. Screaming Fiends rise from rivers of lava and plumes of hellfire and take to the skies, heralding in the being that will end Druskenvald. All peoples across the valley can see the blaze and turn to despair. None are safe, and all shall perish underneath the Crooked Moon.

#### Hell on Earth

The entirety of Wickermoor Hollow is the domain of The Brimstone Behemoth. The scorching of the valley is the first wound that will bring about the demise of Druskenvald. Hellish volcanic eruptions send fiery death across the valley, and the people within twist and transform into Fiends that join the legions of the apocalypse.

##### The Sky is Falling

Boulders of lava and hellfire rain down across the valley. Every hour, there is a 50 percent chance that they crash toward the characters, who must each make a DC 20 Dexterity saving throw against the effects of the Meteor Swarm spell.

##### Mark of the Beast

The corruption of armageddon afflicts the valley. Every hour, the characters must succeed on a DC 20 Charisma saving throw or gain one Mark of the Apocalypse. A character who gains three marks permanently transforms into a Fiend under the command of The Brimstone Behemoth. To determine which, roll `dice:1d2|noform|noparens|avg` (`1d2`). On a 1, they transform into a Balor. On a 2, they transform into a Pit Fiend. A Remove Curse spell removes one or two accumulated marks.

#### Lambs to the Slaughter

People from all over Wickermoor Hollow flee their homes and seek shelter and protection from others, praying, begging, and pleading for salvation from the end of all things.

##### Prayer for the Dying

Groups of faithful gather and pray to the Green Queen, the Patient Lady, the Brightsinger, and any other god who will listen. The characters can stay and spend an hour praying to make a group DC 20 Intelligence (Religion), Wisdom (Insight), or Charisma (Performance) check. On a success, the characters collectively gain one use of the Cleric's Divine Intervention feature and must unanimously decide how it is used.

##### Wolves in Sheep's Clothing

Secret servants of The Brimstone Behemoth operate a doomsday cult. If the characters step within 5 feet of a cultist, the cultist explodes into hellfire and brimstone. Any character within 20 feet of the sacrificed cultist must succeed on a DC 20 Dexterity saving throw or take `dice:3d6|noform|noparens|avg|text(10)` (`3d6`) Fire damage plus `dice:3d6|noform|noparens|avg|text(10)` (`3d6`) Necrotic damage and gain 1 Exhaustion level as their bodies and souls are seared.

#### The Burning Legion

As [The Brimstone Behemoth](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/npc/the-brimstone-behemoth-crookedmoon24.md) rises, it is joined by an army of Fiends, fallen angels, and otherworldly beings that share in the goal of bringing apocalypse to as many worlds as possible.

##### Seventh Seal

Malicious Fiends of all varieties erupt from the shattered, molten earth, and corrupted angels fall from burning skies, taking the form of Barbed Devils, Bearded Devils, Bone Devils, [Crowstorms](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/fiend/crowstorm-crookedmoon24.md), Devas (Fiend), Glabrezu, Gibbering Mouthers (Fiend), Hell Hounds, [Heralds of Fools](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/fiend/herald-of-fools-crookedmoon24.md), Hezrou, Horned Devils, Manticores (Fiend), Nalfeshnees, Nightmares, Planetars (Fiend), Rakshasas, and Vrocks.

##### Inferno

Beings of hellfire and brimstone join the infinite legions, taking the form of Adult Brass Dragons (Fiend), Adult Red Dragons (Fiend), Chimeras (Fiend), Earth Elementals (Fiend), Efreeti (Fiend), Fire Elementals (Fiend), Fire Giants (Fiend), Gargoyles (Fiend), Salamanders (Fiend), [Soulfire Stokers](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/elemental/soulfire-stoker-crookedmoon24.md) (Fiend), [Wildpyres](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/undead/wildpyre-crookedmoon24.md) (Fiend), and Xorn (Fiend).

### Revelations

Although the creature of the apocalypse is bringing doom to Wickermoor Hollow, its physical form does not manifest unless commanded by its summoner, Rowan. Otherwise, it is content to suffuse the land with its power of the end of things. The only way to face, battle, and kill the beast is to confront the spirit of Rowan atop the volcanic mound and draw her ire. Only then will she lose her sense and draw the beast forward to attempt to destroy the characters. However, it requires particular information to provoke the spirit enough to initiate the final battle.

#### Final Folklore

The last hope of Wickermoor Hollow may be the local legends kept alive by those who adhere to the Old Ways. By seeking these stories from those knowledgeable of the valley's lore and succeeding on a group DC 20 Charisma (Persuasion or Intimidation) check, the characters can learn the truth of Rowan. She was a malevolent maiden who sought apocalypse, and her fiendish dabbling made her the prime candidate for Kehlenn's first sacrifice. By appealing to the innocent maiden she once was, the characters who condemn her for her wickedness and how much she had fallen will turn the spirit to rage.

#### Family Ties

The only person Rowan cared for was her sister, whose descendant still lives in Wickermoor Village. By scouring any records for Rowan or her family and succeeding on a group DC 20 Intelligence (Arcana or History) check, the characters discover which townsfolk is her sister's descendant and use this knowledge against her. By appealing to the love of her family she once had, the specter's conflicted feelings will boil into a vengeful wrath.

#### The Number of the Beast

When the characters ascend the ruins of Rowan's Rise and face the shimmering maiden at the center of the bursting brimstone, her wrath is as fiery as the doom she has brought to the valley. With a cry to the moon above and the hellfire below, she heralds the apocalypse. [The Brimstone Behemoth](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/npc/the-brimstone-behemoth-crookedmoon24.md) erupts from the sulfuric lava with an earth-shattering bellow and seeks to annihilate the characters and Druskenvald along with them.

##### To the Farrows and Fields

When The Brimstone Behemoth is destroyed, it explodes in a catastrophic burst of stinking lava that pours down upon Wickermoor Hollow. The lava instantly cools and hardens, forming countless scars upon the landscape. In time, they will provide nutrient-rich soil for a new age of fertility and regrowth.

Rowan's spirit calms, and she has a final moment of clarity, apologizing to the characters for everything she brought to pass before fading forever. With no champions remaining, the Crooked Moon gazes upon the characters themselves.

##### Ability Score Improvement

When the characters destroy The Brimstone Behemoth, they increase one of their ability scores by 4 or two of their ability scores by 2.

##### Fabled Heirloom

The [Hide of The Brimstone Behemoth](/3-Mechanics/CLI/items/hide-of-the-brimstone-behemoth-crookedmoon24.md) remains in the hardening lava after the colossus explodes.

## Conclusion: One Last Choice

With the Crooked Moon only several hundred feet above Wickermoor Hollow, the air is electrified as howling gales rip through the valley. Gravity itself has destabilized. The sneering moon stares directly at the characters and speaks to them in Kehlenn's voice. She offers them one last chance to annihilate Druskenvald and remake it in their image.

The Crooked Queen knows she has been defeated, her final gambit failed and spent. Her last chance for revenge is to convince the characters to accept the power of remaking Druskenvald, which would obliterate all those within and allow them to create a new realm. She is too proud to beg or plead, but appeals to their deepest desires, just as her destroyed consort, The Horned King, once did.

If the offer is accepted, the Crooked Moon crashes into the land, and all of Druskenvald, as well as every soul within it besides the characters, is destroyed. Left alone, the characters swim in a green sea of raw rebirthing energies and may unanimously decide the nature of their new realm. If the offer is refused, the moon returns to the sky, and the face in the crescent disappears as Kehlenn fades from existence, the last of her primordial power expended. Druskenvald is finally safe, and the moon is no longer crooked.
