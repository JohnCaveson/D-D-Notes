# Shadow Drain
*3rd-level, Necromancy*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Self (15-foot Emanation)
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

You siphon away the vitality and sense of self through the shadows of nearby creatures in a 15-foot <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Emanation</span> originating from you.

When you cast this spell, you can designate creatures to be unaffected by it. Whenever any other creature enters the <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Emanation</span> or starts its turn there, or when the <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Emanation</span> enters a creature's space, the creature must make a Charisma saving throw. On a failed save, the creature takes `dice:2d6|noform|noparens|avg` (`2d6`) Necrotic damage and has <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Disadvantage</span> on attack rolls and ability checks until the end of its next turn. On a successful save, the creature takes half as much damage only. A creature makes this save only once per turn.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** The damage increases by `dice:1d6|noform|noparens|avg` (`1d6`) for each spell slot level above 3.
