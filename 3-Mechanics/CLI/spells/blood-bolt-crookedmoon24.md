# Blood Bolt
*cantrip, Necromancy*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 90 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You hurl a twisting bolt of blood at a creature within range. Make a ranged spell attack against the target. On a hit, the target takes `dice:1d6|noform|noparens|avg` (`1d6`) Necrotic damage, and you gain a number of <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Temporary Hit Points</span> equal to your <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Proficiency Bonus</span>.

**Cantrip Upgrade.** The damage increases by `dice:1d6|noform|noparens|avg` (`1d6`) when you reach levels 5 (`dice:2d6|noform|noparens|avg` (`2d6`)), 11 (`dice:3d6|noform|noparens|avg` (`3d6`)), and 17 (`dice:4d6|noform|noparens|avg` (`4d6`)).
