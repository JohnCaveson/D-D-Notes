# Staff of Farryn of the Greenwood
*Staff, weapon (requires attunement)*  
![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheGiddyLimit/homebrew-img/refs/heads/main/img/CrookedMoon24/Items/Staff%20of%20Farryn%20of%20the%20Greenwood.webp#right)

- **Properties**: [Fabled Heirloom](/3-Mechanics/CLI/item-properties.md#Fabled%20Heirloom)
- **Weight**: 4.0 lbs.

This twisted wooden staff is crowned by a trinket-adorned deer's skull. It was fashioned by Farryn before she delved into the dark lore of Gorthos. Soft, fragrant moss and earthy mushrooms grow along the staff's length.

While holding the staff, you can use it as a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Spellcasting Focus</span> for your spells, you gain a bonus to spell attack rolls and your spell save DC determined by the staff's rarity.
