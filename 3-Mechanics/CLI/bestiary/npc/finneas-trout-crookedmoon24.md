# [Finneas Trout](3-Mechanics\CLI\bestiary\npc/finneas-trout-crookedmoon24.md)
*Source: The Crooked Moon (2024) p. 200*  

Finneas Trout enthusiastically teaches the children of the area their letters and numbers, or other more esoteric subjects for the children whose parents value such things. A bookish young man, he wears spectacles under a mop of unkempt brown hair, and his physical features are unimposing. Finneas has a reverence of "book learning" that is seen as admirable to some and silly to others.

He came to Wickermoor Village recently from the city of Baskerton (see chapter 1), and he keeps a room at the Green Queen Inn, paid for by the town and the parents of his more affluent students, who receive additional tutoring.

Finneas falls into the camp of the Newcomers, hoping that the pure light of reason can drive away the darkness of superstition. He has also found a source of brightness in his romantic affection for the chandler, [Oswick Frey](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/npc/oswick-frey-crookedmoon24.md) (see area V17).
