# Staff of Farryn of the Greenwood (Legendary)
*Staff, weapon, legendary (requires attunement)*  
![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheGiddyLimit/homebrew-img/refs/heads/main/img/CrookedMoon24/Items/Staff%20of%20Farryn%20of%20the%20Greenwood.webp#right)

- **Damage**:
  - One-handed: 1d6 bludgeoning
  - Two-handed: 1d8 bludgeoning
- **Properties**: [Fabled Heirloom](/3-Mechanics/CLI/item-properties.md#Fabled%20Heirloom), [Versatile](/3-Mechanics/CLI/item-properties.md#Versatile)
- **Weight**: 4.0 lbs.

This twisted wooden staff is crowned by a trinket-adorned deer's skull. It was fashioned by Farryn before she delved into the dark lore of Gorthos. Soft, fragrant moss and earthy mushrooms grow along the staff's length.

While holding the staff, you can use it as a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Spellcasting Focus</span> for your spells, you gain a +3 bonus to spell attack rolls and your spell save DC determined by the staff's rarity, and you can use the following properties.

## Living Ward (Rare+)

You have <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Resistance</span> to Necrotic damage.

## Servant of the Cycle (Rare+)

You can cast Cure Wounds (level 3 version) from the staff. When you do so, the next time you hit with an attack before the end of your next turn, the target takes an extra `dice:1d10|noform|noparens|avg` (`1d10`) Necrotic damage. This property can be used three times, regaining all expended uses daily at dusk.

## Cycle's Balance (Very Rare+)

You can cast Revivify from the staff. When you do so, the next time a creature fails a saving throw against a spell you cast in the next hour, it and creatures of your choice within 20 feet of it take `dice:4d10|noform|noparens|avg` (`4d10`) Necrotic damage. Once this property is used, it can't be used again until the next dusk.

## Sacrifice of Flesh (Very Rare+)

When a spell you cast restores <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Hit Points</span>, you can take Necrotic damage up to twice the level of the spell that can't be reduced or prevented in any way. If you do so, the target regains additional <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Hit Points</span> equal to three times the damage you take.

## Death Blossom (Legendary)

When a creature you can see within 60 feet of you dies, you can take a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Reaction</span> to make flowers bloom around them. Creatures of your choice within 30 feet of the dead creature regain `dice:4d8|noform|noparens|avg` (`4d8`) <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Hit Points</span> and gain `dice:2d8|noform|noparens|avg` (`2d8`) <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Temporary Hit Points</span>. Once this property is used, it can't be used again until the next dusk.

*Source: The Crooked Moon (2024) p. 605*
