# The Barrow King

## Regional Effects
_Source: The Crooked Moon (2024)_

The region containing the Barrow King's lair is changed by his presence, creating the following effects:

- **Empire of Desecration.** A Hallow spell cast within 1 mile of the lair fails, but any resources used to cast it aren't wasted.  
- **Necrotic Shroud.** When a living creature within 1 mile of the lair regains <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Hit Points</span> as part of finishing a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Long Rest</span>, it can only regain up to half its <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Hit Point</span> maximum.  

If the Barrow King dies or moves his lair elsewhere, these effects end immediately.
