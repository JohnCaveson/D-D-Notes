# Skin of Coffin Nails
*Dark Bargain*  

Your body becomes as coldly unrelenting as iron nails sealing away the dead. You can shrug off staggering punishment, but the waters of the world no longer embrace you, as if spiting your defiance.

## Iron Grit

You gain <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Resistance</span> to Bludgeoning, Piercing, or Slashing damage (your choice when you select this Dark Bargain). You can change the damage type to one of the other options when you finish a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Short</span> or <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Long Rest</span>. When you suffer a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Critical Hit</span> from the chosen damage type, it becomes a normal hit instead.

## Watery Doom

When you are immersed in water or splashed with at least 1 gallon of water (such as from a bucket or being rained on), your skin tightens and grows rusty until you finish a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Short</span> or <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Long Rest</span>. During that time, your <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Speed</span> and the time you can hold your breath are halved, and your <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Resistance</span> to the chosen damage type for Iron Grit becomes <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Vulnerability</span> instead. At the GM's discretion, other liquids can also trigger this feature.

*Source: The Crooked Moon (2024) p. 120*
