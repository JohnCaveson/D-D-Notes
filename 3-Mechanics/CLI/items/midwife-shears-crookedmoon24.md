# Midwife Shears
*Wondrous item, rare (requires attunement)*  
![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheGiddyLimit/homebrew-img/refs/heads/main/img/CrookedMoon24/Items/Midwife%20Shears.webp#right)

- **Properties**: [Cursed Curio](/3-Mechanics/CLI/item-properties.md#Cursed%20Curio)

These scissors are shaped to resemble a stork. Their blades are forever wickedly sharp, and the threads they cut are more than merely physical.

## Cut the Unseen Thread

While holding the scissors, you can cast Dispel Magic (+4 bonus) or Remove Curse from them. Once either spell is cast from the scissors, that spell can't be cast from them again until the next dusk.

## Curse of the Severed Thread

When you make a saving throw to avoid being pushed or to avoid the Prone condition, you have a -1 penalty to the roll. This penalty increases by 1 each time you use the Cut the Unseen Thread property. If you fail such a save by 5 or more, you are pushed `1d6 x 5` feet straight away from the source of the save, in addition to any other distance pushed.

*Source: The Crooked Moon (2024) p. 611*
