### Haint - The Songstress

![[NPC_The_Songstress.png]]
- sitting in one of the velvet chairs is a [[Wayward Haint]] of a beautiful woman in a long, silky dress, smoking a cigarette while glumly looking out the window. 
- Her whole being seems hollow. She only knows herself as a songstress who wished to be a famous singer in life
- feeling empty having never been able to find the perfect song that inspired her to give a performance of a lifetime

```ad-tip
title: Resolutions
- Bringing her the phonograph from [[G2 - Sleeper Car]] and turning it on inspires her to sing the perfect final song.
	- She stands at the center of the lounge and croons a love song that spurs Phillip and Adela to embrace and slowly dance to the performance.
--- 
**All checks made against a resolution start at DC 12 and increases by 2 per 
failure**

After 3 success the Haint is free
After 3 failures the Haint becomes hostile and attacks.
	  
- **Musical Instrument** or **Performance** : Join the Songstress in a duet for an emotional swan song.
- **Arcana** or **Insight** : Consider cosmic or philosophical topics that speak to inspiration and creativity
- **Investigation** or **History** : Explore examples of artistic work that help inspire the Songstress
```
