# Harvest Moonglow
*3rd-level, Evocation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (a piece of moonstone)
- **Duration:** 1 hour

You call upon the light of the harvest moon to illuminate a 20-foot-radius <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Sphere</span> centered on a point within range. The area is bathed in silvery <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Dim Light</span>. Creatures and objects with the Invisible condition in the light become visible as translucent, shimmering images. When one of your allies is within the light, it can use a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Bonus Action</span> to absorb some of the light and gain `dice:2d6|noform|noparens|avg` (`2d6`) <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Temporary Hit Points</span>.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** The <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Temporary Hit Points</span> increase by `dice:1d6|noform|noparens|avg` (`1d6`) for each spell slot level above 3.
