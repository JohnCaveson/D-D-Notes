# Dissolution
*cantrip, Transmutation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You touch a creature or nonmagical object and break the target's substance down into its most fundamental components. Make a melee spell attack. On a hit, the target takes `dice:1d8|noform|noparens|avg` (`1d8`) Force damage, and its AC is reduced by 1 against the next attack roll made against it before the end of your next turn.

**Cantrip Upgrade.** The damage increases by `dice:1d8|noform|noparens|avg` (`1d8`) when you reach levels 5 (`dice:2d8|noform|noparens|avg` (`2d8`)), 11 (`dice:3d8|noform|noparens|avg` (`3d8`)), and 17 (`dice:4d8|noform|noparens|avg` (`4d8`)).
