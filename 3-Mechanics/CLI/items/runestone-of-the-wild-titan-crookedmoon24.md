# Runestone of the Wild Titan
*Wondrous item (requires attunement)*  
![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheGiddyLimit/homebrew-img/refs/heads/main/img/CrookedMoon24/Items/Runestone%20of%20the%20Wild%20Titan.webp#right)

- **Properties**: [Fabled Heirloom](/3-Mechanics/CLI/item-properties.md#Fabled%20Heirloom)
- **Weight**: 50.0 lbs.

This mighty granite runestone is etched with complex glyphs on much of its angular surface. Though an incredible burden to carry, it thrums with the power of the wild titan. The stone weighs 50 pounds.

While holding the stone, you gain a bonus to Strength and Constitution saving throws determined by the runestone's rarity.
