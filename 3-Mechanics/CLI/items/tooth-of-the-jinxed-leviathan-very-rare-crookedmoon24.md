# Tooth of the Jinxed Leviathan (Very Rare)
*Weapon (dagger), very rare (requires attunement)*  
![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheGiddyLimit/homebrew-img/refs/heads/main/img/CrookedMoon24/Items/Tooth%20of%20the%20Jinxed%20Leviathan.webp#right)

- **Damage**: 1d4 piercing
- **Range**: 20/60
- **Properties**: [Fabled Heirloom](/3-Mechanics/CLI/item-properties.md#Fabled%20Heirloom), [Finesse](/3-Mechanics/CLI/item-properties.md#Finesse), [Light](/3-Mechanics/CLI/item-properties.md#Light), [Thrown](/3-Mechanics/CLI/item-properties.md#Thrown)
- **Weight**: 1.0 lbs.

This tooth is razor sharp and can be wielded as a Dagger. Its "hilt" is wrapped in alligator hide, and the flat of its blade flickers with magenta hex magic in carved glyphs.

You gain a +2 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon determined by its rarity. While holding the tooth, you can use it as a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Spellcasting Focus</span> for your spells, and you can use the following properties.

## Jinx of Dire Fortune (Rare+)

As a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Bonus Action</span>, you can force one creature you can see within 60 feet of you to make a Charisma saving throw (DC = 16 + the tooth's bonus). On a failed save, the target is jinxed for 1 minute. While jinxed, when the target makes a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">D20 Test</span> and rolls an even number on the `dice:d20|noform|noparens|avg` (`d20`), it must reroll the `dice:d20|noform|noparens|avg` (`d20`) and take the new roll. On a successful save, you must make the same saving throw, becoming jinxed for 1 minute on a failure. This property can't be used again while another creature is jinxed by it.

## Exposed Weak Spot (Very Rare+)

When you make an attack roll with the tooth, you score a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Critical Hit</span> on a roll of 19 or 20 on the `dice:d20|noform|noparens|avg` (`d20`).

## Steal Unluck (Very Rare+)

When an enemy you can see within 60 feet of you makes a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">D20 Test</span> with <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Advantage</span>, you can take a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Reaction</span> to negate their <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Advantage</span>. Once this property is used, it can't be used again until a creature you can see makes a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">D20 Test</span> and rolls a 1 on the `dice:d20|noform|noparens|avg` (`d20`), or until the next dusk.

*Source: The Crooked Moon (2024) p. 606*
