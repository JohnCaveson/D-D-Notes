# Cult of Personality
*Dark Bargain*  

You are a force of charm and allure with which to be reckoned. While others fall over themselves to stumble into your good graces, you might find that you end up needing them more than they need you.

## Honeyed Words

As a Magic action, you can speak sweet words to a creature within 60 feet of you. The creature has the Charmed condition for 24 hours, until you use this feature again, or until you or your allies deal any damage to the target or force it to make a saving throw. The Charmed target is your ally and follows your commands as long as they don't require the target to harm itself. If the target carries out a command it finds odious, it makes a DC 15 Wisdom saving throw, ending the condition on itself on a success. Once the condition ends, the target is immune to this feature for 24 hours.

If the target has <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Immunity</span> to the Charmed condition, it instead has <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Disadvantage</span> on <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">D20 Tests</span> until the start of your next turn, and you gain a number of <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Temporary Hit Points</span> equal to your level. Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Short</span> or <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Long Rest</span>.

## Solitary Weakness

If you start your turn with no allies that you can see or hear within 20 feet of you, you lose any <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Temporary Hit Points</span> you have from any source, and you have <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Disadvantage</span> on <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">D20 Tests</span> until the start of your next turn.

*Source: The Crooked Moon (2024) p. 119*
