### The Mariner

![[NPC_The_Mariner.png]]
- In a sailor’s coat with a clay pipe. Grumbling, he purposelessly heaves sacks of mail from one end of the car to the other as if he was loading cargo on a ship. There is a distant regretful look in his eyes as he goes about his labor.
- He regularly mentions that he wishes he could see the sea one last time with glum wistfulness.
