# The Wicker Man

## Regional Effects
_Source: The Crooked Moon (2024)_

The region containing the Wicker Man's lair is changed by its presence, creating the following effects:

- **Biting Flames.** Within 1 mile of the lair, when Fire damage is dealt to a creature outside the Wicker Man's dedicated cult that has <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Resistance</span> or <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Immunity</span> to Fire damage, the creature takes an extra `dice:1d6|noform|noparens|avg` (`1d6`) Necrotic damage.  
- **Endless Praise.** Within 1 mile of the lair, cultists of the Wicker Man who sing their cult's praises or hymns can be heard up to 300 feet away, even through a Silence spell.  

If the Wicker Man dies or moves its lair elsewhere, these effects end immediately.
