# [Jean Myriel](3-Mechanics\CLI\bestiary\npc/jean-myriel-crookedmoon24.md)
*Source: The Crooked Moon (2024) p. 204*  

Jean Myriel emigrated to Wickermoor Village from Dawn's Gate several years ago. The unique methods with metalwork and strange designs he developed in his apprenticeship were seen as too bizarre and unorthodox for the conservative community. Stealing away in the dead of night and heading east, he found a home in Wickermoor Village.

While he once kept his beard wild and untamed, he's recently made the decision to trim his facial hair to make himself more "presentable." His shorter frame belies a mass of muscles beneath.

He is sure of himself and his skills, but he also knows when to accept the advice of others. After leaving the faith of the Crimson Rose behind in Dawn's Gate (see chapter 14), he has remained non-religious and upholds the ideals of the Newcomers.
