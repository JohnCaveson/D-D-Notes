# Hungering Blade
*1st-level, Necromancy*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Bonus Action
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** 1 minute

You imbue a weapon you are holding, or your <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Unarmed Strikes</span>, with ravenous negative energy. For the duration, when you hit a creature with an attack using the empowered weapon or <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Unarmed Strike</span> for the first time on a turn, the target takes Necrotic damage equal to your spellcasting ability modifier, and you gain a number of <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Temporary Hit Points</span> equal to the Necrotic damage dealt.
