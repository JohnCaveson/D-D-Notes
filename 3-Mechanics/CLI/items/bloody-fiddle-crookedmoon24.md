# Bloody Fiddle
*Wondrous item, rare (requires attunement)*  
![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheGiddyLimit/homebrew-img/refs/heads/main/img/CrookedMoon24/Items/Bloody%20Fiddle.webp#right)

- **Properties**: [Cursed Curio](/3-Mechanics/CLI/item-properties.md#Cursed%20Curio)

This scarlet-lacquered fiddle is warm to the touch, like the heat of fresh blood. Though it looks like it has seen better days, its sound is clear and strong.

While you hold the fiddle, you gain a +2 bonus to Charisma (Performance) checks, and you can use the following properties.

## Song of Stirred Blood

As a Magic action, you can play a lively or stirring tune on the fiddle. Up to six creatures of your choice within 30 feet of you gain <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Temporary Hit Points</span> equal to `dice:1d10|noform|noparens|avg` (`1d10`) + your <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Proficiency Bonus</span>, and if you have Bardic Inspiration dice, you can expend and roll one to add it to the total. Once this property is used, it can't be used again until the next dusk.

## Sanguine Curse

The first time that you play the fiddle after finishing a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Long Rest</span>, you take 1 Slashing damage that ignores <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Resistance</span>. When you use the Song of Stirred Blood property, this Slashing damage increases by 1.

*Source: The Crooked Moon (2024) p. 609*
