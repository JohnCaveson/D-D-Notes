# Sanguine Secrets
*2nd-level, Divination*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Bonus Action
- **Range:** 30 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (a drop of blood, which the spell consumes)
- **Duration:** 1 round

You draw knowledge through the power of blood about a creature you can see within range. You learn the target's species and creature type, its current <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Hit Points</span>, and any conditions affecting it. You also learn if it has any of the following, and, if so, what they are: <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Vulnerability</span>, <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Resistance</span>, or <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Immunity</span> to damage  or conditions. Additionally, the next ability check or attack roll you make against the target before the end of your next turn has <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Advantage</span>.
