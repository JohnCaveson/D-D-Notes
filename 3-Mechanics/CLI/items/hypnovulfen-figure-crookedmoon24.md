# Hypnovulfen Figure
*Wondrous item, rare (requires attunement)*  
![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheGiddyLimit/homebrew-img/refs/heads/main/img/CrookedMoon24/Items/Hypnovulfen%20Figure.webp#right)

- **Properties**: [Cursed Curio](/3-Mechanics/CLI/item-properties.md#Cursed%20Curio)

This carved wooden figurine depicts a crooked wolf with large, leering eyes and a wild, toothy maw. While holding the figure, you can use it as a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Spellcasting Focus</span>, and you gain a +1 bonus to spell attack rolls and spell save DCs. The figure has the following properties.

## Hypnovulfen Bite

Your teeth become viciously sharp. You can bite as an <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Unarmed Strike</span> that deals `dice:1d6|noform|noparens|avg` (`1d6`) Piercing damage instead of Bludgeoning damage.

## Hypnovulfen Eye

While holding the figure, you can cast Confusion (save DC 14) with it. Once this property is used, it can't be used again until the next dusk.

## Cursed Hunger

When you roll <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Initiative</span>, you must succeed on a DC 0 Wisdom saving throw or you can't use the Attack or Magic actions except to use the figure's properties until you hit a creature with a Hypnovulfen Bite attack. When you use the Hypnovulfen Eye property, the DC of this saving throw increases by 2.

*Source: The Crooked Moon (2024) p. 610*
