# [Jeremiah Stover](3-Mechanics\CLI\bestiary\npc/jeremiah-stover-crookedmoon24.md)
*Source: The Crooked Moon (2024) p. 209*  

The farmers living around Wickermoor Village work long hours and perform hard labor to keep the population fed. They have established a rudimentary union to ensure that they receive fair compensation for their work. The representative they've chosen is Jeremiah Stover, a tuber farmer whose dedication to the Old Ways, the land, and its workers makes him the perfect candidate. He works equally hard in his fields as he does in advocating for his peers.
