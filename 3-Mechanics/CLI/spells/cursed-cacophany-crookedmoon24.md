# Cursed Cacophany
*3rd-level, Enchantment (ritual)*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action unless cast as a ritual
- **Range:** Special
- **Components:** S, M (a sheet of music)
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 10 minutes

You focus on a creature that you are familiar with that is on the same plane of existence as you. The target must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or be cursed for the duration. Once a target fails its saving throw against this spell, you can't target it with a ritual casting of the spell for 24 hours.

While cursed, the target hears haunting, discordant music and has <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Disadvantage</span> on <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">D20 Tests</span>. The first time the target fails a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">D20 Test</span> during a turn, it repeats the save against this spell with neither <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Advantage</span> nor <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Disadvantage</span>, ending the spell early on a success.
