# Idol of the Beast of Blight (Legendary)
*Wondrous item, legendary (requires attunement)*  
![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheGiddyLimit/homebrew-img/refs/heads/main/img/CrookedMoon24/Items/Idol%20of%20the%20Beast%20of%20Blight.webp#right)

- **Properties**: [Fabled Heirloom](/3-Mechanics/CLI/item-properties.md#Fabled%20Heirloom)

Made of bleached and mutated bone, this idol in the shape of a stag contains the sinister knowledge of the Beast of Blight. It pulses with the power of rot and decay.

While holding the idol, you can use it as a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Spellcasting Focus</span> for your spells, you gain a +3 bonus to spell attack rolls and your spell save DC determined by the idol's rarity, and you can use the following properties.

## Rotting Touch (Rare+)

You can use your Strength, Dexterity, or Wisdom modifier for the attack and damage rolls of your <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Unarmed Strikes</span>, and on a hit, your strike deals an extra `dice:1d6|noform|noparens|avg` (`1d6`) Necrotic damage (Rare), `dice:1d8|noform|noparens|avg` (`1d8`) Necrotic damage (Very Rare), or `dice:1d10|noform|noparens|avg` (`1d10`) Necrotic damage (Legendary).

## Touch of Oblivion (Rare+)

As a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Bonus Action</span>, you can touch a creature that has been dead for less than 1 hour. It rots away to bleached bone and can't be used for this property again. The next time you hit with an <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Unarmed Strike</span> using the Rotting Touch property before the end of your next turn, it is a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Critical Hit</span> instead.

## Hallucinatory Spores (Very Rare+)

When you cast a spell using a spell slot or from the idol, you can choose one creature that you can see within 10 feet of you. It must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw (DC = 16 + the idol's bonus) or take `dice:2d6|noform|noparens|avg` (`2d6`) Psychic damage and have <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Disadvantage</span> on attack rolls until the start of your next turn.

## Rot in the Flesh (Very Rare+)

You can cast Barkskin from the idol targeting only yourself. For the duration, a creature that hits you with a melee attack roll takes `dice:1d8|noform|noparens|avg` (`1d8`) Necrotic damage. Once the spell is cast from the idol, it can't be cast from it again until the next dusk.

## Hollow with Rot (Legendary)

When you regain <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Hit Points</span> from a spell, you also gain <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Temporary Hit Points</span> equal to the spell's level.

*Source: The Crooked Moon (2024) p. 600*
