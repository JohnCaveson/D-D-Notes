# Hide of the Brimstone Behemoth
*Wondrous item (requires attunement)*  
![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheGiddyLimit/homebrew-img/refs/heads/main/img/CrookedMoon24/Items/Hide%20of%20the%20Brimstone%20Behemoth.webp#right)

- **Properties**: [Fabled Heirloom](/3-Mechanics/CLI/item-properties.md#Fabled%20Heirloom)

This cloak was made from the hide of a reptilian magma Fiend, incomparable in its destructive power. Flame now shies away from it, for one who has mastered such a titanic creature can master its element.

While wearing this cloak, you gain a bonus to <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Armor Class</span> and Constitution saving throws determined by the cloak's rarity.
