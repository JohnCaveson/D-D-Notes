# Culling Sickle
*3rd-level, Necromancy*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Bonus Action
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

You create a ghostly sickle that lasts for the duration and can strike out at your foes. The sickle appears hovering in your space and moves with you. When you cast this spell and as a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Bonus Action</span> on each of your turns, you can direct it to fly at and strike a creature within 30 feet of you. Make a ranged spell attack. On a hit, the target takes `dice:2d8|noform|noparens|avg` (`2d8`) Necrotic damage, and you gain <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Temporary Hit Points</span> equal to half the damage dealt. After the sickle hits or misses, it returns to hover in your space.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** The damage increases by `dice:1d8|noform|noparens|avg` (`1d8`) for each spell slot level above 3.
