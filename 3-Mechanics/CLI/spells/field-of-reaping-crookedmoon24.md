# Field of Reaping
*5th-level, Necromancy*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 90 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (a dried corn husk or wheat hull)
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

You create a shadowy field that reaps escaping life force in a 30-foot <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Cube</span> originating from a point within range. A creature <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Hostile</span> to you in the field when it appears, or when it enters the field for the first time on a turn or ends its turn there, is cursed until the end of its next turn. While cursed, the first time the creature takes damage during a turn, it takes an extra `dice:1d6|noform|noparens|avg` (`1d6`) Necrotic damage, and a creature of your choice within the field that isn't cursed by it regains a number of <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Hit Points</span> equal to the Necrotic damage taken.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** The damage increases by `dice:1d6|noform|noparens|avg` (`1d6`) for each spell slot level above 5.
