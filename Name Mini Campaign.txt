D&D ideas:
The land of Zondonerous

 -- Names are a source of power --
   
    The player characters are named but they are unaware of the weight a name carries in this section of the forgotten realms
    The NPCs are unnamed and never ask the players names since it is such a moot point at this time and place
        - Fright engulfs any new NPC that learns of the PC names for they have only ever known the BBEG name among them. 
    The BBEG is a known name and that is why the NPCs are scared of the PCs

  ~Eman~ - BBEG

 When a name is spoken before realization, something happens outside of player agency that is also harmful to surroundings or is unheard by npcs... (cursed)

 When a name is realized the character's power grows exponentially. 
 
 The NPCs are known by their professions.
    - Blacksmith (Smith)
    - Barkeep (Bark) 
    - Apothecary (Apo)
    - Merchant (Merch)
    - Haberdasher (Haber)
 
  Names are stripped
  by means of magic long forgotten by any of the realm of Zondonerous.


 A name stripped away this way is a near death experience. 
 
 The inital purge of the commonfolk happened many many centuries ago and there hasn't been a culling since. Only the strong survived. 
 The village and outskirts are thinly populated at this point in time.

 The PCs were previously residents of this village but upon awakening remembered a word that they realize is their name.
 Campaign starts with combat - underlings of BBEG coming to terrorize/collect something from the village.
 post-combat the NPCs are scared of the heroes shunning them from the village scared of the power and possible correlation
  with the BBEG - one NPC meets them on the outskirts of the village, a friend of all 4 yet unknown, this person seems familiar 
  but only recently.
 Hooded figure is BBEG but keeps it hidden, refers to themself as Townsmith (mayor of sorts), has awakened our heroes potential to do their bidding
