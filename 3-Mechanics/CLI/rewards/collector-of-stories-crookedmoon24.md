# Collector of Stories
*Dark Bargain*  

You have an insatiable desire to gather the tales of the world. Sometimes, you inadvertently take the story of meeting another person and keep it to yourself, forever.

## Gathered Tales

You always have the Legend Lore spell prepared. You can cast it once without a spell slot or spell components, and you regain the ability to cast it in that way when you finish a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Long Rest</span>. You can also cast it using slots you have of the appropriate level. Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma is your spellcasting ability for the spell when you cast it without a spell slot (choose the ability when you select this Dark Bargain).

## Hoarded Stories

When you use this bargain to cast Legend Lore without a spell slot, a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Friendly</span> NPC (the GM chooses) forgets ever meeting you. You can meet them again and attempt to rebuild a relationship, but you forever cause an unsettling discomfort in them. That NPC's attitude toward you can't ever improve beyond <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Indifferent</span>, and you have <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Disadvantage</span> on all Charisma checks against them.

*Source: The Crooked Moon (2024) p. 118*
