# Crown of the Barrow King
*Wondrous item (requires attunement)*  
![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheGiddyLimit/homebrew-img/refs/heads/main/img/CrookedMoon24/Items/Crown%20of%20the%20Barrow%20King.webp#right)

- **Properties**: [Fabled Heirloom](/3-Mechanics/CLI/item-properties.md#Fabled%20Heirloom)

This crown of twisted iron has five sharp, tall spikes that are crusted with dried blood. None who see it doubt the malice and corruption that the Barrow King imbued into it.

While you wear the crown, you gain a bonus determined by the crown's rarity to spell attack rolls and to your spell save DC for Necromancy spells and spells that deal Necrotic damage.
