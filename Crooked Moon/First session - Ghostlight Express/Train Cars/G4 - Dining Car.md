### Phantasmal Food
- The dining car allows for the conjuration of food by sitting at a booth and saying the name of the food or drink item and it will be prepared perfectly and presented to the player.
- A character who asks for steak and eggs can take the plate of food with them and use it to help free the [[Jailbird]] in the [[G6 - Baggage Car]].
