# Visage of the Old Ways (Very Rare)
*Wondrous item, very rare (requires attunement)*  
![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheGiddyLimit/homebrew-img/refs/heads/main/img/CrookedMoon24/Items/Visage%20of%20the%20Old%20Ways.webp#right)

- **Properties**: [Fabled Heirloom](/3-Mechanics/CLI/item-properties.md#Fabled%20Heirloom)

This wood-carved mask has a haunted expression and makes the wearer's eyes look like empty shadows. Rabbit ears protrude over the head. While you wear the mask, you hear the whispers of an ancient forest archfey deep within your mind.

While wearing the mask, you gain a +4 bonus to your damage rolls when you deal Fire damage determined by the mask's rarity, and you can use the following properties.

## Cultic Mask (Rare+)

You can't be targeted by Divination spells or perceived through scrying sensors. Ability checks made to discern your identity have a minimum DC of 25.

## Fire Within (Rare+)

While you are burning, your weapon attacks deal an extra `dice:2d4|noform|noparens|avg` (`2d4`) Fire damage. You can take a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Bonus Action</span> to begin or stop burning.

## Strange Sight (Rare+)

You have Darkvision with a range of 60 feet, and you can see normally through areas obscured by smoke.

## Mastery of Flame (Very Rare+)

Fire damage you deal ignores <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Resistance</span>.

## Sacrificial Flame (Very Rare+)

As a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Bonus Action</span>, you can cause a creature you can see with 0 <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Hit Points</span> that is within 30 feet of you to burst into flame. Its body burns to ash over the next minute, and when a creature <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Hostile</span> to you starts its turn in a 10-foot <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Emanation</span> originating from the body, it takes `dice:2d8|noform|noparens|avg` (`2d8`) Fire damage. The flame is extinguished if the target regains any <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Hit Points</span>.

*Source: The Crooked Moon (2024) p. 607*
