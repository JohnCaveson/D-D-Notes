# Cutlass of Briggsy Kratch (Very Rare)
*Weapon (scimitar), very rare (requires attunement)*  
![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheGiddyLimit/homebrew-img/refs/heads/main/img/CrookedMoon24/Items/Cutlass%20of%20Briggsy%20Kratch.webp#right)

- **Damage**: 1d6 slashing
- **Properties**: [Fabled Heirloom](/3-Mechanics/CLI/item-properties.md#Fabled%20Heirloom), [Finesse](/3-Mechanics/CLI/item-properties.md#Finesse), [Light](/3-Mechanics/CLI/item-properties.md#Light)
- **Weight**: 3.0 lbs.

A fearsome weapon imbued with jinxcraft magic, this cutlass was once wielded by the notorious pirate captain, Briggsy "The Kutlass" Kratch. Its tarnished blade glimmers with a faint, otherworldly light. A skull and crossbones adorn the leather-wrapped hilt, and arcane symbols etched into the blade pulse with hidden power.

You gain a +2 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with the cutlass determined by its rarity. While holding the cutlass, you can use the following properties.

## Raise the Stakes (Rare+)

As a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Bonus Action</span>, choose a creature within 5 feet of you and mark it until the start of your next turn. Count the number of times you hit the target and the number of times it hits you while the mark lasts. If you hit the target more times, it takes `dice:2d10|noform|noparens|avg` (`2d10`) Psychic damage. If it hits you more times, you take `dice:2d10|noform|noparens|avg` (`2d10`) Psychic damage. If your hits are tied, you both take `dice:2d10|noform|noparens|avg` (`2d10`) Psychic damage. Once this property is used, it can't be used again until the next dusk.

## Sea Legs (Rare+)

When you take the Disengage action, <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Difficult Terrain</span> doesn't cost you extra movement until the end of that turn.

## Double Down (Very Rare+)

When you take the Attack action on your turn, you can make one additional attack against a target marked by the Raise the Stakes property.

## Jinxcraft Panache (Very Rare+)

When a creature hits you with an attack roll, you can take a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Reaction</span> to increase your AC against that attack by twice the cutlass's bonus to attack and damage rolls if the creature already dealt damage to you on this turn.

*Source: The Crooked Moon (2024) p. 598*
