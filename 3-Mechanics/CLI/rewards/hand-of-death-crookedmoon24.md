# Hand of Death
*Dark Bargain*  

Death has no claim on you. In exchange for staying its cold hand without that hand closing, you become a maw through which death feeds on the world around you.

## Adamant Endurance

You have <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Advantage</span> on Constitution saving throws.

## Cling to Life

When you have 0 <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Hit Points</span> at the start of your turn, or when you die, you can instead choose to regain a number of <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Hit Points</span> equal to half your maximum. Once you use this feature, you can't use it again. After using it, whenever you regain <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Hit Points</span> through any means, you gain only half that amount. Once a Humanoid (other than you) dies within 30 feet of you, you regain the use of this feature, and you can regain <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Hit Points</span> as normal.

## Death Knell

Other creatures within 10 feet of you have <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Disadvantage</span> on <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Death Saving Throws</span>, and you can't restore <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Hit Points</span> to a creature with 0 <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Hit Points</span> by any means.

*Source: The Crooked Moon (2024) p. 119*
