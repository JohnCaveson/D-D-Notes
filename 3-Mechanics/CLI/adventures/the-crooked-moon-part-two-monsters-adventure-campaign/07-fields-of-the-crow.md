# Fields of the Crow
*Source: The Crooked Moon Part Two: Monsters & Adventure Campaign, p. 239* 

![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheGiddyLimit/homebrew-img/refs/heads/main/img/CrookedMoon24/Adventure/142-06-001.fields-of-the-crow-opener.webp#center)

> [!quote] A quote from Raum, The Harvest Terror  
> 
> The crows sing. The fields remember. With every rustle of the stalks, every whispered secret, I feed. Come now. Let the stalks say your name. Let the crows sing your story. I'll harvest what you hide.

A demon has come to Foxwillow. Once a quiet town cradled by endless cornfields, now these fields devour any who dares enter. They belong to Raum, [The Harvest Terror](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/npc/the-harvest-terror-crookedmoon24.md)—a demonic scarecrow with the head of a crow, cursed with an insatiable hunger for secrets. His very being is nourished by forbidden truths, yet he doesn't merely feed on them. He weaves them into chaos, turning neighbor against neighbor, sowing discord as effortlessly as seeds. Only the destruction of Raum can save this once-peaceful town.

## Chapter Background

East of Wickermoor Village lies the town of Foxwillow, known far and wide as the breadbasket of Wickermoor Hollow. The people of this idyllic town are friendly, rural folk. They worship the Patient Lady, dutifully tend to the fields, help one another, and never speak ill of other souls... to their faces, at least. But evil lurks below the façade.

Secret resentments have multiplied in recent months, darkening Foxwillow like the shadow cast by a murder of crows. People have grown suspicious of others, certain their neighbors harbor vile secrets. Crops have failed (sparing only the corn), insects swarm, and cattle waste away and die, while the Foxwillow folk blame each other for negligence, laziness, and stupidity.

It began six months ago when Ol' Jericho Sticks disappeared. A living scarecrow, Jericho had wandered Foxwillow for generations, crafting effigies for the fields and singing as evening fell. When he vanished into the corn, no trace of him remained. Whispers of murder spread, and hidden grudges tore the town apart.

As blame grew, the crops withered—except for the cornfields to the east. Worse still, more townsfolk disappeared, their places taken by grotesque scarecrow effigies (see The Harvest Terror later in this chapter).

## Chapter Summary

Foxwillow is a haunted husk of a town. None dare trust enough to band together, nor is anyone brave enough to venture into the corn where the lost have vanished. Characters drawn to the town by the Quest Hook later in this chapter have these goals:

- Venture to Foxwillow with the quest-giver, William Lodge.  
- Stop the survivors in Foxwillow from turning on each other.  
- Offer secrets to Raum (their own, or secrets learned within Foxwillow) to gain entrance to the demonic corn maze demiplane known as the Fields of the Crow.  
- Survive the dangers of the Fields of the Crow and find Raum.  
- Destroy Raum, [The Harvest Terror](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/npc/the-harvest-terror-crookedmoon24.md).  

Before running this chapter, familiarize yourself with Raum, the key NPC, to infuse his menace throughout. A casual reading of the Foxwillow locations helps when you improvise interactions. Lastly, reviewing the climactic battle, The Wicked Harvest, ensures a smooth and gripping final encounter.

*This chapter is designed for four level 4 characters.*

## The Harvest Terror

A strange and wonderful creature named Ol' Jericho Sticks, a sentient scarecrow, came to Wickermoor Hollow years ago. The scarecrow, a wandering tunesmith, held a secret inside of him, literally and figuratively: a crow demon lurked within Jericho's straw-stuffed body and was the true and terrible power that gave him life.

### Ol' Jericho Sticks

![Raum and Ol' Jericho Sticks](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheGiddyLimit/homebrew-img/refs/heads/main/img/CrookedMoon24/Adventure/143-06-002.raum-and-ol-jericho-sticks.webp#center)

Jericho was a haunted scarecrow—but a friendly one. He traipsed about the farmlands around Foxwillow, singing songs to drown out the sounds of sorrow within him. The people of Foxwillow loved the straw-stuffed drifter who wove through the rows of straw and stover, serenading the fieldhands on his banjo as they worked.

But eventually, even music was not enough to resist the voice inside his head. As the decades passed, the demon that Jericho called "Virgil" grew in power and slowly spread his discord to the people of Foxwillow. As a result, the townsfolk became more suspicious of him, filling the scarecrow with increasing self-doubt. Virgil preyed on this uncertainty, manipulating the wounded psyche of his host until Jericho gave in to the whispers.

When Jericho realized the demon inside him was gaining control, he retreated into the cornfields to keep his beloved Foxwillowers safe. Tragically, the struggle was short. His soul was devoured by Virgil, and the two merged into a new demon in the crumpled scarecrow body. He is now Raum, [The Harvest Terror](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/npc/the-harvest-terror-crookedmoon24.md), a crow-like Fiend with a body of straw and rusted metal.

### The Scourge of Foxwillow

Raum *(rah-OOM)* loves to destroy all that Jericho loved to create. He stalks the rural lands of eastern Wickermoor Hollow, wielding sharp blades and sharper words to drive fear into the hearts of all who live there. He takes special glee in luring people from Jericho's beloved Foxwillow into his demiplane of endless corn mazes, the Fields of the Crow, to steal their bodies, souls, and secrets.

Jericho is gone forever, but tiny scraps of his kindly consciousness still cling like rotten flesh to Raum's vile bones. If the characters learn the tender secrets of Ol' Jericho Sticks, what's left of him fights back and aids them (see Gathering Information and The Wicked Harvest later in this chapter).

### Raum's Curse

Raum feeds on secrets and delights in destruction. Learning secrets sustains him and grants him greater power. Not only do they increase his demonic magic, but they also empower him to manipulate mortal hearts. He has two primary means of harvesting secrets:

![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheGiddyLimit/homebrew-img/refs/heads/main/img/CrookedMoon24/Adventure/144-06-003.crows.webp#center)

#### Black-Feathered Spies

Murders of crows gaze hungrily upon Foxwillow in numbers great enough to make bare trees look flush with black leaves when they gather. They are always listening, waiting to report back bits of information to Raum.

#### Gateway to the Stalks

The second method is the gateway to the Fields of the Crow. All who approach it of their own free will are addressed by Raum directly. They are bidden to sacrifice a damning secret if they wish to gain entry to Raum's domain. This sacrifice empowers Raum's game statistics (see The Wicked Harvest at the end of this chapter). At your discretion, this secret sparks nasty rumors about the character who divulged it back in Foxwillow and Wickermoor Village.

## NPCs of Foxwillow

![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheGiddyLimit/homebrew-img/refs/heads/main/img/CrookedMoon24/Adventure/145-06-004.foxwillow-scene.webp#center)

The town of Foxwillow is largely abandoned. Most of its people have been lured into the cornfield and vanished. All missing folk have been replaced in town by eerie scarecrows, described wherever they are in town (see Areas within Foxwillow later in this chapter). However, there are five noteworthy NPCs still in town.

These NPCs have complicated relationships and hide many secrets from one another that prevent them from trusting each other. When the characters meet the NPCs in town, use these descriptions and the Foxwillow Relationship Chart at the end of this section to help you roleplay them.

### William Lodge

A tenant farmer of the wealthy Thatcher family, William (Neutral Good, human Commoner) lives on the outskirts of Foxwillow and tends grains and gourds while raising livestock. As such, he has a close relationship with both the people of Foxwillow and of Wickermoor Village, which he often travels to monthly for market.

![William Lodge](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheGiddyLimit/homebrew-img/refs/heads/main/img/CrookedMoon24/Adventure/146-06-005.william-lodge.webp#center)

#### Changing William's Attitude

Though <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Indifferent</span> toward the characters when they meet him, his attitude increases one step to <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Friendly</span> if they agree to join him in saving Foxwillow. His attitude decreases one step to <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Hostile</span> if they intentionally cause destruction or loot the town (such as looting the statue in area J2) but increases one step if they take action to help (such as putting out fires when the town is burning).

His attitude increases one step if the characters demonstrate selfless heroism or emotional tenderness. His attitude decreases one step if they are selfish or try to Influence him to learn a secret from him and fail their Charisma check.

#### William's Secret

Though unmarried himself, William had an affair with another farmer's wife, Martha Langtree, who soon had a son with him. This son is the middle Langtree child, Isaac.

##### Learning His Secret

As an Influence action, a character convinces William to reveal this secret with a successful DC 15 Charisma (Intimidation or Persuasion) check.

### Martha Langtree

Martha (Lawful Good, human Commoner) is a widow of ten years who works her farm with the help of her three children Adelaide, Isaac, and Rosemary. Lean and toned from years of hard work, she has a brash personality and is often called a force of nature by the townsfolk.

Martha loves all her children dearly but worries for them. Adelaide has acted strangely since Ol' Jericho Sticks vanished, then Rosemary vanished a week ago. Just this dusk, Isaac went missing too and Adelaide seems unconcerned. Martha is at her wits' end and inadvertently started a brawl in the Old Windmill (see Foxwillow in Flames later in this chapter) while William was out of town seeking aid.

![Martha Langtree](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheGiddyLimit/homebrew-img/refs/heads/main/img/CrookedMoon24/Adventure/147-06-006.martha-langtree.webp#center)

#### Changing Martha's Attitude

Despite the horrors occurring at her doorstep, Martha is easy to befriend. She becomes immediately <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Friendly</span> if they save Jonah from death in the The Burning Mill encounter. Otherwise, she is <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Indifferent</span>.

Her attitude increases one step if the characters demonstrate selfless heroism or emotional tenderness. Her attitude decreases one step if they are selfish or try to Influence her to learn a secret from her and fail their Charisma check.

#### Martha's Secret

Farmer Malachai, Martha's husband, was a violent drunk, and Martha accidentally killed him while defending herself. She has kept this secret ever since to protect her children.

##### Learning Her Secret

As an Influence action, a character convinces Martha to reveal this secret with a successful DC 15 Charisma (Intimidation or Persuasion) check. This secret is in addition to her extramarital role in William's secret (see above).

### Adelaide Langtree

Adelaide (Chaotic Evil, human Cultist) is the eldest child of Martha at twenty-five years old. She was a difficult child who played over-competitively with the other children and enjoyed morbid practical jokes to peeve her unpleasant father, like hiding rat carcasses in his boots overnight. Jericho was her best friend because she had a hard time making friends with other kids her age.

![Adelaide Langtree](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheGiddyLimit/homebrew-img/refs/heads/main/img/CrookedMoon24/Adventure/148-06-007.adelaide-langtree.webp#center)

As she grew older, Adelaide's antisocial behavior deepened. Jericho vanished on her twenty-fifth birthday, and ever since, she's wandered into the cornfields after her nightly work, only returning when it's time for bed. Some whisper she killed Jericho, while others claim she's searching for her only friend. None who followed her into the corn have ever returned.

Three weeks after Jericho's disappearance, she found him—now the monstrous, crow-headed demon, Raum. The Harvest Terror spoke with Jericho's voice, calling himself her best friend, vowing to love her if she revealed her secrets. She confessed witnessing her father's abuse and her mother's fatal act.

The demon Raum told her that he is an angel sent to reveal the evil secrets in Foxwillow. Confused, but seduced by his words, Adelaide has spent recent months gathering secrets and luring folks into the cornfields, where Raum spirits them away to wander forever in the Fields of the Crow.

#### Changing Adelaide's Attitude

Adelaide is fully in Raum's thrall. She can't be swayed from being <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Hostile</span> toward the characters.

#### Adelaide's Secret

In her communion with Raum, Adelaide has learned William's secret—that her brother Isaac is really her half-brother. She's been manipulated into believing that her mother's infidelity and her half-brother's birth are the real causes of her fear and suffering in childhood. She was goaded by Raum into murdering Isaac, and she hid his corpse in the basement of the Old Windmill (area J1), where it is incubating a demonic servant of The Harvest Terror.

##### Learning Her Secret

Adelaide's murderous secret is revealed when the characters find her brother's corpse in the Windmill Cellar (area J9b). If her mother Martha is told of what happened, Martha weeps and gives the characters a handmade brass locket her brother made for her to try and earn her love as a child.

### Jonah Sawyer

Jonah (Neutral Good, human Tough) has a reputation as a rugged, broad-shouldered man with a humor as hefty as his sinewed frame. As Foxwillow's butcher, he's often seen in a bloodstained apron striding through town. His signature platform shoes, worn to keep his feet dry from pigs' blood, earned him the nickname "Heels." His only daughter, Liza, has gone missing in recent nights.

Though he's truly not responsible for all Foxwillow's ills, Jonah has been on edge lately, burdened by guilt over the town's curse and the disappearance of his daughter. In a fit of rage, he cursed William Lodge, and his shame has made him antagonistic toward his neighbors (see Foxwillow in Flames later in this chapter).

#### Changing Jonah's Attitude

Suspicious by nature, Jonah is difficult to befriend. If characters talk with him about breaking the curse, he asks if they think whoever caused the curse was evil, or just misguided. His attitude increases one step to <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Friendly</span> if they say they'll be sympathetic to the curse-maker or that they'll keep an eye out for Liza. If they suggest that they'll kill the person who cursed Foxwillow, he becomes fearful and <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Hostile</span>, refusing to speak at length.

![Jonah Sawyer](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheGiddyLimit/homebrew-img/refs/heads/main/img/CrookedMoon24/Adventure/149-06-008.jonah-sawyer.webp#center)

#### Jonah's Secret

Jonah has harbored resentment for William Lodge for years, though he no longer recalls why. By all accounts, William is well-liked and even sells livestock to the butcher. But Jonah recently discovered William had been selling him scrawnier stock than he sells to Siv Harga in Wickermoor Village, who pays a higher price for well-cared-for prize beasts.

In a fit of ire, Jonah slaughtered a lamb and invoked an old folk hex his grandmother once whispered about. He cursed William's fields and herds and—to his horror—the curse took root. Now, Foxwillow's crops and livestock wither—save for the demon-infested cornfield, which thrives unnaturally. Jonah, wracked with guilt, grows defensive, fearing the town's ruin rests on his shoulders.

##### Learning His Secret

As an Influence action, characters convince Jonah to reveal this secret with a successful DC 15 Charisma (Intimidation or Persuasion) check.

### Isaac Langtree

A boyishly handsome man of twenty-one, Isaac Langtree ran the Hooded Lantern, his family's tavern, but he continued helping his mother on the farm when able. He vanished the night before the characters arrived, but not into the cornfield. He was lured into the basement of the Old Windmill (area J1) by his sister Adelaide and ritualistically murdered. His corpse is there still.

#### Isaac's Secret

Charm and good looks made him popular in town, but he secretly despised the quaint, folksy ways of his rural neighbors. He had developed an especially intense dislike of manual labor after apprenticing as a butcher under Jonah Sawyer in his youth. Each market night, he skimmed money from his mother and stashed it under a floorboard in the tavern in preparation to leave for a life on the road. He was trying to convince his sister Adelaide to skip town with him when she killed him.

##### Learning His Secret

Isaac's duplicitous secret is revealed when the characters find his secret cache under a floorboard in the Hooded Lantern (area J4).

### Scarecrow Townsfolk

![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheGiddyLimit/homebrew-img/refs/heads/main/img/CrookedMoon24/Adventure/150-06-012.weird-pumpkin.webp#center)

Most of Foxwillow's people have vanished, lost in the Fields of the Crow. Their bodies, twisted under Raum's control, are now grotesque scarecrows—burlap stuffed with straw and pumpkin heads carved into haunting mockeries of their former faces. Their spirits, however, are doomed to wander the endless maze of cornstalks. Only Raum's destruction frees the lost spirits and turns the scarecrows back into people.

These scarecrows litter the town, sitting slumped in homes and shops, grim reminders of those they replaced. Descriptions of several scarecrows are found in each area.

#### Jumpscarecrow!

If the investigation of the town is slowing down, `dice:1d3|noform|noparens|avg` (`1d3`) [Pumpkin Heads](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/fiend/pumpkin-head-crookedmoon24.md) spring to life and attack. After the scarecrows are defeated, have a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Friendly</span> NPC rush up and share a Vital Clue or Optional Clue they've learned (see Investigating Foxwillow later in this chapter) to give the characters a new lead.

These scarecrows are the missing townsfolk's mortal bodies. If a pumpkin head is reduced to 0 <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Hit Points</span> by Fire damage (burning the body) or Bludgeoning damage (squashing the pumpkin head), the mortal body is also destroyed in a similarly gruesome way when it reverts back to its original human form after Raum is defeated. Other types of damage typically just muss the straw or burlap, leaving the mortal body with scars but no lasting wounds once reverted.

## Quest Hook

The many ills of Foxwillow (described in the Chapter Background) have finally driven one man to flee his town and seek aid in Wickermoor Village.

### Foxwillow's Cursed!

In Wickermoor Village, the characters are approached by Jeremiah Stover and introduced to his friend William Lodge, a man from Foxwillow who has told Jeremiah his tale of fear and woe. Jeremiah asks William to repeat it and pleads with the characters to listen. William Lodge is a salt-of-the-earth, middle-aged farmer with a folksy accent and an earnest love for his village in his eyes. He lives on one of the failing farms on the outskirts of town, so he doesn't know the whole story, but he explains what has happened as best he knows it,

- Ol' Jericho Sticks, the living, singing scarecrow, vanished from town six months ago. Ever since, townsfolk have been accusing one another of murdering the beloved handyman and songster. (If asked for more information on Jericho, William explains that no one knows how the friendly strawman came to be, but he's been beloved by Foxwillow since before William was born.)  
- After a few months, no one in town trusted each other, and recently everything started going wrong. All crops but the corn failed, livestock withered and died, and strange sigils started appearing in the cornfields.  
- Then, only weeks ago, townsfolk began going missing, seemingly replaced by mysterious scarecrows.  
- Hardly anyone is left in Foxwillow, and most everyone has barricaded themselves inside their houses. No one sets their suspicions aside to work together.  

Jeremiah explains that while the farmers of Wickermoor Village produce as much food as possible, they still rely on the grain Foxwillow produces to feed the town. If the characters break the curse upon Foxwillow, save its people, and salvage the harvest, Jeremiah rallies the town to chip in and pay them each 50 GP.

If Phillip Druskenvald is with the characters at the time or learns of the request from them later, he offers to match the 50 GP from his own funds and says he wants to take a more active role in helping the people of Wickermoor Hollow since it's technically his responsibility as Lord of Druskenvald.

If the characters have more questions, William says he'll be happy to talk about the villagers and the specifics while they travel, offering passage on his horse-drawn cart; it'll take them a couple nights to reach Foxwillow, so there'll be plenty of time.

## Foxwillow in Flames

The journey to Foxwillow is about 2 nights at a Normal pace. At the end of the trek, they find Foxwillow aflame, with lifelike scarecrows brawling in the streets. It happened like this:

- Jonah Sawyer called Martha Langtree's daughter Adelaide a "creep" and a "killer" to Martha's face in the town square.  
- Martha, already on edge due to the disappearance of Rosemary last week, threw a punch at Jonah, who retaliated.  
- Their anger awakened the hatred lingering within the bodies of the scarecrow townsfolk (see Scarecrow Townsfolk earlier in this chapter). As they fought in the square, the scarecrows came to life and joined the brawl.  
- Horrified, Martha and Jonah fled to the windmill. In their haste, the butcher dropped his lantern. Flour in the mill went up like tinder, setting the whole structure ablaze.  
- The windmill's burning, spinning sails spread the fire to nearby houses.  
- Raum's scarecrows continue to fight until Martha and Jonah apologize to each other.  

### Arriving in Foxwillow

The characters arrive at the outskirts of Foxwillow amidst this conflagration.

> [!readaloud] 
> 
> For hours, your cart has creaked through barren fields littered with ragged scarecrows, wilted wheat, and rotting pumpkins. The air carries the bitter scent of smoke, and ahead, a fiery glow consumes the horizon—the town is burning. William urges the horses faster.
> 
> Moments later, you arrive in the town square, where a wild brawl unfolds among a dozen farmers. Two buildings—the town hall and a small shop—are engulfed in flames, set alight by the blazing windmill, now a spinning tower of fire. William turns to you, eyes wide with horror, and pleads, "By the Lady! Please save Foxwillow!"
^258

The brawlers are all scarecrows of the townsfolk dressed in their clothes and with eerie carved or painted faces (see Scarecrow Townsfolk, earlier in this chapter), though their pumpkin heads are obscured by hair, hats, or bonnets from a distance. As a Search action, characters scrutinize the townsfolk and discern what the scarecrows are with a successful DC 13 Wisdom (Perception) check. A character within 5 feet of a brawler sees the truth automatically.

The solution to ending this brawl is to save the people who started it: Martha Langtree and Jonah Sawyer. They're fighting in the Old Windmill (see The Burning Mill). Likewise, the town continues to burn until the windmill fire is put out.

The characters are under no obligation to stop the fight or put out the fires, but William urges them to help and mentions the pump west of the town square (see Getting Water below). It's a good idea, too: they have an easier time entering the Fields of the Crow and finding Raum if they've met Martha.

### Navigating the Brawl

Using the Foxwillow map, the characters must pass through Magpie Square (area J2) to reach the Old Windmill (area J1). Each brawling townsperson is a [Pumpkin Head](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/fiend/pumpkin-head-crookedmoon24.md) animated by Raum's demonic influence.

The scarecrows exclusively fight each other unless their attention is drawn by casting a spell, attacking one of the pumpkin heads, or putting out the fires in areas J5 and J8. During this event, scarecrows are found in:

Magpie Square (area J2): Three pumpkin heads hurl each other about and kick up the mud in the streets, making the town square <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Difficult Terrain</span>.

General Store (area J5): Two pumpkin heads toss bottles and bags of cornmeal while the building burns around them. A creature that ends its turn in the store takes `dice:1d4|noform|noparens|avg|text(2)` (`1d4`) Fire damage.

Town Hall (area J8): Three pumpkin heads smash chairs over each other. The fire hasn't spread past the roof yet.

The scarecrows in other areas of town are inanimate.

### Firefighting

Fire is raging in the General Store (area J5) and the Town Hall (area J8). If these buildings aren't doused, the fire spreads through the densely packed town square and burns Foxwillow (and all the scarecrows in it) to the ground.

#### Putting Out a Fire

Putting out the fire in areas J5 and J8 requires at least 10 gallons of water to be hurled on each area's blaze.

#### Getting Water

The best way to put out these fires is by casting the Create or Destroy Water spell or using other magic that creates water. There is also a water pump west of Magpie Square (area J2). William assists the characters by operating the pump to fill buckets of water. Characters who grab a filled bucket can take the Utilize action to douse an area's flames with 1 gallon of water.

#### Drawing Attention

Putting out a fire causes all the [Pumpkin Heads](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/fiend/pumpkin-head-crookedmoon24.md) in that area to attack the characters responsible.

### The Burning Mill

Isaac's corpse is in the basement of the windmill, but the roar of the fire and the collapsed debris obscures his remains. When the fire ends, the characters can find his body in the basement (area J9a). Adelaide is missing and under Raum's influence (see Into the Fields of the Crow).

Martha Langtree and Jonah Sawyer are trapped in the windmill. If they die without setting their anger aside, the scarecrows remain fueled by hate and fight until they're destroyed. The Windmill map (see below) shows the interior of the windmill, including its four floors and basement.

#### Burning

Spaces marked with Xs on the map are burning. A creature that enters a burning space for the first time on a turn or starts its turn there must succeed on a DC 10 Dexterity saving throw or take `dice:2d4|noform|noparens|avg|text(5)` (`2d4`) Fire damage.

#### Townsfolk

Jonah Sawyer is on the fourth floor, trying to fend off a slowly approaching [Pumpkin Head](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/fiend/pumpkin-head-crookedmoon24.md) that has taken the shape of his daughter, Liza. Martha is on the first floor, trying to clear rubble blocking the door.

#### J1a: Windmill Exterior

Read or paraphrase the following when the characters reach the door to the windmill:

> [!readaloud] 
> 
> The windmill's sails spin wildly, spewing cinders all around. A portion of the wall above the door has collapsed, blocking the door with burning debris. You hear rubble being shifted and a woman yelping in pain from the other side of the door.
^263

Two 5-foot squares in front of the main doors and two more 5-foot squares behind it are filled with burning rubble. The door opens outward, and all four squares of rubble need to be cleared for the characters to enter.

##### Difficult Terrain

A space of rubble is <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Difficult Terrain</span>, and as a Utilize action, characters clear it with a successful DC 15 Strength check. It is also cleared by bringing a 5-foot space of rubble to 0 <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Hit Points</span> (AC 8, HP 20, <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Immunity</span> to Poison and Psychic damage, and <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Vulnerability</span> to Bludgeoning, Cold, and Thunder damage). A creature that touches the rubble without tools takes `dice:2d4|noform|noparens|avg|text(5)` (`2d4`) Fire damage.

##### Alternative Route

The source of the rubble is the wall above the door. There is a 5-foot-wide hole in the wall 10 feet above the ground. This space is burning.

#### J1b: Windmill First Floor

Inside the burning mill is Martha Langtree. Her hair singed, her hands burned and hastily wrapped in torn cloth, she stammers her thanks. Shaking, she admits the fire was her doing—a fight with Jonah led to a lantern falling, igniting the mill. Then, in terror, she recounts how a grotesque scarecrow burst through the door before the wall collapsed, chasing him upstairs. "You have to help Jonah!" she pleads, fleeing the windmill.

#### J1c: Windmill Second Floor

This floor is burning, and the flames have spread to block the path to the stairs leading up.

##### Time Pressure

There are no foes in this room, but roll <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Initiative</span> when a character ascends to this floor and keep them in <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Initiative</span> order the entire time they are in the windmill. On <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Initiative</span> count 20 of each round, describe how the windmill trembles as the strain of its wildly spinning sails threatens to tear the fire-weakened structure apart. Each creature in the mill must make a DC 10 Dexterity saving throw, taking `dice:2d4|noform|noparens|avg|text(5)` (`2d4`) Fire damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.

On <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Initiative</span> count 0 (losing <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Initiative</span> ties) of the ninth round after the characters enter this floor of the windmill, the entire structure collapses. When it does, each creature in the windmill falls to the bottom floor, taking `dice:6d6|noform|noparens|avg|text(21)` (`6d6`) Fire damage from the collapse, plus `dice:1d6|noform|noparens|avg|text(3)` (`1d6`) Bludgeoning damage for each floor they fell.

#### J1d: Windmill Third Floor

This floor has collapsed, leaving nothing more than smoldering support beams and the wildly spinning central shaft and driven gears.

##### Spinning Cog

On <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Initiative</span> count 20 (losing <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Initiative</span> ties), the cogs spin 180 degrees, moving any creature on the cog to the space opposite of the space they were on.

#### J1e: Windmill Fourth Floor

The cramped top floor of the windmill is dominated by a single horizontal beam that supports the mill's main shaft and the gears which turn it. This beam is burning, and Jonah Sawyer is on the far side. A [Pumpkin Head](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/fiend/pumpkin-head-crookedmoon24.md) is on the side closest to the ladder the characters are ascending. The scarecrow is menacing Jonah as it tries to find a way around the fire. He's been reduced to 15 <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Hit Points</span>. Jonah begs the characters to not destroy the scarecrow, as its face is twisted into the visage of his missing daughter, Liza.

The characters must get Jonah out before the mill collapses (see area J1c).

### An End to the Brawl

The resolution to the burning mill depends on if Jonah survived or not.

#### If Jonah Survived

Once Martha and Jonah escape the windmill, they exchange apologies. Martha, trembling, apologizes for striking Jonah, and he begrudgingly admits fault for insulting Adelaide, especially with Rosemary missing for a week. To their shock, all remaining scarecrows collapse as they speak. Regardless of the fate of the Liza scarecrow, Jonah takes a moment to mourn his daughter. He sees it as a punishment for cursing Foxwillow.

Martha then tells the characters that, to her knowledge, all residents of Foxwillow but her, Jonah, and William have gone missing. Her children, Isaac and Adelaide, haven't been seen tonight. Martha believes they're hiding, as everyone else who vanished was replaced by a scarecrow. She also suspects any who might remain have locked themselves inside their homes, refusing to emerge until the terror has passed.

Jonah privately confides that he shouldn't have insulted Martha's daughter, though he adds that Adelaide has always been withdrawn, and the curse on Foxwillow has only deepened her isolation.

Martha returns to the Hooded Lantern (area J4) and is <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Friendly</span> to the characters (see Townsfolk Attitude below). She asks them to speak to her there if they need help investigating the town. Jonah likewise returns to his shop (area J6) but is <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Indifferent</span> toward the characters. William Lodge heads to the Town Hall (area J8) after reuniting with Martha and Jonah.

If there are still fires in town, Martha, Jonah, and William work together to put them out.

#### If Jonah Died

Without being able to apologize to Jonah, Martha's anger at him keeps the scarecrows alive. They continue to stalk the town. Whenever the characters roll <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Initiative</span> while in Foxwillow, two [Pumpkin Heads](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/fiend/pumpkin-head-crookedmoon24.md) join the combat on <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Initiative</span> count 20 of the second round.

Martha and William hide in the Town Hall (area J8). Any remaining fires slowly burn out. The town hall's sturdy roof prevents serious damage to it, but all other buildings in the town square eventually burn to the ground if no one tends to them.

## Investigating Foxwillow

Once Martha and (possibly) Jonah have been saved from the windmill, the characters begin their investigation regarding the curse and learn how to enter the Fields of the Crow. They interview the townsfolk to gain vital clues and optional secrets.

It is possible to skip the windmill and the investigation in town by proceeding directly to the Fields of the Crow, but it would be at the cost of the town and the only people left in it.

### Townsfolk Attitude

![Foxwillow Relationship Chart](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheGiddyLimit/homebrew-img/refs/heads/main/img/CrookedMoon24/Adventure/156-06-009.foxwillow-relationship-chart.webp#center)

All of the Foxwillow NPCs (except William Lodge) are <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Hostile</span> toward each other and the characters during the Foxwillow in Flames section, above. However, the townsfolks' attitudes change based on the actions during the brawl and while investigating the town. Their attitude affects how easily the characters Influence them to aid them or divulge information, which is vital to gaining the upper hand in their battle against Raum.

#### Starting Attitude

After the brawl, the NPCs of Foxwillow have the following attitudes, which are tracked on the Foxwillow Relationship Chart.

- William Lodge: <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Indifferent</span>  
- Martha Langtree: <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Indifferent</span> (<span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Friendly</span> if Jonah survived)  
- Adelaide Langtree: <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Hostile</span>  
- Jonah Sawyer: <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Indifferent</span>  

### Gathering Information

By the end of their investigation, the characters must learn several key clues, and they might also learn optional secrets that aid them in the battle against Raum. It's your job to naturally supply the must-know information and the players' job to decide if they want to dig deeper or press on. Ultimately, you want the players to use their wits but not get stuck.

#### Vital Clues

Be sure to convey these points clearly. Any NPC in town is quick to communicate the information below, especially if the pace of the investigation is dragging:

- The demonic happenings only started after the scarecrow Ol' Jericho Sticks went missing.  
- A few people who later went missing reported a horrific birdlike monster stalking the fields and heard a name whispered to them: Raum.  
- Whenever someone goes missing, a scarecrow wearing their clothes with a pumpkin head carved or painted in their likeness appears in their home or workplace.  
- Cornfields surround the town, but people only go missing after they venture into the cornfields to the east of Foxwillow.  
- Just before people vanish into the cornfield, they have been seen whispering to the stalks as the wind rustles through them.  

#### Optional Clues

The NPCs also know optional clues that aid in navigating the Fields of the Crow or fighting Raum. They only reveal these secrets if they're <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Friendly</span> with the characters. You decide which questions succeed on a DC 15 Charisma check to get an NPC to divulge this information:

- The Old Windmill had a cellar (area J9b) that Adelaide and Isaac used to play in as children. She had been spending more time there as of late.  
- Jonah is poised to answer Raum's call and vanish into the corn in search of his daughter after being attacked by a scarecrow bearing her face in the Old Windmill (area J1e).  
- The living scarecrow called Ol' Jericho Sticks liked to sing a song called "Virgil's Birdcage," which was about a kindly man with an angry crow for a heart, and only singing and dancing could keep the nasty crow asleep. The librarian loved the tune and would always ask Jericho to play it for her.  
- The General store owner loved Jericho's music so much that he secretly commissioned a master luthier to make Jericho a new banjo.  
- The people of Foxwillow had become so fond of Jericho that they asked William Lodge to petition the mayor to make him a citizen.  

### Foxwillow Locations

The Foxwillow map shows the center of Foxwillow, but the true heart of the town lies in the farmlands the characters passed to reach the square. The town center holds all the information needed, while the cursed fields beyond are barren. Farmhouses scattered across the land are either abandoned by those who vanished into the corn or sealed shut by frightened farmers unwilling to speak. There's little left to see here (though for creating your own regions within Foxwillow, see the Outside of Town: Pastures section later).

#### J1: Old Windmill

This area is only visited during the Foxwillow in Flames encounter earlier in this chapter. After the windmill burns out, see the Burned-Out Windmill (area J9).

#### J2: Magpie Square

Foxwillow's market square is called Magpie Square by its townsfolk because of a stone statue of a flock of magpies painted in lifelike piebald colors.

##### Treasure

As a Study action, anyone who inspects the statue and succeeds on a DC 16 Intelligence (Investigation) check finds that the magpies' eyes are made of tiny black onyx stones. Working for 10 minutes to remove the eyes produces eighteen onyx marbles each worth 10 GP.

#### J3: Foxwillow Schoolhouse

All twenty seats in this schoolhouse are filled with child-sized scarecrows, and an effigy of a human woman stands before the blackboard, chalked with lyrics for a children's song called "Tubers and Treacle."

##### Clue

Drawn on the blackboard beneath the lyrics is an eerie image of a crow head emerging through tall stalks. The bird is speaking to a group of children and holding out a stick hand toward them, as if expecting payment.

#### J4: The Hooded Lantern

This inn was run by Isaac Langtree. The door is locked, opened with a successful DC 13 Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) check using Thieves' Tools, and the interior is tidy. He wasn't taken by force. All the rented rooms hold a scarecrow effigy of various travelers but no clues. Martha Langtree takes shelter here after the fire has fully burned out.

##### Searching the Reception

Those who spend 10 minutes searching the inn and succeed on a DC 15 Intelligence (Investigation) check find an upturned board behind the counter with a hidden compartment below.

##### Isaac's Secret

Underneath the floorboard is a sack of 300 SP and 1,000 CP embezzled from Martha Langtree. Outside of the sack is a single black coin with a feather icon on one face. The coin crumbles to corn kernels when touched, and whoever touched it hears Raum's raspy voice whisper, "What mother doesn't know won't hurt her. I'll pay her back someday."

#### J5: General Store

The local General Store's roof collapsed during the fire. Sifting through the rubble, the characters find two scarecrows of the shopkeepers with their pumpkin heads caved in.

##### A Tender Secret

As a Search action, characters who succeed on a DC 15 Wisdom (Perception) check find a beautiful banjo buried unharmed in the rubble. Attached to it is a note that reads, "Keep it secret from Jericho 'til next market night. He can't know how much we spent ordering this six-stringer for him."

Finding this secret strengthens the character who finds it in the battle against Raum (see The Wicked Harvest later in this chapter).

#### J6: Butcher's Shop

Jonah Sawyer takes refuge here once the brawl subsides (assuming he didn't perish in the windmill). The shop's storefront leads to a back room, heavy with the metallic stench of blood. A search reveals no meat left left—Foxwillow's livestock have withered. Only crows remain, and a few pitiful, plucked bird carcasses hang limply in the shop, a grim reminder of the town's decay.

##### Investigating the Shop

Jonah won't let anyone into the back room. If a character manages to get in, as a Search action, a successful DC 13 Intelligence (Investigation) check finds the following:

- A ritual circle drawn in dried calf's blood hidden under a rug  
- A cow skull, a chicken skull, and a lamb skull on a shelf, all marked with a bloody thumbprint  
- A bundle of dried wheat, barley, and flax, bound in a blood-drenched rag  

As a Study action, those that succeed on a DC 13 Intelligence (Arcana) check realize that these are components for a ritual curse that causes crops to wither and animals to waste away. It couldn't cause the horrors surrounding the scarecrows and the vanishings. Confronting Jonah with this evidence causes him to reveal his secret but reduces his attitude by one step.

##### Jonah's Plan

As a Search action, those who succeed on a DC 13 Wisdom (Insight) check realize something is bothering Jonah. He keeps toying with a crow feather. If Jonah is <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Friendly</span>, he tells the characters that he's going into the cornfields in a few hours. Seeing a scarecrow with his daughter's face chilled him, and he decided he must save her. As an Influence action, a successful DC 18 Charisma (Intimidation or Persuasion) check convinces him not to go. On a failure, he suggests that he become bait for whatever is lurking in the field so the characters can sneak in after him.

If Jonah isn't on <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Friendly</span> terms with them, a successful group DC 12 Dexterity (Stealth) check allows them to follow him to the edge of the cornfield.

#### J7: Library

The Library is small and doesn't have many books in it. However, it has hundreds of town records, maps showing the boundaries of local farmers' property, and other civic documents. A scarecrow of the librarian has been shoved into an empty bookshelf.

##### A Tender Secret

Clutched in the librarian scarecrow's hand is a gorgeous transcription of a song titled, "Virgil's Birdcage," one of Jericho's classics. If taken, she awakens as a [Pumpkin Head](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/fiend/pumpkin-head-crookedmoon24.md) and attacks. If the scarecrow is reduced to 0 <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Hit Points</span>, it mutters, "He loved this song... I wanted to give him this..."

Finding this secret strengthens the character who finds it in the battle against Raum (see The Wicked Harvest later in this chapter).

#### J8: Town Hall

Foxwillow's Town Hall looms in the heart of the settlement, where the farming folk once gathered at twilight. A crooked billboard stands outside, covered with weathered papers that cling to it stubbornly. William Lodge, who usually holds meetings here, now scours the building for clues while the characters investigate.

##### A Tender Secret

If William is asked if he's found anything, he admits he hasn't found much, except for something strange: the mayor had granted Ol' Jericho Sticks official citizenship, complete with a "birth certificate." William hands it over with a shrug, unsure of its significance.

Finding this secret empowers the character against Raum in the final battle (see The Wicked Harvest later in this chapter).

#### J9: Burned-Out Windmill

When the characters arrive in town, this windmill is a towering inferno thanks to a lantern that was knocked over during a brawl. The burning windmill is described in The Burning Mill, above. This section describes the windmill after the battle.

#### J9a: Charred Foundations

Not much remains of the old windmill after the blaze.

##### Scritch-Scratch

As a Search action, those who examine the area and succeed on a DC 13 Wisdom (Perception) check hear scratching sounds from beneath a charred rug covering a trapdoor to the cellar (see area J9b).

Opening the trapdoor reveals a ladder and a [Ravencraft](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/undead/ravencraft-crookedmoon24.md) that's trying to push the trapdoor up and escape. The macabre creature is the work of Adelaide Langtree, and it croaks and caws at the characters but doesn't attack unless provoked. As an Influence action, a successful DC 12 Wisdom (Animal Handling) or Charisma (Persuasion) check shifts its attitude to <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Friendly</span>. After being calmed, it agrees to serve one of the spellcasting characters as a familiar.

#### J9b: Windmill Cellar

##### Cause of Death

His chest was then gruesomely carved open. While investigating the corpse, a song is heard being sung from within Isaac's chest called "Virgil's Birdcage," which is about a nasty crow living in the chest of a kindly man. #### Outside of Town: Pastures

At the edge of the town square, a cluster of farmhouses encircle a courtyard with an old water pump. Acres of pasture stretch beyond, anchored by the Langtree farm. Lazarus Thatcher, a wealthy farmer, once formed this cooperative with twelve families, but now none remain. All have vanished into the cornfields, replaced by eerie scarecrow effigies.

## Into the Fields of the Crow

To the east of Foxwillow is a cornfield with overly tall stalks that sway and whistle in the wind. The outer edge of this cornfield is a supernatural border; everything within is a nightmarish, demonic demiplane that morphs according to Raum's whims.

### Entering the Cornfield

The Fields of the Crow are guarded by a looming and impenetrable wall of stalks. When approached, two large, orange eyes with pupils in the shape of four-pointed stars appear through the stalks and a raspy, croaking voice whispers:

> [!readaloud] 
> 
> "Lay bare your secrets. Lighten your burden. Do this and you may enter my land of dreams."
^294

If a character tries to force their way through, they push into the corn only to find themselves emerging from the same spot they entered. The thing in the rows laughs gratingly and repeats its offer after the character is forced out. The thing recoils if attacked and retreats into the stalks for 1 hour, after which it returns to repeat its offer to any who approach.

![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheGiddyLimit/homebrew-img/refs/heads/main/img/CrookedMoon24/Adventure/158-06-010.weird-crow.webp#center)

#### Dark Wings, Dark Words

The cornrows are entered by revealing a dark, personal secret to the presence lurking within, or by slipping through with another who paid in secrets before the threshold seals. Sharing the secret feeds Raum, granting him greater strength and control over the character (see The Wicked Harvest later in this chapter).

Those who share someone else's secret can also enter. This grants Raum extra power, but no control over the character.

#### Sneaking In

When someone enters the Fields of the Crow, the stalks remain open for them, and only them, for up to 1 minute. However, those who make a successful DC 18 Dexterity (Stealth) check slip through unnoticed by Raum. If this check fails, they are ejected from the corn as described earlier.

### Fields of the Crow Features

The Fields of the Crow have the following features:

#### Impenetrable Boundary

The Fields of the Crow is a hellish corn maze. Trying to push through or damage the stalks causes the wind to howl and the stalks to thrash at the intruder. The stalks have <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Immunity</span> to all damage, and if the intruder is within 5 feet of the stalks, they are pushed 10 feet away and must make a DC 13 Strength saving throw, taking `dice:3d6|noform|noparens|avg|text(10)` (`3d6`) Bludgeoning damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.

#### Infinite Stalks

Corn stalks grow as tall as redwoods, and creatures that fly can soar upward forever and never reach the top. Despite constantly moving upward, every time a flying creature reaches 120 feet above the ground, they are suddenly 60 feet above the ground.

- **Lighting.** A nightmarish swirl of orange clouds stretches like writhing tendrils from an orange harvest moon that fills half the sky and obscures the stars. The entire area is bathed in Dim Light, tinged pumpkin-orange by the moon.  

#### Sounds of Murder

Hollow echoes of thousands of overlapping cawing crows fill the area.

### Navigating the Maze

![Thread of Malediction](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheGiddyLimit/homebrew-img/refs/heads/main/img/CrookedMoon24/Adventure/056-02-038.thread-of-malediction.webp#center)

When the characters enter the Fields of the Crow, Raum's voice rings out upon the wind:

> [!readaloud] 
> 
> "Such strong wills have entered the rows. I can feel your souls struggle to cling to your flesh. Now you scurry like rats in a maze. No more than three creeping hours until you are lost forever. Come to me in the heart of the fields. Feed me your secrets and drift into eternal sleep."
^29d

After entering the demonic realm of Raum, the characters experience a reality warp into a waking nightmare as they are trapped in an infinite corn maze. They must reach the center of the maze to find and destroy Raum. Along the way, they find wandering spirits of the people of Foxwillow. Destroying Raum saves these spirits, but freeing them from their listless wandering grants the characters boons in the final confrontation.

#### Time Pressure

While in the Fields of the Crow, the characters feel, with growing dread, their sense of self slowly slipping away. After three hours, the characters' souls are ripped from their bodies, condemned to wander the Fields of the Crow for eternity. Their bodies transform into scarecrows, forever trapped in Foxwillow. This could mark the end of the campaign—unless a path to salvation remains. Perhaps a new band of adventurers is introduced, guided by the willful spirits of the fallen, or The Crooked Man, acting for the Crooked Queen, offers salvation at a dreadful price.

#### Which Way?

This maze has no map. Instead, those lost inside must choose the correct path at three Junctions to reach Raum. Each Junction is a 50-foot-radius clearing with three new paths to choose from: Left, Right, or Center. To discover which path is correct, a Search action and a successful DC 18 Wisdom (Perception) check allows the characters to discern which path has the loudest cawing of crows. On a failure, they only learn which path is the quietest (narrowing their options down to two). If the check fails by 5 or more, the crass cacophony of the crows surrounds them, making it impossible to discern the paths. The correct order of paths to follow is:

- First Junction: Left Path  
- Second Junction: Right Path  
- Third Junction: Left Path  

When the characters enter the maze, they travel through the winding, 5-foot-wide paths of the maze for 10 minutes before reaching a Junction with three paths and a random encounter (see "Random Encounters" below). If they choose the wrong path at any Junction, they wander for 10 minutes before reaching a Dead End (also a 50-foot radius clearing in the corn maze and a random encounter). They must then backtrack to the last Junction to choose again.

If they choose the correct path, they wind through the corn maze for 10 minutes before reaching the next Junction. When they choose the correct path from the Third Junction, they travel for 10 minutes before reaching the center of the maze. See The Wicked Harvest, for the final battle against Raum.

##### Disorienting Crowsong

A murder of crows circles above the corn maze clustering thick enough to blot out the orange moon, and their song becomes a disorienting cacophony. At the start of each character's turn while in this Junction, roll `dice:1d6|noform|noparens|avg` (`1d6`) and subtract the number rolled from their attack rolls and spell save DC until the start of their next turn. Anyone who paid a secret hears Raum taunting them about it in the crowsong and must roll `dice:1d10|noform|noparens|avg` (`1d10`) instead.

##### Shadows of the Lost

Shadowy, insubstantial figures rise out of the ground in this Junction—screaming images of all the people that The Harvest Terror spirited away into the Fields of the Crow. At the start of each of their turns, a character must succeed on a DC 13 Wisdom saving throw or take `dice:2d10|noform|noparens|avg|text(11)` (`2d10`) Psychic damage. Those who have paid a secret to enter the maze have <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Disadvantage</span> on this saving throw.

## The Wicked Harvest

[The Harvest Terror](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/npc/the-harvest-terror-crookedmoon24.md) lurks at the heart of the cornfield, weaving the secrets given to him by his crow messengers into fiendish straw, which he uses to stuff his fiendish scarecrow body. Use the Circle of Secrets map for this battle.

### Demonic Influence

On <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Initiative</span> count 20 (losing <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Initiative</span> ties), the demonic influence suffusing the Fields of the Crow causes one of the following effects; it can't use the same effect two rounds in a row:

#### Glean Secrets

The fields steal fragments of a victim's secrets and whisper them to Raum. Wisdom Saving Throw: DC 15, one creature in the clearing. Failure: The target's attack rolls against The Harvest Terror have <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Disadvantage</span> until <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Initiative</span> count 20 on the following turn.

#### Stalking Corn

Demonic corn snakes around an intruder. Strength Saving Throw: DC 15, up to three creatures in the clearing. Failure: The target's <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Speed</span> is reduced to 0 until <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Initiative</span> count 20 on the following round.

### Phase 1: A Feast for Crows

![Map 6.03: Circle of Secrets](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheGiddyLimit/homebrew-img/refs/heads/main/img/CrookedMoon24/Adventure/161-map-06.03-circle-of-secrets.webp#center)

In phase one of the fight, Raum conjures thousands of demonic crows to his side to gather the full power of his hoarded secrets over the course of three rounds. Read or paraphrase the following when the characters enter this arena:

> [!readaloud] 
> 
> You break free from the maze's twisting paths and step into a large crop circle where the corn is carved into arcane sigils and demonic eyes. At the center stands a scarecrow—burlap head, straw hat, and a rusty cage for a chest. Slowly, it swivels toward you, and in a trembling, terrified whisper, it says, "Virgil is coming! Too late for me. No, gosh, no... he's here! You gotta get out of—!"
> 
> The scarecrow's body erupts into a great abomination of black feathers, twisted metal, and ragged burlap, a horrific demon that lets out a high pitched screech followed by rapid clicking. A guttural voice crows out, "Feed me your secrets, your flesh, your souls, your power!" Raum, The Harvest Terror, spreads four black wings and lunges forward with its rusted claw and scythe appendages raised high toward the fiendish harvest moon above.
^2af

The desperate cries are the last vestiges of Ol' Jericho Sticks, still trapped deep within Raum, [The Harvest Terror](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/npc/the-harvest-terror-crookedmoon24.md). Roll Initiative! This first phase of the battle is to stop The Harvest Terror from devouring secrets in the form of the amber-eyed crows, limiting his power. Three crows that break off from the mass are [Corvodaemons](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/fiend/corvodaemon-crookedmoon24.md). The demons seek to shred the intruders, prioritizing anyone attacking or catching amber-eyed crows (see Growing Murder below). The demons have the following special considerations:

- The demons lack the Multiattack action.  
- If a corvodaemon takes any damage, it vanishes in a swirl of feathers and black mist. It reforms in an unoccupied space of The Harvest Terror's choice on <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Initiative</span> count 0 (losing <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Initiative</span> ties).  

#### Growing Murder

A dense murder of amber crows surrounds The Harvest Terror in a 5-foot <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Emanation</span> with the following effects:

- A creature other than a demon has <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Disadvantage</span> on attack rolls while in this area.  
- A creature that ends its turn in the area must succeed on a DC 13 Strength saving throw or have the Prone condition.  

#### Devourer of Secrets

While the murder of crows persists, The Harvest Terror can take a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Bonus Action</span> to catch and devour one of the amber-eyed crows. Each time it does, the following occurs:

- The Harvest Terror gains 10 <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Temporary Hit Points</span>. Each crow beyond the first increases this number by 10.  
- The <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Emanation</span> of crows increases by 5 feet.  

The characters can stop Raum from devouring crows in two ways:

##### Catching

A character can catch a crow with an <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Unarmed Strike</span> to grapple it, catch it in a Net, or any other method that you deem acceptable. To grab a crow by hand, a character must enter the murder.

##### Destroying

An amber-eyed crow is a Tiny Fiend with AC 14, HP 5, and +0 to any <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">D20 Tests</span> it is forced to make. It automatically succeeds on Dexterity saving throws against areas of effect and takes no damage from such effects that would deal half damage on a successful save.

Each time a crow is caught or destroyed, it vanishes in a puff of amber motes, and the characters hear a whispered secret, such as "I always loved you," "I took it," or something similar, with no indication of who the secret belonged to.

#### Ending the Phase

When The Harvest Terror devours three amber-eyed crows, or when six crows are destroyed or captured, the swarming murder explodes into feathers and its effects end. Any remaining corvodaemons have 20 <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Hit Points</span> and no longer vanish when they take damage. Move on to the next phase, Demon of Secrets, below.

### Phase 2: Demon of Secrets

Fully empowered by secrets, Raum seeks to feast on the interlopers' flesh and souls.

#### Foxwillow's Tender Secrets

Not all secrets are sinister. If the characters have discovered any of the tender secrets of the Foxwillowers' love for Ol' Jericho Sticks while investigating (see Optional Clues earlier in this chapter), read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud] 
> 
> A memory flares in your mind of a joyful secret that the people of Foxwillow kept for Ol' Jericho Sticks. As the memory fades, an amber strand of straw winds around your wrist, radiating protective warmth.

When The Harvest Terror reduces a character to 0 <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Hit Points</span>, the amber strand shatters, immediately restoring half of that character's maximum <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Hit Points</span>. Each character can benefit from this effect only once.

#### The Heroes' Dark Secrets

Those who paid their own secret for entrance to the Fields of the Crow have granted Raum special power over them. The Harvest Terror gains one additional use of Legendary Resistance for each secret that was paid by the characters (not by NPCs such as Jonah) to enter the Fields of the Crow.

## Conclusion: Out of the Maze

![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheGiddyLimit/homebrew-img/refs/heads/main/img/CrookedMoon24/Adventure/163-06-017.shows-over.webp#center)

The Fields of the Crow have been banished from Druskenvald, and now the corn can be harvested as usual. After a few nights of uneasy rest, the people of Foxwillow return to their work, sending shipments to Wickermoor as though nothing had happened. But something did happen, and it cannot be forgotten.

![Thread of Pilgrimage](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheGiddyLimit/homebrew-img/refs/heads/main/img/CrookedMoon24/Adventure/057-02-039.thread-of-pilgrimage.webp#center)

Martha's youngest, Rosemary, is among the saved, as is Jonah's daughter Liza—if her scarecrow wasn't lost to the flames. Regardless of Adelaide's fate in the maze, she never returns from the Fields of the Crow. Martha grieves for her two eldest but finds solace in their vengeance and the souls that were spared.

William thanks the characters for their help and sends them to Wickermoor Village with a letter for Jeremiah Stover, detailing their heroic deed. Before they leave, he offers to host a feast in their honor, sharing the crops they saved. Whether they stay or move on is their choice.

If the characters ever return to Foxwillow or meet a familiar face at the market in Wickermoor Village, they'll find the town's dark secrets have been laid bare. The people, haunted by their ordeal, have shed tears, and those bonds unbroken by secrets are now stronger, forged in shared suffering.

### Back in Wickermoor Village

Jeremiah Stover cries the news of the victory in Foxwillow after reading William's letter, easily gathering the reward owed to the characters. Whether or not Phillip had previously offered, upon hearing the news, he matches the reward from his own purse.

Sister Rain arrives in Wickermoor Village, seeking members of the Fold of the Patient Lady to help in Foxwillow. Jonah Sawyer's curse still hangs over the land, and Sister Rain hopes to bring the Patient Lady's blessings to heal the withered crops and livestock. She extends an invitation to the characters, believing they can assist.

Adela Druskenvald approaches with a painting from Gaston Doray, hauntingly similar to Raum's demonic realm. She reveals it came from a prophetic vision and hands them a second vision-inspired painting: a swarm of bats in the mountains beneath a blood-red moon.
