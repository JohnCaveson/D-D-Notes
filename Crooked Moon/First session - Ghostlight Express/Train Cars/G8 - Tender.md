### Firebox
- An iron furnace sits at the far end of the car. It is heated by a [[Soulfire Stoker]]
	- The heavy iron door can not be opened without a special key possessed by the Vagrant.

### Blazing Battle
- Roll for Initiative!
	- The stoker only has 30 HP
	- Only makes one Sear attack when using its Multiattack action
	- Follows the characters relentlessly
- Corrupted Fuel
	- The raging ghastly flame within the corrupted firebox is made more oppressive for each haint that was destroyed instead of saved!
	- On initiative count 20 losing ties, characters withoutResistance or Immunity to Fire damage must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or gain 1 Exhaustion level.
		- Saving throw is 5 + 2 for each haint that was destroyed on the train.
