# [Gilly Jenkin](3-Mechanics\CLI\bestiary\npc/gilly-jenkin-crookedmoon24.md)
*Source: The Crooked Moon (2024) p. 219*  

Gilly Jenkin has known [Walter](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/npc/walter-jenkin-crookedmoon24.md) since they were children, and they have loved each other just as long. She is not as devoted to the Druskenvalds as her husband; her concerns lie more with their daughter. She was pregnant with [Dani](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/npc/dani-jenkin-crookedmoon24.md) when they first became employed by [Mayor Somerton](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/npc/wendel-somerton-crookedmoon24.md) over a decade ago, and she is overly protective of the girl. Gilly feels the presence of the strange forces in Wickermoor Village, and she fears her daughter Dani has some sort of strong connection to these powers.
