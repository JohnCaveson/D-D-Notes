# The Jinxed Leviathan

## Regional Effects
_Source: The Crooked Moon (2024)_

The region containing the Jinxed Leviathan's lair is changed by its presence, creating the following effects:

- **Hanging Hex.** Within 1 mile of the lair, when a creature other than the Jinxed Leviathan rolls a 1 or a 20 on a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">D20 Test</span>, the next <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">D20 Test</span> the creature makes has <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Disadvantage</span>, and it can't have <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Advantage</span>.  
- **Murky Waters.** Within 1 mile of the lair, swamp water is cloudy and shows fleeting faces in the murk. Creatures in the water have <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Advantage</span> on Dexterity (Stealth) checks.  

If the Jinxed Leviathan dies or moves its lair elsewhere, these effects end immediately.
