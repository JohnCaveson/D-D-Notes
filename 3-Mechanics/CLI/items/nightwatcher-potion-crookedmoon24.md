# Nightwatcher Potion
*Potion, rare*  
![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheGiddyLimit/homebrew-img/refs/heads/main/img/CrookedMoon24/Items/Nightwatcher%20Potion.webp#right)


When you drink this potion, you have Darkvision out to 120 feet for the next 24 hours. During that time, you don't need to sleep, and if you take a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Long Rest</span>, you can remain conscious throughout.

This clay bottle with a waxed stopper is painted with the head of a great horned owl, and the liquid within is midnight blue

## Heightened Potency

The duration increases to 72 hours.

*Source: The Crooked Moon (2024) p. 616*
