# Devil's Due
*1st-level, Necromancy*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Reaction
- **Range:** 30 feet
- **Components:** S, M (a glob of pitch)
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You absorb a measure of a creature's suffering and escaping vital essence. Expend and roll one <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Hit Points Die</span>. You regain a number of <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Hit Points</span> equal to the number rolled plus your spellcasting ability modifier.

If the creature took damage from a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Critical Hit</span>, double the number of <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Hit Points</span> you regain.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** You can expend one additional <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Hit Points Die</span> for each spell slot level above 1.
