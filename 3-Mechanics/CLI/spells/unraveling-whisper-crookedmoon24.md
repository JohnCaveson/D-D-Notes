# Unraveling Whisper
*2nd-level, Enchantment*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 30 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** 1 round

Choose a creature you can see within range and whisper a frightful phrase that only the target can hear. The target must make a Wisdom saving throw. On a failed save, the target takes `dice:2d6|noform|noparens|avg` (`2d6`) Psychic damage and has the Frightened condition until the start of your next turn. On a successful save, the target takes half as much damage only.

While Frightened in this way, the creature has <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Vulnerability</span> to your choice of Bludgeoning, Piercing, or Slashing damage.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** You can target one additional creature for each spell slot level above 2.
