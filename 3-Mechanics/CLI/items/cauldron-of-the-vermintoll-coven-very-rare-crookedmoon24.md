# Cauldron of the Vermintoll Coven (Very Rare)
*Wondrous item, very rare (requires attunement)*  
![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheGiddyLimit/homebrew-img/refs/heads/main/img/CrookedMoon24/Items/Cauldron%20of%20the%20Vermintoll%20Coven.webp#right)

- **Properties**: [Fabled Heirloom](/3-Mechanics/CLI/item-properties.md#Fabled%20Heirloom)
- **Weight**: 1.0 lbs.

The tarnished cauldron bubbles with a strange, dark liquid. Even in transport, the liquid stays within the rune-emblazoned rim of the cauldron.

While you hold the cauldron, you can use it as a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Spellcasting Focus</span> for your spells, you gain a +2 bonus determined by the cauldron's rarity to all saving throws, and you can use the following properties.

## Size Change (Rare+)

You can perform a 1-minute ritual to change the cauldron's size from Tiny to Medium, or from Medium to Tiny. It weighs 1 lb. when Tiny and 80 lb. when Medium. As a Medium object, its feet can move. It has a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Speed</span> of 20 feet and moves when you command it during your turn (no action required).

## Cookpot (Rare+)

As part of a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Short</span> or <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Long Rest</span>, you can use the Medium cauldron to prepare food for up to ten creatures. At the end of the rest, each creature that eats gains `dice:2d6|noform|noparens|avg` (`2d6`) <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Temporary Hit Points</span>.

## Vermincall (Rare+)

As a Magic action, you can spill liquid from the cauldron to cast Conjure Animals with it. The conjured spirits take the form of grotesque insects or rats. Once this property is used, it can't be used again until the next dusk.

## Fortifying Gorge (Very Rare+)

When you use the Cookpot property, one creature of your choice who eats gains a +2 bonus to AC until you finish a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Short</span> or <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Long Rest</span>. During this time, the creature's skin is hardened and monstrous.

*Source: The Crooked Moon (2024) p. 597*
