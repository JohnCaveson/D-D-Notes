# Cutlass of Briggsy Kratch
*Weapon (scimitar) (requires attunement)*  
![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheGiddyLimit/homebrew-img/refs/heads/main/img/CrookedMoon24/Items/Cutlass%20of%20Briggsy%20Kratch.webp#right)

- **Properties**: [Fabled Heirloom](/3-Mechanics/CLI/item-properties.md#Fabled%20Heirloom)
- **Weight**: 3.0 lbs.

A fearsome weapon imbued with jinxcraft magic, this cutlass was once wielded by the notorious pirate captain, Briggsy "The Kutlass" Kratch. Its tarnished blade glimmers with a faint, otherworldly light. A skull and crossbones adorn the leather-wrapped hilt, and arcane symbols etched into the blade pulse with hidden power.

You gain a bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with the cutlass determined by its rarity.
