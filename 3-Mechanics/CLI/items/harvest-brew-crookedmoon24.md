# Harvest Brew
*Potion, rare*  
![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheGiddyLimit/homebrew-img/refs/heads/main/img/CrookedMoon24/Items/Harvest%20Brew.webp#right)


When you drink this potion, you are filled with good health and bounty, providing you with enough nourishment to sustain you for 1 day. For the next hour, when you regain <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Hit Points</span>, you regain additional <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Hit Points</span> equal to your <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Proficiency Bonus</span>.

The potion's bottle is made from a hollowed-out gourd, and the thick, liquid inside is a deep orange that tastes of nutmeg and cardamom.

## Heightened Potency

The additional <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Hit Points</span> you regain are equal to half your level rather than your <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Proficiency Bonus</span> (minimum +2).

*Source: The Crooked Moon (2024) p. 615*
