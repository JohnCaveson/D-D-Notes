# Mysterious Presence
*cantrip, Illusion*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

You touch a willing creature and shroud its presence. Until the spell ends, other creatures must subtract `dice:1d4|noform|noparens|avg` (`1d4`) from any Wisdom (Insight or Perception) checks made against the target.

**Classes**: [Bard (Magical Secrets)](/3-Mechanics/CLI/lists/list-spells-classes-bard.md)

*Source: The Crooked Moon (2024) p. 131*
