# Bogborn
*Source: The Crooked Moon (2024) p. 55*  
![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheGiddyLimit/homebrew-img/refs/heads/main/img/CrookedMoon24/Races/Bogborn.webp#right)

- **Ability Scores**: None
- **Type**: giant
- **Size**: Medium
- **Speed**: 30 ft.

## Traits

### Bog Bulk

You have <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Advantage</span> on any ability check you make to end the Grappled condition.

You also count as one size larger when determining your carrying capacity.

### Darkvision

You have Darkvision with a range of 60 feet.

### Guiding Bond

As a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Bonus Action</span>, you can magically place a mark on a creature you can see within 60 feet of yourself.

The mark lasts for 1 minute, and takes the form of ephemeral plants, moss, fungus, or swamp animals that only you can see. For the duration, you can add `dice:1d4|noform|noparens|avg` (`1d4`) to ability checks and attack rolls you make against the marked creature. You can use this <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Bonus Action</span> a number of times equal to your <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Proficiency Bonus</span>, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Long Rest</span>.

### Keen Senses

You have proficiency in the Perception or Survival skill.

### Regeneration

As a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Bonus Action</span>, you can expend one of your <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Hit Point Dice</span>, roll the die, and regain a number of <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Hit Points</span> equal to the number rolled plus your Constitution modifier (minimum of 1 <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Hit Point</span>).

If you take Acid or Fire damage, this trait doesn't function until the end of your next turn.

## Description

Bogborn are a troll-like species native to "Zulrogg", usually rising from the still, dark waters of the swampy province. From the moment of rebirth, they have a symbiotic relationship with flora and fauna of the wild—and often treacherous—mire. Their ability to survive is further enhanced by the naturally regenerative biology often found in trolls.

Towering over most, these hearty folk can face any trial of nature, and they often utilize their athleticism in pursuits of hunting and survival. Bearing prominent noses and tusks, their tough hides are generally shades of green or blue. Bogborn typically live 100 years before moving on to the spirit world.
