# Cane of Phillip Druskenvald
*(Requires attunement)*  
![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheGiddyLimit/homebrew-img/refs/heads/main/img/CrookedMoon24/Items/Cane%20of%20Phillip%20Druskenvald.webp#right)

- **Properties**: [Fabled Heirloom](/3-Mechanics/CLI/item-properties.md#Fabled%20Heirloom)

This ornate cane is made from polished black wood and is topped with a silver goat-skull ornament. Even a glance at it sparks greed and envy in all but the truly humble.

While holding the cane, you can use it as a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Spellcasting Focus</span> for your spells, you gain a bonus to spell attack rolls and your spell save DC determined by the cane's rarity.
