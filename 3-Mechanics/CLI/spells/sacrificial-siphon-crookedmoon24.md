# Sacrificial Siphon
*4th-level, Necromancy*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** S
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

You touch another creature and lay a curse that drains the target's vitality and transfers it to you. The target must make a Charisma saving throw. On a failed save, the target is cursed for the duration. While cursed, the target must subtract `dice:1d4|noform|noparens|avg` (`1d4`) from its attack rolls and ability checks, and when the target takes damage, you can take a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Reaction</span> to gain 10 <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Temporary Hit Points</span>. On a successful save, you gain 5 <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Temporary Hit Points</span>, and the target must subtract `dice:1d4|noform|noparens|avg` (`1d4`) from its ability checks until the start of your next turn.
