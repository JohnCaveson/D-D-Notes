# Maidenmist Cemetery
*Source: The Crooked Moon Part Two: Monsters & Adventure Campaign, p. 367* 

![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheGiddyLimit/homebrew-img/refs/heads/main/img/CrookedMoon24/Adventure/245-12-001.maidenmist-cemetery-opener.webp#center)

> [!quote] A quote from Yorgrim, the Gravedigger  
> 
> My friends... I am but one light in the darkness. One soul against the crushing weight of the hate and hunger of death. It is happening. It is inevitable. I will break, and so will the chains around the shrieking dead. My friends... Forgive me... I have failed...

The gates of Maidenmist Cemetery have burst open, and the raging dead rise from their graves to escape. [The Chained Reaper](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/npc/the-chained-reaper-crookedmoon24.md), the necropolis's caretaker, stands alone against a rising tide of undeath, slowly corrupted by the incessant whispers of the malicious, ravening spirits he has sworn to entomb and imprison. Unless aided by the characters, the jailer will break, and a wave of shrieking spirits will crash upon Wickermoor Hollow and devour every living soul within.

## Chapter Background

Maidenmist Cemetery sprawls across the fogbound, rolling hills to the southeast of Wickermoor Village, riddled with headstones, crypts, mausoleums, and every other kind of grave marker. It serves as the final resting place for all mortal beings in Wickermoor Hollow and has done so for millennia. None know who first built the cemetery, for it has always been there. It is said that the wrought iron fence that surrounds it inches forward night by night, forever welcoming the endlessly increasing deceased.

For years, a ghostly orc known as Yorgrim has served as the sole gravedigger and sexton of the cemetery. However, he is not just a caretaker for the dead that he buries and for whom he performs the final rites. He also serves as a warden, for the cemetery's true purpose is a prison for thousands of screaming spirits that seek only to corrupt and consume the souls of both the living and the dead.

The cemetery's secret is that it was built upon a tear into the Shroud, which normally serves as an impenetrable barrier around Druskenvald, but here it serves as a gateway for the twisted spirits to cross over into the realm freely. These souls are filled with inhuman wrath for being deprived of a true afterlife; left unchecked, they would spill across the valley and the entire domain beyond, feasting upon the living.

## Chapter Summary

The haunted hills of Maidenmist Cemetery have never been safe for the living, but the warding lights of the necropolis have gone out, and their caretaker slowly succumbs to the corruption of the Shroud. Now, the cemetery is a prison overrun with rioting dead. Only the relighting of the balefire beacons and the sealing of the tear into the Shroud can stop the coming tide of death. Characters who are drawn to Maidenmist Cemetery by the Quest Hook later in this chapter have several goals:

- Find Yorgrim, the Gravedigger.  
- Light the five extinguished balefire beacons.  
- Discover the source of the dead's corruption within the sepulcher.  
- Destroy [the Chained Reaper](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/npc/the-chained-reaper-crookedmoon24.md) and seal the tear to the Shroud.  

Before running this chapter, familiarize yourself with Yorgrim, the key NPC, to convincingly convey his grim duty and descent into corruption. Refresh yourself on the stories of the other Fallen (see chapter The Crooked Moon) to provide the characters with inspiration in performing their rites. Review the locations of Maidenmist Cemetery so you are prepared to improvise encounters as the characters explore.

This chapter is designed for four level 9 characters.

## The Gravedigger

Yorgrim, an orcish spirit bound to an eternity of penance, is the dour sexton that oversees Maidenmist Cemetery, hunched beneath the crushing weight of his duty and the heavy stone chained to his back. Haunted by the failures of his past and the loss of his friends to their respective sins, he goes about his duties with grim determination, never relenting despite the constant cracking of his soul and the chains that bind the spirits he keeps imprisoned.

### The Penitent Pilgrim

Before coming to Druskenvald, Yorgrim was a seer for his people, the orcs of the Shadestone Clan. He communed with the spirits of the deceased, performing their burial rites and guiding them to the afterlife. However, he was deceived by his chieftain and unwittingly performed a necromantic ritual that slaughtered his entire community and annihilated their souls with unspeakable and forbidden power.

![Yorgrim the Gravedigger and Yorgrim the Seer](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheGiddyLimit/homebrew-img/refs/heads/main/img/CrookedMoon24/Adventure/246-12-002.yorgrim-and-yorgrim.webp#center)

Yorgrim took his vengeance, but it was a hollow victory. His people were dead, and their souls would never find peace. As a self-imposed penance, the seer etched the name of every fallen orc onto a heavy stone that he chained to himself, committing to carrying the weight forever as he sought to wander the land and bring peace to souls that were not yet beyond help.

For years, Yorgrim trudged across the land, a dour pilgrim performing burial rites for countless souls until his body finally gave out beneath the crushing weight of his penance. However, his spirit did not yield, and he arrived in Druskenvald not reborn but instead fated to forever exist as a ghost, still chained to the heavy gravestone even in death.

In this new realm, with so many restless dead in need of peace, Yorgrim wandered Druskenvald, performing rites alone until he finally made connections with five other souls as powerful and burdened as his. Despite his gloomy disposition, Yorgrim developed an affection for and eventual friendship with Ol' Jericho Sticks, Sir Marius Renathyr, Mister Crossroads, Matron Lethica, and Farryn of the Greenwood, regularly visiting each throughout his wayward travels.

### The Shroud Warden

Shortly after meeting his new friends and softening his stony heart, he discovered the mysterious Maidenmist Cemetery in Wickermoor Hollow. Deep within the sepulcher at the center of the necropolis was a small tear into the primordial mists of death called the Shroud (see chapter "Druskenvald" in "The Crooked Moon Part One"), and the shrieks and whispers of the tortured souls within concerned Yorgrim deeply. He feared an apocalyptic reckoning should the shrouded abominations freely pass into Druskenvald.

Feeling as if he had discovered his true purpose, he bound himself to the cemetery and took up the mantle of its sexton and gravedigger, performing the burial rites for the thousands of dead he found there. His duty was to provide this service for every new death in Wickermoor Hollow. This prevented the spirits of the Shroud from slipping through to possess and reanimate the corpses that lay within the earth of the necropolis.

However, as the years went on, the tear to the Shroud grew larger, and Yorgrim beseeched his friends for aid. All agreed, and each of the six unlikely companions offered a fragment of their soul to kindle great braziers of spectral balefire, the light of which repelled the Shroud and the abominable spirits within.

After parting ways, Yorgrim would never see his friends again; the lighting of the beacons bound him to the cemetery as a jailer and warden of the shrieking spirits and occasionally restless dead within. In his duty, he took up a lantern tethered to the anima of his soul that serves as his primary weapon and tool to keep the unruly spirits chained.

As each of his former friends fell one by one to the sin of [The Horned King](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/npc/the-horned-king-crookedmoon24.md), the balefire beacons dimmed greatly, and Yorgrim's nightly duties became far more challenging. When the Ghostlight Express crashed into Wickermoor Hollow, bringing the ghastly fog of the Shroud with it, the tear in the sepulcher grew immensely. And following the deaths of each of the Fallen, their respective balefire beacons have gone completely dark. Now, Yorgrim expends every measure of his being to keep the riotous and raging dead from spilling forth, his own soul weakening with every passing night.

### Parley with the Gravedigger

Yorgrim is dour, grim, and stoic, but he is not cruel. He views the characters as the only hope to relight the balefire beacons and investigate the source of the Shroud. He is not talkative and speaks with few words but doesn't withhold important information that the characters need to know.

These are some responses to common questions the characters may ask of this spectral sextant:

#### Who Are You

"I am called Yorgrim. This is my cemetery. I serve the dead here."

#### What Is this Place

"The graves of Maidenmist bear the dead of the hollow. Centuries of souls rest here. It was here before me. It will be here after me."

#### Are You Dead

"My life ended long ago, but my duty did not. It persists until I atone for my sins and failures. This is my burden to bear."

#### Who Is the Dog

"This is Isolde. She is a grim, a spirit bound in duty to the dead. We are of a kind. She is my sole companion."

#### What Is the Shroud

"The Shroud is an element. Like the air you breathe and the earth you stand upon. It is death incarnate. It should not be here. Not even in an afterlife."

#### What Are the Shrouded

"Souls once living but devoured and corrupted by the Shroud. They have lost everything they once were in life. Abominations."

#### You Were Friends

"The monsters you killed were my friends. Before they succumbed to corruption, they had strong wills and good hearts, like you. They fell to their burdens, like you might yet. None are immune to the weight of failure."

#### Why Would We Perform Rites for Villains

"We will not perform the rites for the evil you faced. We do not mourn [The Crimson Abbot](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/npc/the-crimson-abbot-crookedmoon24.md), [The Grinning Sinner](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/npc/the-grinning-sinner-crookedmoon24.md), or the rest. I mourn who they were when there was light in their souls. It is for them we perform the rites. It is the only way."

### Isolde, the Grim

![Isolde](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheGiddyLimit/homebrew-img/refs/heads/main/img/CrookedMoon24/Adventure/247-12-003.isolde.webp#center)

After isolating himself, Yorgrim found companionship in Isolde (ih-ZOAL-duh), a type of guardian spirit known as a grim. These spectral watchers guard cemeteries, graveyards, and other burial sites and can take the form of any animal. Isolde (Neutral Good Ghost) manifests as a black hound with bright white eyes. She is deeply loyal to Yorgrim, and carries in her mouth a Hooded Lantern lit with a spark of the gravedigger's soul.

While Isolde is similarly bound to Maidenmist Cemetery like her master, she is able to exit its cold iron gates and walk beyond its borders. In recent years, she has served as the primary messenger between Yorgrim and Mori Shade, the undertaker of Wickermoor Village. With a supernatural intuition that only a dog can possess, she can sense that Yorgrim is suffering from the gibbering of the Shroud and is close to breaking.

### The Shrouded

The shrouded are malicious, ravenous spirits entirely stripped of their anima—the soul-stuff that retains an individual spirit's essence and identity. Having lost whoever they might have been in life, the shrouded exist as devouring forces of the primordial element of death itself, filled only with the hunger to corrupt and consume souls that retain their anima, whether they be living or dead. They only feel rage and hate for those who don't share their fate, forever drifting through the Shroud and seeking new prey to corrupt into shrouded themselves.

In Maidenmist Cemetery, numerous shrouded have spilled forth from the tear in the depths of the great sepulcher, and some have even slithered out between the cracks of the ancient tomb. They drift through the ghastly rolling fogbanks or possess the corpses once interred in the earth but now shamble about the necropolis.

## Quest Hook

Throughout their time in Wickermoor Hollow, the characters may have heard tales of Maidenmist Cemetery and perhaps even attempted to visit if their curiosity was piqued by the mysterious, haunted place. Until now, they would have found the gates closed and heavily chained, only spying the distant flicker of Yorgrim's lantern as he dug a grave or tended to the grounds. Now, the rising tide of the shrouded has smashed the gates open and undeath threatens to spill into Wickermoor Village and the hollow beyond.

### Dusk of the Dead

Late into the Dusk Hour, screams ring out through Wickermoor Village. Eight Ghasts wordlessly shriek as they slaughter any villagers they can find and feast on the slain. Alarm bells toll from the clocktower, and if the characters do not dispatch the Undead, the ghasts are brought down by a rallied militia of armed townsfolk (Guards) led by Constable Doris Squire (Lawful Neutral, human Knight), suffering heavy losses.

After the ghasts are defeated, as an action, a character who succeeds on a DC 10 Intelligence (Investigation) or Wisdom (Medicine) check can deduce that the risen corpses had been dead for varying lengths of time, with the oldest ghast having been dead for centuries and the freshest one only several weeks deceased.

![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheGiddyLimit/homebrew-img/refs/heads/main/img/CrookedMoon24/Adventure/248-12-004.phantom-hearse.webp#center)

Mori Shade (see chapter Wickermoor Village) arrives shortly after the battle and recognizes the most recently deceased corpse as Gregor Bolton, a once-burly workman who died from a fall while repairing the roof of Mayor Somerton's manor. She mentions that she had delivered his body to Maidenmist Cemetery shortly before the characters arrived at Wickermoor Village.

She is deeply concerned by the arrival of Undead creatures in Wickermoor Village, suspecting that something is wrong in Maidenmist Cemetery. She beseeches the characters to investigate to prevent more death and destruction from the walking dead. Use the details below to guide the conversation with Mori:

- The shambling corpses' aggressiveness and shrieking suggests they aren't normal Undead.  
- From Yorgrim, she knows the Shroud can turn spirits and corpses into ravening monsters.  
- She has not heard from Yorgrim or seen Isolde since her delivery of Gregor Bolton's body to the cemetery.  
- A pile of corpses from recent deadly events around town has accumulated near the funeral home.  
- She went to Maidenmist Cemetery a week prior to look for Yorgrim, finding the gates chained and the cemetery covered in a dense fog.  
- There was no sign of the blazing fires or Yorgrim and Isolde's flickering lantern light, and her calls to them went unanswered.  

Mori implores the characters to see if they can find a way into Maidenmist Cemetery and discover what's become of Yorgrim. She then works with the townsfolk to collect the corpses from the battle and the funeral home, piling them on the outskirts of town to burn them, ensuring that they do not rise in undeath. If the characters interrupt the burning, the ghasts rise once more during the Dusk Hour of the following night.

If the characters fail to act, another group of townsfolk volunteers to make the journey to the cemetery. They never return, and can be found shambling among the Endless Gravestones (area Y3) as Zombies. Every night of inaction, the village sees more attacks, the number of Undead in the horde doubling with each passing night.

## Maidenmist Cemetery

Maidenmist Cemetery sprawls across many miles of hilly land in southeast Wickermoor Hollow. It has existed for centuries, its original builders lost to time. Countless dead rest here through every manner of burial and interment, and each of the thousands of grave markers that cover the land is never more than a few feet away from the next.

### Through Cold Iron Gates

It takes approximately half a night's travel southeast at a Normal pace to reach the edge of Maidenmist Cemetery from Wickermoor Village, crossing the Silverbell River by way of an old cobblestone bridge. With each passing mile, the landscape becomes increasingly foggy, and the air grows colder. When the characters arrive at the cemetery, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud] 
> 
> The night is strangely cold and quiet as you arrive at Maidenmist Cemetery. A tall iron fence stretches out on either side of you, with thick, tightly spaced pickets that look like prison bars. The gate, flanked by metal sculptures of hooded reapers, hangs askew, bent as if it had been smashed open from the inside. The landscape before you, dotted with thousands of tombstones, rises in elevation before disappearing into the dense fog that shrouds the hills, wisps of blue-green mist billowing amidst the gray. A faint wail sounds from somewhere in the rolling fog.
^5b5

A 15-foot-tall iron fence encircles the entirety of Maidenmist Cemetery, its base heavily reinforced with stone. A Tiny creature can pass through the fence's narrow pickets.

As a Study action, a character who examines the bent gate and succeeds on a DC 13 Intelligence (Investigation) check finds a 50-foot Chain in the damp earth, along with several broken links, as if it snapped from a single violent thrust against the inside of the gate.

A cobblestone path leads into the cemetery up the hill, but it disappears into the fog. The blue-green mist is reminiscent of the Shroud, which the characters experienced during their trip aboard the *Ghostlight Express*.

### Maidenmist Cemetery Features

Unless otherwise noted, Maidenmist Cemetery has the following features:

#### Ghastly Mist

Eerie, billowing mist rolls across the landscape. The entire cemetery is <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Lightly Obscured</span> out to 60 feet, and <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Heavily Obscured</span> beyond that distance.

#### Lighting

The light of the Crooked Moon is caught and refracted in the mist, bathing the cemetery in <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Dim Light</span>.

#### Shrouded Spirits

Corrupted spirits lurk in the mist, searching for souls to devour. Every hour that the characters travel through the mist, three [Shrouded](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/undead/shrouded-crookedmoon24.md) (one of each variant) attack until they are destroyed.

#### Wails of the Damned

The mist of the cemetery is interwoven with the Shroud, and the shrieking souls within wail incessantly. Constitution saving throws made to maintain Concentration have <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Disadvantage</span>.

#### Corrupting Shroud

If a creature that isn't a shrouded dies in the cemetery, it is corrupted by the Shroud and rises 10 minutes later, <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Hostile</span> toward all creatures that aren't Undead. A Humanoid rises as an Undead, as determined on the Restless Dead table below. An Undead creature rises as a [Shrouded](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/undead/shrouded-crookedmoon24.md) (random variant).

### The Phantom Gravedigger

While [The Chained Reaper](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/npc/the-chained-reaper-crookedmoon24.md) clings to his identity and duty, the weight of his penance and the insurmountable struggle against the tide of undeath has taken a toll on the gravedigger. His spirit is weak, tired, and fated to become The Chained Reaper. Before this transformation takes place in the Tomb of the Shrouded (area Y15), his shade can barely interact with the physical world, with most of his remaining power focused in the spectral lantern he always carries.

Therefore, when Yorgrim (found in area Y2) joins the characters in Maidenmist Cemetery, he doesn't use a stat block, has <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Immunity</span> to all damage, and can't be affected by spells or other magical effects. His size is Medium, and he has a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Speed</span> of 30 feet. He can move through other creatures and can share a space with them.

#### Balefire Lantern

[The Chained Reaper](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/npc/the-chained-reaper-crookedmoon24.md) carries a spectral azure lantern that is as incorporeal as he is, which sheds <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Bright Light</span> in a 30-foot radius and <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Dim Light</span> for an additional 30 feet. Once all six balefire beacons are lit, the lantern dims, shedding <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Bright Light</span> in a 5-foot radius and <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Dim Light</span> for an additional 15 feet.

The lantern has the following features:

##### Azure Protection

Creatures in the lantern's <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Bright Light</span> are immune to the Shrouded Spirits and Corrupting Shroud features (see "Maidenmist Cemetery Features" earlier in this chapter) of the cemetery's mist.

- **Balefire Warding.** All [Shrouded](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/undead/shrouded-crookedmoon24.md) cannot enter the lantern's <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Bright Light</span>. Shrouded in the lantern's light have <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Disadvantage</span> on <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">D20 Tests</span>.  

#### Gravedigger Actions

While [The Chained Reaper](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/npc/the-chained-reaper-crookedmoon24.md) is generally unable to interact with the physical world, when the characters are in danger, he summons up what little strength he has left to help.

While carrying the balefire lantern, on <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Initiative</span> count 20 (losing <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Initiative</span> ties), Yorgrim causes one of the following effects; he can't use the same effect two rounds in a row:

##### Balefire Burst

Yorgrim's lantern erupts with balefire. Each creature of Yorgrim's choice within the lantern's <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Bright Light</span> must make a DC 15 Dexterity saving throw, taking `dice:1d8|noform|noparens|avg|text(4)` (`1d8`) Fire damage and `dice:1d8|noform|noparens|avg|text(4)` (`1d8`) Necrotic damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.

- **Chained Phantom.** Yorgrim hurls a spectral chain to bind a foe. One creature Yorgrim can see within the lantern's light must succeed on a DC 15 Strength saving throw or have the Restrained condition. The target repeats the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on a success. After 1 minute, it succeeds automatically.  

##### Garish Light

Yorgrim unleashes a blinding flare from his lantern. Each creature of Yorgrim's choice within the lantern's <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Bright Light</span> must succeed on a DC 15 Constitution saving throw or have the Blinded condition until <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Initiative</span> count 20 on the following round.

##### Grave Resilience

Yorgrim's lantern imparts vitality. Each creature of Yorgrim's choice within the lantern's light gains `dice:2d12|noform|noparens|avg|text(13)` (`2d12`) <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Temporary Hit Points</span>.

#### Balefire Beacons

Six beacons of balefire, each with its own hue, are meant to roar in braziers around the cemetery to ward it from the encroaching Shroud. Five have gone dark and must be reignited to banish the eldritch fog and the vicious spirits within from all outdoor locations of Maidenmist Cemetery. This is the only way to open the doors to the Great Sepulcher (area Y2) so that the characters and Yorgrim can investigate the growing tear in the Shroud deep beneath the cemetery.

The balefire beacons have gone out following the deaths of the Fallen, the powerful beings whose souls were used to spark the flames. To reignite these beacons, the characters must travel across the cemetery to assist Yorgrim in performing each of the Fallen's final rites at their flame's respective brazier. The beacons, their locations, and the Fallen whose souls they were sparked from are as follows:

##### Crimson Beacon

Flame of Sir Marius Renathyr (see chapter The Crimson Monastery), found in the Highborn Crypts (area Y4).

##### Violet Beacon

Flame of Lethica Nightborne (see chapter Memory's Rest), found in the Cremation Gardens (area Y5).

##### Amber Beacon

Flame of Ol' Jericho Sticks (see chapter Fields of the Crow), found in the Potter's Field (area Y6).

##### Magenta Beacon

Flame of Captain Briggsy Kratch (see chapter The Drowned Crossroads), found in the Mausoleum Lanes (area Y7).

##### Emerald Beacon

Flame of Farryn of the Greenwood (see chapter Hartsblight Forest), found in the Burial Mounds (area Y8).

##### Azure Beacon

Flame of Yorgrim, found in the Shadestone Sanctuary (area Y9).

The Azure Beacon is the only one that remains ablaze. When each of the remaining five beacons is lit, its corresponding sconce above the doors of the Great Sepulcher (area Y2) and within the Balefire Chamber (area Y11) lights with the same-colored flame. When all six beacons are lit, the sepulcher's doors unseal.

#### Final Rites

To perform the final rites of the Fallen, a 1-minute ritual led by [The Chained Reaper](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/npc/the-chained-reaper-crookedmoon24.md) must be conducted at a balefire brazier. During the ritual, the characters must slay the attacking monsters and succeed on six ability checks. The specific details to complete the ritual are as follows:

##### Spark the Flame

Special kindling connected to the Fallen, kept entombed at the respective cemetery location, must be placed in the brazier and ignited, beginning the ritual. Roll <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Initiative</span>! If 1 minute passes without the beacon becoming fully lit, the flame goes out, and a character can offer a fragment of their soul to restore the kindling, gaining 1 Exhaustion level. The characters can then attempt the ritual again.

##### Sacrifice a Soul

The ignition of the flame draws the attention of <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Hostile</span> cemetery monsters corrupted by the Shroud, committed to preventing the beacon from being lit. These monsters must be slain during the ritual as an offering to the balefire (see "The Beacon is Lit" below).

##### Hold Back the Shroud

The ravenous Shroud is drawn to the ritual, kept at bay with immense effort from Yorgrim. It billows around the fringes of the light cast by Yorgrim's lantern (see "Balefire Lantern" earlier in this chapter), waiting for the light to be snuffed out. Everything outside of the lantern's light is <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Heavily Obscured</span>. Additionally, any character who leaves the light and steps into the Shroud is attacked by three [Shrouded](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/undead/shrouded-crookedmoon24.md) (one of each variant).

![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheGiddyLimit/homebrew-img/refs/heads/main/img/CrookedMoon24/Adventure/250-12-006.yorgrim.webp#center)

##### Honor the Fallen

Once the balefire flame is lit, it burns small and must be stoked. As a Magic action, a character can make a contribution to the ceremony that honors who the Fallen were before they fell. The character makes a DC 15 ability check. Each of the Fallen has a list of suggested "resolution proficiencies" that have the best chance of contributing to the ritual. Other checks can work at your discretion, but the DC for such checks rises to 18 unless you feel the justification is especially fitting.

##### Stoke the Fire

Each successful ability check is one step toward finishing a Fallen's final rites (see "The Beacon is Lit" below). With each failure, tormented souls from the Shroud take on mocking shapes of the Fallen and unleash hateful words to torment the gravedigger and the characters. Each creature that can hear this verbal assault must succeed on a DC 13 Wisdom saving throw or take `dice:1d6|noform|noparens|avg|text(3)` (`1d6`) Psychic damage and gain the Stunned condition until the end of its next turn. A creature can choose to take an extra `dice:6d6|noform|noparens|avg|text(21)` (`6d6`) Psychic damage to avoid the Stunned condition.

##### The Beacon Is Lit

After the destruction of the guardian monsters and six successful ability checks, the ritual concludes and the balefire beacon is fully lit, banishing the Shroud from the cemetery location. All shrouded in the beacon's location are destroyed, and the area loses the effects of the Shrouded Spirits, Wails of the Damned, and Corrupting Shroud features (see "Maidenmist Cemetery Features" earlier in this chapter).

##### Brilliant Success

How well the characters perform the final rites affects the encounter in the Balefire Chamber (area Y11) within the sepulcher. Each ability check during a ritual that succeeds by 5 or more is considered a Brilliant Success. The number of Brilliant Successes achieved during the ritual determines the balefire beacon's intensity, as shown in the Beacon Intensity table below (important in area Y11).

#### Maidenmist Cemetery Locations

The following locations are keyed to the map of Maidenmist Cemetary.

#### Y1: Corpse Road

> [!readaloud] 
> 
> A long road wide enough that two carriages can pass side by side leads toward a huge sepulcher that sits atop the hill at the center of the cemetery. At the base of the great stone structure, you see small lights flickering a pale blue. The sight of the hill is swallowed by the gloom as the fog rolls in, becoming incredibly dense all around you.
^5d5

This 20-foot-wide cobblestone road stretches three miles from the cemetery gates to the Great Sepulcher (area Y2), and it once served as the main pathway for hearses and carriages delivering corpses to be buried. The mist is at its most dense around the gates, with the billowing plumes of the Shroud seeking to be free of the confines of the cemetery. Traveling this road without the protection of balefire is dangerous, and new threats emerge after each mile traveled.

##### First Mile

The shrieking, whispering, and gibbering of the tormented spirits of the Shroud threaten to corrupt the characters' souls and psyche. Any non-Undead in the mist must make a DC 15 Wisdom saving throw, taking `dice:2d6|noform|noparens|avg|text(7)` (`2d6`) Psychic damage and `dice:2d6|noform|noparens|avg|text(7)` (`2d6`) Necrotic damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.

##### Second Mile

The characters draw the attention of malign spirits. Six [Shrouded](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/undead/shrouded-crookedmoon24.md) (two of each variant) attack until they are destroyed. This resets the time of the cemetery's Shrouded Spirits feature (see "Maidenmist Cemetery Features" earlier in the chapter).

##### Third Mile

The oppressiveness of the Shroud becomes overwhelming. Any non-Undead in the mist must succeed on a DC 15 Constitution saving throw or gain 1 Exhaustion level.

#### Y2: Great Sepulcher

![Thread of Duality](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheGiddyLimit/homebrew-img/refs/heads/main/img/CrookedMoon24/Adventure/051-02-033.thread-of-duality.webp#center)

> [!readaloud] 
> 
> You step out of the mist into a flickering blue glow as you arrive at the base of a great sepulcher, seemingly ancient with long thin cracks and dense moss covering its surface. The cobblestone road circles around the structure and splits off at several points that lead further into the cemetery.
> 
> Before the huge double doors are two spectral figures holding lanterns that radiate with ghastly blue light. One is a bearded orcish spirit, hunched low by a tombstone chained to his back. The other is a shadowy hound with bright white lights for eyes. Both shine their lanterns upon the narrow gap between the doors to burn away the snaking tendrils of ghastly gray and blue-green mist that writhe between the seams in hopes of escape.
> 
> Mounted into the stone above the doors are six iron sconces, five of which are unlit. In the sixth sconce on the far right flickers an azure flame that casts the same light as the two lanterns held by the spirits before you.
^5da

The two spirits are [The Chained Reaper](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/npc/the-chained-reaper-crookedmoon24.md) the gravedigger and Isolde the grim (Neutral Good Ghost), who are straining to keep the Shroud from escaping the sepulcher. Yorgrim looks especially hollow and tired, failing to notice the characters' arrival until they announce themselves or Isolde alerts him.

When the characters approach Yorgrim, he is surprised that mortals have made it to the sepulcher alive, but he's relieved that they might be able to set things right in the cemetery and push back the Shroud. Use the details below to guide the conversation with Yorgrim:

- The tear in the Shroud within the sepulcher is growing, as shown by the shrouded trying to escape.  
- The balefire beacons that ward against the Shroud have gone out one by one in recent weeks.  
- Only one beacon remains lit, and its azure color matches his lantern. Both flames are tethered to his soul.  
- His five former friends, similarly tied to the now-unlit beacons, have all been slain.  
- The burial rites for the Fallen must be performed at each of the beacons to relight them.  
- Undead creatures and other monsters corrupted by the Shroud guard the beacons' unlit braziers.  
- Yorgrim is weakened due to battling the spirits but will aid the characters in this task and protect them from the mist.  
- Isolde stays to keep her light on the sepulcher doors. She can only last sixteen hours on her own before her spirit's essence is spent and she fades away.  
- Once all six balefire beacons are lit, the doors to the sepulcher can be opened to investigate the rift.  

##### Ghost of a Chance

Yorgrim joins the characters in their exploration of Maidenmist Cemetery to protect them from the Shroud (see "The Phantom Gravedigger" earlier in this chapter), share what he knows about the Fallen (see chapter *The Crooked Moon*), and assist in performing the burial rites for each of his slain friends so that their respective balefire beacons can be lit.

This is a tremendous strain on his spirit. After each challenge his fortitude lessens, his spirit appears more hollow, and the maddening voices of the shrouded affect him more and more, leading toward his inevitable transformation into [The Chained Reaper](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/npc/the-chained-reaper-crookedmoon24.md) in the Tomb of the Shrouded (area Y15). Once all six balefire beacons are lit, Yorgrim's balefire lantern dims, shedding <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Bright Light</span> in a 5-foot radius and <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Dim Light</span> for an additional 15 feet.

##### Loyal Hound

While Yorgrim and the characters explore Maidenmist Cemetery, Isolde is left alone to defend the doors of the sepulcher against the Shroud. She dutifully devotes herself to keep her balefire lantern lit, slowly draining her spectral essence. After 16 hours, Isolde's spirit is spent and she fades away, allowing the ghastly fog of the Shroud to seep out of the door and coalesce around the sepulcher.

Every 15 minutes after Isolde is gone, a [Shrouded](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/undead/shrouded-crookedmoon24.md) (random variant) escapes its prison and lurks in the gloom, waiting to attack the characters when they return. This effect stops once all six balefire beacons are lit, but the shrouded remain until they are destroyed.

##### Cemetery Paths

Six paved paths lead from the sepulcher to the locations containing balefire beacons, and a seventh leads to King's Rest (area Y10). Each one is 3 miles long.

#### Y3: Endless Gravestones

Between the major locations of Maidenmist Cemetery are vast stretches of hilly terrain covered in numerous tombstones that mark the gravesites of Wickermoor Hollow's dead throughout the centuries.

##### Shortcut

These grave-laden hills can be traversed as a 1-mile shortcut between adjacent outdoor locations of Maidenmist Cemetery (see map of Maidenmist Cemetary) instead of returning along the path back to the Great Sepulcher (area Y2) before moving to the next location.

##### Ravenous Dead

The corruption of the Shroud has turned many once-restful corpses into hordes of shambling Undead creatures that seek only to devour the living. Whenever the characters travel across the Endless Gravestones, they are attacked by a group of these ravening dead before arriving at the next location. Roll on the Ravening Hordes table to determine which horrors rise to attack the characters.

#### Y4: Highborn Crypts

![Thread of Immortality](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheGiddyLimit/homebrew-img/refs/heads/main/img/CrookedMoon24/Adventure/053-02-035.thread-of-immortality.webp#center)

> [!readaloud] 
> 
> The paved pathway splits into various offshoots that lead to small courtyards, around which stand exquisitely crafted crypts topped with leering gargoyles. These structures, built of polished dark stone, are grand and gaudy, with the family names of the dead chiseled above each crypt's door. The air is filled with the sound of flapping wings and chittering as bats take off into the night sky in gyrating swarms.
^5e2

Wealthy and noble families of Wickermoor Hollow are entombed within these imposing crypts. They are arranged around small courtyards and circled with wrought iron fences. [The Chained Reaper](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/npc/the-chained-reaper-crookedmoon24.md) leads the characters to a courtyard that contains a large metal brazier filled with dull red coals—the site where the final rites must be performed for Father Renathyr, The Crimson Abbot (see "Balefire Beacons" earlier in this chapter) to light the Crimson Beacon. For battles in this location, use the highborn crypts map.

##### Blood Tithe

The doors to the crypts are magically sealed and deeply tied to the noble blood of those entombed within. As a Study action, a character who examines a door and succeeds on a DC 15 Intelligence (Arcana or Religion) check deduces that to open the door, fresh blood must be shed into a basin built into the crypt. A creature can shed its own blood, taking `dice:1d12|noform|noparens|avg|text(6)` (`1d12`) Necrotic damage and unsealing the crypt's door.

##### Bat-Filled Crypts

The crypts here are large structures, each housing a staircase that leads to an underground chamber with several stone sarcophagi containing the corpses of the once rich and powerful. Bat colonies that don't wish to be disturbed roost in these dark spaces. The first time a crypt's door opens, the bats spill out and into the night sky, biting and shrieking. Each creature within 10 feet of the door must succeed on a DC 17 Dexterity saving throw or take `dice:4d4|noform|noparens|avg|text(10)` (`4d4`) Piercing damage from the bats.

##### Knight's Tale

While preparing to perform the final rites of Sir Marius Renathyr, the characters may recall what they learned in their exploration of the Crimson Monastery (see chapter The Crimson Monastery). Additionally, Yorgrim can share the following details of his old friend:

- Marius was a valiant knight that served a god of the morning and his king Viktor Denathria, who was like a brother to him.  
- He was a relentless destroyer of evil and protector of the innocent, riding to quests atop his trusted steed named Dawn.  
- The righteous wrath that raged within him could only be soothed by his true love, the elven priestess Lethica Nightborne.  

##### Crimson Kindling

The kindling to light the Crimson Beacon, a dried red rose, is kept in an underground chamber in one of the crypts, but Yorgrim's fragmented memory cannot recall which. As a Study action, a character who observes the crypts and succeeds on a DC 15 Intelligence (History or Religion) check determines the correct crypt by noting the imagery of suns and roses etched in the stone.

##### Crimson Rites

When performing the ritual to light the Crimson Beacon, below are the suggested resolution proficiencies the characters can utilize to perform Marius's final rites:

- Acrobatics or Athletics: Replicate great physical feats of heroism from his knightly quests.  
- History or Religion: Recall the chivalric tenets of his king, his oath, and his god of the morning.  
- Painter's Supplies or Persuasion: Appeal to the personal connections he had with his love.  

##### Creatures of the Night

When the ritual to light the Crimson Beacon begins, five [Night Creatures](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/fiend/night-creature-crookedmoon24.md) emerge from nearby crypts and attack until they are destroyed. For each failed check to locate the kindling, a Ghoul joins the battle.

##### Grotesque Gargoyles

On <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Initiative</span> count 20 (losing <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Initiative</span> ties) of each round during the ritual to light the Crimson Beacon, the hideous gargoyles perched atop the crypts pulse with a terrifying aura. Each creature that isn't a Fiend or an Undead in a 60-foot <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Emanation</span> originating from the brazier must succeed on a DC 15 Wisdom saving throw or have the Frightened condition until the end of its next turn. When Frightened in this way, the creature must immediately take a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Reaction</span> to move up to its <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Speed</span> away from the brazier by the safest route possible.

##### Wishing Fountains

Numerous coins rest below the surface of the water of every fountain—6,000 GP worth of coins in total—remnants of lucky offerings from centuries of mourners. As a Study action, a character who examines the fountains and succeeds on a DC 18 Intelligence (Arcana or Investigation) check determines that there is a boon for tossing in an offering and a punishment for stealing from the fountain.

Each character who tosses 1 GP worth of coins into a fountain gains the benefits of the Bless spell for `dice:1d4|noform|noparens|avg` (`1d4`) hours. Each time a creature takes a coin from a fountain, it must succeed on a DC 18 Wisdom saving throw or be cursed for `dice:1d4|noform|noparens|avg` (`1d4`) hours. While cursed in this way, the creature has <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Disadvantage</span> on <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">D20 Tests</span>.

##### Mourning Urns

The urns that line the garden's pathways contain the ashes of the dead and the sorrow of those they left behind. The lids are fastened tightly but can be removed with a Utilize action and a successful DC 13 Strength check. When opened, the urn emits the sound of inconsolable mourning wails. Any creatures within 15 feet of the urn that can hear the wails must succeed on a DC 13 Wisdom saving throw or drop to 0 <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Hit Points</span>.

##### Night's Tale

While preparing to perform the final rites of Lethica Nightborne, the characters may recall what they learned in their exploration of Memory's Rest Sanatorium (see chapter Memory's Rest). Additionally, Yorgrim can share the following details of his old friend:

- Lethica was a devout priestess of the Nightmother, a goddess of night, healing, and motherhood who takes the form of a barn owl.  
- The gravely ill traveled from far and wide on pilgrimages to have their ailments cured by her and her acolytes with magic and healing brews.  
- She forever wore a mask and hid her face from all until she met Sir Marius Renathyr and experienced true love, finally revealing herself to him.  

##### Violet Kindling

The kindling to light the Violet Beacon, a barn owl feather, is kept in an urn within the garden, but Yorgrim's fragmented memory cannot recall which. As a Study action, a character who observes the urns and succeeds on a DC 15 Intelligence (Nature or Religion) check can find the correct urn by finding the imagery of barn owls and new moons. Opening this urn does not cause mourning wails (see "Mourning Urns" above).

##### Violet Rites

When performing the ritual to light the Violet Beacon, below are the suggested resolution proficiencies the characters can utilize to perform Lethica's final rites:

- Brewer's Supplies or Medicine: Showcase her favored healing techniques, tonics, and salves.  
- Calligrapher's Supplies or Persuasion: Appeal to the personal connection she had with her love.  
- Performance or Religion: Sing the hymns and recite the tenets of the Nightmother.  

##### Black Coach

When the ritual to light the Violet Beacon begins, a [Phantom Hearse](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/undead/phantom-hearse-crookedmoon24.md) rattles down the pathway from deeper into the garden, attacking until it is destroyed.

##### Death Knell

On <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Initiative</span> count 20 (losing <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Initiative</span> ties) of each round during the ritual to light the Violet Beacon, the tolling of a phantom bell booms out over the garden. Each creature that isn't Undead in a 60-foot <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Emanation</span> originating from the brazier must make a DC 15 Wisdom saving throw, taking `dice:2d12|noform|noparens|avg|text(13)` (`2d12`) Necrotic damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.

#### Y6: Potter's Field

> 
> The path turns from a paved walkway to a dirt road that eventually ends at a sprawling expanse of hills topped with countless simple wooden posts, each marking a nameless grave. The cawing and croaking of carrion birds fills the air as the fog rolls along this potter's field. Within the gloom, many amber-hued lights flicker in the distance.

This rolling potter's field is where the poor and forgotten of Wickermoor Hollow are buried, their final resting place only marked by wordless wooden posts. For battles in this location, use the map of Potter's Field.

##### Finding the Brazier

[The Chained Reaper](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/npc/the-chained-reaper-crookedmoon24.md)'s fraying memory cannot recall the location of the Amber Beacon amidst the endless expanse of nameless gravesites. The characters must find the orange coals in the gloom, but trickster spirits known as kackles flit about the hills and carry lanterns that glow with the same color in hopes of luring victims to their death.

To travel through the potter's field, the characters can observe the amber lights in the darkness and make a group DC 18 Wisdom (Insight) check. On a success, they arrive at a brazier sitting amidst a scattering of nameless gravesites that's filled with dim orange coals—the site where the final rites must be performed for Ol' Jericho Sticks (see "Balefire Beacons" earlier in this chapter) to light the Amber Beacon.

On a failure, the characters are deceived and are led to a deep mass grave that appears suddenly out of the gloom. Each character deceived in this way must make a DC 17 Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, a creature falls into the open grave, has the Prone condition, and has the Restrained condition from dozens of skeletal hands that emerge from the earth. A creature that enters the grave willingly must succeed on a DC 15 Strength saving throw or have the Restrained condition. A Restrained creature repeats the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the condition on itself on a success. ##### Shallow Graves

Most of the graves marked by the wooden posts are shallow and swarmed with sickly crows, ravens, and vultures that peck at the rotting remains that protrude from the earth. Approaching a grave disturbs the carrion birds, which scatter into the night sky, shedding their pestilence on those below. Each creature in a 15-foot <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Emanation</span> originating from the grave must succeed on a DC 15 Constitution saving throw or have the Poisoned condition for 1 hour.

After the carrion birds have fled, the grave can be dug up in 10 minutes using a Shovel or other digging tools, or in 1 hour using bare hands.

##### Scarecrow's Ballad

While preparing to perform the final rites of Ol' Jericho Sicks, the characters may recall what they learned in their exploration of Foxwillow and the Fields of the Crow (see chapter Fields of the Crow). Additionally, Yorgrim can share the following details of his old friend:

- Jericho was a kindly scarecrow who, at his heart, was a tunesmith, favoring his banjo, whistling, and singing in his warbling timbre.  
- He loved to travel from town to town and tell stories and fables to the villagers, especially the children who hung on his every word.  
- He was a skilled handyman and craftsman, lending his expertise to build or fix whatever people needed and offering to help tend to the fields.  

##### Amber Kindling

The kindling to light the Amber Beacon, a bundle of straw, is buried in a shallow grave in the potter's field, but [The Chained Reaper](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/npc/the-chained-reaper-crookedmoon24.md) does not remember which. As a Search action, a character who walks among the graves and succeeds on a DC 17 Wisdom (Animal Handling or Perception) check notices the correct grave, which is flocked exclusively with crows.

##### Amber Rites

- Disguise Kit or History: Tell one of his tall tales with a flourish or act out his folktales in costume.  
- Carpenter's Tools or Nature: Showcase his handy skillset or knowledge of all things farming.  

##### Trickster Spirits

When the ritual begins, two [Kackles](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/undead/kackle-crookedmoon24.md) emerge from the gloom and attack until they are destroyed. For each failed check to locate the kindling, a Specter joins the battle.

##### Dancing Lanterns

On <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Initiative</span> count 20 (losing <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Initiative</span> ties) of each round during the ritual, colorful lanterns flit about the fog in hypnotic patterns. Each creature that isn't Undead and that can see the lights must succeed on a DC 15 Wisdom saving throw or have the Charmed condition. While Charmed in this way, the creature is compelled to move into the fog. The creature repeats the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.

##### Acrid Muck

The boggy terrain threatens to swallow the tiles that form the paths between the vaults. For every 10 minutes of travel down the mausoleum lanes, each character must succeed on a DC 17 Strength saving throw or slip into the muck and have the Restrained condition. A Restrained character takes `dice:1d10|noform|noparens|avg|text(5)` (`1d10`) Acid damage at the start of each of its turns. It can take an action to repeat the saving throw, freeing itself on a success.

##### Hexed Vaults

The mausoleums that house the resting dead are jinxed with powerful magic to ward off would-be graverobbers. The heavy stone doors are tightly sealed and can be opened with a successful DC 20 Strength (Athletics) check, but any creature that attempts to open the vault must first make a DC 18 Charisma saving throw as the protective hex is activated. On a failed save, the creature takes `dice:2d10|noform|noparens|avg|text(11)` (`2d10`) Force damage, is pushed 10 feet away from the door, and gains the effects of the Bane spell for `dice:1d4|noform|noparens|avg` (`1d4`) hours. On a successful save, the creature takes half as much damage only.

##### Pirate's Shanty

- He was skilled at swordplay, earning the moniker "The Kutlass," but was also infamous for his clever plans and cunning tricks.  
- Luck always seemed to be on his side, and he excelled at games of chance, eventually mastering the art of fate-bending sorcery.

## Sepulcher of the Shroud

Once all six balefire beacons are lit, the door to the Great Sepulcher (area Y2) can be opened, and the characters can proceed into the heart of the necropolis. When the characters approach the sepulcher after they've lit all the beacons, read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud] 
> 
> As you return to the sepulcher, you see the flickering multi-colored lights of balefire dancing across its great entrance. Crimson, violet, amber, magenta, emerald, and azure flames burn in the sconces affixed to the stone, and not a sliver of the Shroud can be seen escaping from the crack between the doors.
^61b

The tremendous strain on [The Chained Reaper](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/npc/the-chained-reaper-crookedmoon24.md)'s spirit has weakened him greatly, and his ghostly form is visibly more faint and hollow. His fading essence has caused his lantern to dim, which now only sheds <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Bright Light</span> in a 5-foot radius and <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Dim Light</span> for an additional 15 feet (see "Balefire Lantern" and "Gravedigger Actions" earlier in this chapter).

As his mind continues to unravel, he is clearly more affected by the gibbering of the shrouded, and he becomes less aware of the characters, directly engaging them in conversation less and less as they proceed. Despite this, he is determined to discover why the surge of the Shroud is increasing, and he won't turn back.

If Isolde has not been destroyed, Yorgrim commands her to stay behind and guard the door to the sepulcher and ensure that no Undead follow the characters inside. In truth, Yorgrim does not believe he will return from the depths and wants to ensure Isolde can help guide the next caretaker of the cemetery.

Yorgrim, still burdened by his gravestone, heaves a scythe of corrupted balefire and glares at you through skeletal eye sockets. The last of the Fallen falls, and Yorgrim has been transformed into [The Chained Reaper](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/npc/the-chained-reaper-crookedmoon24.md), now little more than a manifestation of death itself and the hunger of the Shroud. Use the map of the Tomb of the Shrouded for this combat.

### Shroud Influence

On <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Initiative</span> count 20 (losing <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Initiative</span> ties), the will of the Shroud causes one of the following effects; it can't use the same effect two rounds in a row:

#### Abominable Spirits

Three [Shrouded](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/undead/shrouded-crookedmoon24.md) (one of each variant) appear in an unoccupied space within the tomb (GM's choice). They act immediately and on <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Initiative</span> count 20 on subsequent rounds. The shrouded vanish when they die, when this effect happens again, or when the Shroud tear is sealed.

#### Oppressive Gloom

Dense blue-green mist spills forth from the tear and fills the room, making the tomb Heavily Obscured until <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Initiative</span> count 20 on the following round.

#### Tide of Death

Waves of shrieking fog burst forth from the malevolent souls. Strength Saving Throw: DC 15, each enemy of [The Chained Reaper](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/npc/the-chained-reaper-crookedmoon24.md) within a 5-foot <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Emanation</span> originating from the reaper and any Shrouded. Failure: The target is pushed up to 15 feet straight away from the source.

### Phase 1: Cold Iron

The countless chains that rattle from [The Chained Reaper](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/npc/the-chained-reaper-crookedmoon24.md) slither out like tentacles, seeking to make his foes experience the burden and imprisonment he's suffered. Three [Shrouded](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/undead/shrouded-crookedmoon24.md) (one of each variant) appear in unoccupied spaces within the tomb and attack with the Chained Reaper.

#### The Jailer

Once per turn, whenever the Chained Reaper is hit by a melee or ranged attack roll, a chain shoots out toward the attacker. The attacker must succeed on a DC 15 Strength saving throw or have the Grappled condition (escape DC 13) until the end of the attacker's next turn.

#### Corrupted Braziers

Corrupted balefire roars in eight braziers in the tomb, radiating with the soul-sapping power of the Shroud. A creature that passes through the fire must succeed on a DC 13 Charisma saving throw or gain 1 Exhaustion level. A creature makes this save only once during a turn.

#### Azure Defiance

When the Chained Reaper is Bloodied, faint wisps of [The Chained Reaper](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/npc/the-chained-reaper-crookedmoon24.md)'s azure light flit through his form as the hold of the Shroud on his soul lessens, causing the malevolent eldritch mist to lash out. The voices within the Shroud let out sickening shrieks in unison. Each character must succeed on a DC 13 Wisdom saving throw or gain the Stunned condition until the end of their next turn. This transitions the battle to Phase 2.

#### Corrupted Beacons

The braziers' corrupted balefire rages brighter. The saving throw DC of the braziers increases to 15, and a creature that ends its turn within 5 feet of a brazier must make the save.

#### Fallen Effigies

Six stone coffins rest upon the tomb's platforms, five of which contain simple adult-sized effigies of the other Fallen. The sixth is empty. As [The Chained Reaper](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/npc/the-chained-reaper-crookedmoon24.md)'s azure hue returns to [The Chained Reaper](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/npc/the-chained-reaper-crookedmoon24.md) (see "Azure Defiance" above), these effigies begin to glow with their respective balefire colors. Characters who succeed on a DC 13 Intelligence (Arcana or Investigation) check realize the effigies can serve as kindling and be used to cleanse the corrupted balefire braziers.

As a Utilize action, a character can pick up one of the effigies and place it into the brazier while standing within 5 feet of it, cleansing the brazier and removing the effects of "Corrupted Beacons" from it. The calming and kind voice of Yorgrim's friend calls out to him as the effigy burns, dealing 10 Psychic damage to the Chained Reaper.

Additionally, the flame turns the color of the Fallen effigy that was used to purify it (see "Balefire Beacons" earlier in this chapter), shedding warding light in a 20-foot radius around the brazier. If the Chained Reaper or any shrouded are fully within the light of this cleansed balefire, they have <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Disadvantage</span> on <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">D20 Tests</span>.

#### Devouring Balefire

The Shroud and its corruptive balefire seeks to devour the souls of all who oppose it. Constitution saving throw: DC 15, a creature that starts its turn within 10 feet of the Chained Reaper. Failure: `dice:1d6|noform|noparens|avg|text(3)` (`1d6`) Necrotic damage.

### A Light in the Darkness

When [The Chained Reaper](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/npc/the-chained-reaper-crookedmoon24.md) is reduced to 0 <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Hit Points</span>, read or paraphrase the following:

> 
> The colossal reaper before you gyrates violently, not able to maintain its form. Its chains break, its tombstone crumbles, its scythe snaps, and the ghastly gray and blue-green mist bursts from within in an explosion of azure light. Thousands of shrieking voices scream out and are silenced until one remains—Yorgrim's—emanating from the huge, ornate lantern before you, now flickering with azure flame. "Perform my rites... Seal the tear..."
^64b

The Chained Reaper is destroyed, but the tear in the Shroud is still open and must be sealed to put an end to the invasion of the shrieking dead once and for all. Any character who considers [The Chained Reaper](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/npc/the-chained-reaper-crookedmoon24.md)'s words, observes the lantern left behind from the reaper's scythe, and succeeds on a DC 13 Intelligence (Arcana or Religion) check can deduce that Yorgrim is the final Fallen whose final rites must be performed.

#### Azure Rites

Despite the destruction of the Chained Reaper, the battle for the fate of Maidenmist Cemetery is not over, <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Initiative</span> order is maintained, and the Shroud still acts against the characters (see "Shroud Influence" earlier in this chapter). Yorgrim's final rites must be performed by fulfilling the "Honor the Fallen" step of the 1-minute ritual (see "Balefire Beacons" earlier in this chapter). Below are the suggested resolution proficiencies the characters can utilize to perform Yorgrim's final rites:

- Arcana or Mason's Tools: Showcase his abilities in tending to the dead, performing burial rites, or communing with the spirit world.  
- History or Religion: Recall his dutiful commitment to the Shadestone Clan and the performance of his ritualistic duties.  
- Performance or Persuasion: Perform a funeral dirge and extol the bond he had with his people, his friends, or his hound Isolde.  

After six successful checks, read or paraphrase the following:

A moment later, the lantern erupts into a blaze of soothing balefire, filling the tomb with light that becomes so bright it blinds you. Hissing shrieks of tormented and twisted souls ring out for a moment before sizzling into nothing. When your vision returns, the tear into the Shroud is gone.

## Conclusion: Don't Fear the Reaper

Peace returns to Maidenmist Cemetery, and the six balefire beacons remain lit as a final reminder of the six wayward souls who once bore good intentions but fell to the wickedness that lingers and lurks within every mortal heart.

If Isolde was not destroyed by the Shroud, she mourns the loss of [The Chained Reaper](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/npc/the-chained-reaper-crookedmoon24.md) but continues her eternal duty bound to Maidenmist Cemetery. She awaits the next soul brave enough to take up the mantle of the cemetery's sexton. As the characters leave the sepulcher, she approaches them and gives them thankful licks, attempting to guide them to the Shadestone Sanctuary (area Y9) for a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Long Rest</span> before leaving the cemetery.

### Back in Wickermoor Village

The nightly assaults of the shambling dead cease, and uneasy quiet returns to Wickermoor Village. When Mori Shade discovers the news about the loss of [The Chained Reaper](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/npc/the-chained-reaper-crookedmoon24.md), she grieves in her own stoic way but goes about seeking a new caretaker for Maidenmist Cemetery. She considers taking up the role herself if she cannot find a suitable volunteer or apprentice.

Adela Druskenvald approaches the characters with a look of relief and tells them that she had seen a vision of a massive swarm of spirits tearing through Wickermoor Village, and she incorrectly guesses that this was a future that was stopped with the defeat of [The Chained Reaper](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/npc/the-chained-reaper-crookedmoon24.md).
