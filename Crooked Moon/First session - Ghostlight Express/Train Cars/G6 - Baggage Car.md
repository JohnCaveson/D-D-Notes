### Druskenvald's Luggage
- If it tampered with then Phillip comes to investigate if they are moved or tampered with. Instinctively knows where it is at all times. Cases are protected with *Arcane Lock* (DC 20 Arcana Check)

### Sleeping Tiger
- A [[Wildpyre]] in the form of a tiger made of ghostly green flame slumbers behind a stack of baggage, filling the car with grumbling, bestial snoring.
- Dealing damage to the wildpyre wakes it, and it attackes until the characters leave the car, or until the creature that damaged the tiger is reduced to 0 Hit Points whereupon it returns to its makeshift den and goes back to sleep. 
- Removing the ring reduces the Wildpyre to 0 Hit Points and vanishes in a swirl of embers that pour into the ring. 
### Relevant checks

Wildpyre
- Sleight of Hand (Dexterity)
	- As a Utilize action, a character can slip the ring free with a successful DC 15.
		- On a failed check, the wildpyre stirs and lashes out dealing **7 (2d6) Fire damage**
	- Can be used to free the [[Harlequin]] in [[G7 - Circus Car]]

Perception (Wisdom) or Investigation (Intelligence) 
- As a Search action, on a 15 each of the following can be found but only once.
	- 75 SP
	- A delicate silver ring set with a ruby, worth 20 GP
	- 10 sets of varying Traveler's Clothes
	- a pair of [[goggles-of-night|Goggles of Night]]
- **Disclaimer: a character who spends 10 minutes searching finds everything with no ability checks required**
