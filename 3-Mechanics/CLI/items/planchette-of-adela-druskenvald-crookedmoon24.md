# Planchette of Adela Druskenvald
*Wondrous item (requires attunement)*  
![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheGiddyLimit/homebrew-img/refs/heads/main/img/CrookedMoon24/Items/Planchette%20of%20Adela%20Druskenvald.webp#right)

- **Properties**: [Fabled Heirloom](/3-Mechanics/CLI/item-properties.md#Fabled%20Heirloom)

A small, heart-shaped silver pointer hangs from a silver necklace. It is inscribed with occult symbols. When placed on an open book or other assortment of letters, the air nearby feels heavy and cold.

While wearing the planchette, you can use it as a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Spellcasting Focus</span> for your spells, you gain a bonus to Intelligence and Wisdom ability checks determined by the planchette's rarity, and you can use the following properties.
