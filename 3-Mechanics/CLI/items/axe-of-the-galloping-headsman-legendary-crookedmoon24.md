# Axe of the Galloping Headsman (Legendary)
*Weapon (battleaxe), legendary (requires attunement)*  
![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheGiddyLimit/homebrew-img/refs/heads/main/img/CrookedMoon24/Items/Axe%20of%20the%20Galloping%20Headsman.webp#right)

- **Damage**:
  - One-handed: 1d8 slashing
  - Two-handed: 1d10 slashing
- **Properties**: [Fabled Heirloom](/3-Mechanics/CLI/item-properties.md#Fabled%20Heirloom), [Versatile](/3-Mechanics/CLI/item-properties.md#Versatile)
- **Weight**: 4.0 lbs.

This axe has a wickedly curved head, honed to a razor's edge and lined with dark fire. It's handle is wrapped in cracked leather, and the entirety is etched with malevolent runes.

You gain a +3 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon determined by the axe's rarity. You can also use the following properties.

## Fiery Smite (Rare+)

This axe deals an extra `dice:1d6|noform|noparens|avg` (`1d6`) Fire damage on a hit. If the target is a creature that damaged you since the end of your last turn, the extra damage becomes `dice:1d10|noform|noparens|avg` (`1d10`).

## Mark of Guilt (Very Rare+)

When you hit a creature with the axe, you can mark it for punishment (no action required). You know the direction and distance to the creature. If it leaves this plane of existence, you know the destination plane and the mark ends. The mark otherwise ends when this property is used again, or when the target dies.

## Sense Guilt (Very Rare+)

You can take a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Bonus Action</span> to hold the blade of the axe to a creature's neck (if it has one), and it must succeed on a Charisma saving throw (DC = 16 + the axe's bonus) or you learn something the creature feels guilty about. Once this property is used, the target is immune to it for 24 hours.

## Dark Binding (Legendary)

Once during your turn when you hit a creature with the axe, you can immediately take a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Bonus Action</span> to force the target to succeed on a DC 19 Strength saving throw or have the Paralyzed and Prone conditions until the start of your next turn. If its body shape allows it, the target kneels and presents its neck.

## Executioner's Blade (Legendary)

When you attack a creature with the Prone condition using the axe, you score a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Critical Hit</span> on a roll of 19 or 20 on the `dice:d20|noform|noparens|avg` (`d20`). On a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Critical Hit</span>, the axe deals an extra `dice:3d6|noform|noparens|avg` (`3d6`) Fire damage, and if the creature is left with 20 or fewer <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Hit Points</span> after taking this damage, it must succeed on a DC 10 Constitution saving throw or die.

*Source: The Crooked Moon (2024) p. 595*
