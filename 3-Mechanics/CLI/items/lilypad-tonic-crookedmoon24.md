# Lilypad Tonic
*Potion, common*  
![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheGiddyLimit/homebrew-img/refs/heads/main/img/CrookedMoon24/Items/Lilypad%20Tonic.webp#right)


When you drink this potion, you gain the effect of the Polymorph spell (no Concentration required), turning you into a Frog.

This potion is found in a clay jar shaped and painted like a large frog, with a cork stopper in its back.

## Heightened Potency

The potion has six doses instead of one.

*Source: The Crooked Moon (2024) p. 615*
