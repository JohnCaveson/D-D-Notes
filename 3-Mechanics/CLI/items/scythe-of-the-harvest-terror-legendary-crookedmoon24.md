# Scythe of the Harvest Terror (Legendary)
*Weapon (halberd), legendary (requires attunement)*  
![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheGiddyLimit/homebrew-img/refs/heads/main/img/CrookedMoon24/Items/Scythe%20of%20the%20Harvest%20Terror.webp#right)

- **Damage**: 1d10 slashing
- **Properties**: [Fabled Heirloom](/3-Mechanics/CLI/item-properties.md#Fabled%20Heirloom), [Heavy](/3-Mechanics/CLI/item-properties.md#Heavy), [Reach](/3-Mechanics/CLI/item-properties.md#Reach), [Two-Handed](/3-Mechanics/CLI/item-properties.md#Two-Handed)
- **Weight**: 6.0 lbs.

This menacing scythe with a rusted blade was wielded by the Harvest Terror, a demon of secrets and fear. The scythe appears old and worn, with a handle wrapped in ancient, weathered cloth. The blade is etched with hatch marks, tallying its kills.

You gain a +3 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls with this magic weapon determined by its rarity. While holding the scythe, you can use the following properties.

## Feed on Fear (Rare+)

Once during your turn, when you attack with the scythe and hit a creature that has the Frightened condition, it takes an extra `dice:1d10|noform|noparens|avg` (`1d10`) (Legendary: `dice:2d10|noform|noparens|avg` (`2d10`)) Psychic damage.

## Sweep of Dread (Rare+)

You can cast Fear (save DC = 16 + the scythe's bonus, no Concentration required) from the scythe. Once this property is used, it can't be used again until the next dusk.

## Harvest of the Hidden Word (Very Rare+)

As a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Bonus Action</span>, you can force one creature you can see that has the Frightened condition to make a Charisma saving throw (DC = 16 + the scythe's bonus). On a failed save, you learn one fact that it deliberately keeps secret. The creature is then immune to this property for 24 hours.

## Seed of Terror (Very Rare+)

Immediately after you hit a creature with the scythe, you can take a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Bonus Action</span> to force one creature within 30 feet of the creature you hit to make a Wisdom saving throw (DC = 16 + the Scythe's bonus). On a failed save, the creature gains the Frightened condition until the end of your next turn.

## Lurking Terror (Legendary)

When a creature you can see that has the Frightened condition ends its turn within 60 feet of you, you can take a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Reaction</span> to teleport up to 60 feet to an unoccupied space the Frightened creature can see.

*Source: The Crooked Moon (2024) p. 603*
