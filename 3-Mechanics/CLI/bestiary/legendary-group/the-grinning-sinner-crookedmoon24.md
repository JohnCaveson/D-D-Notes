# The Grinning Sinner

## Regional Effects
_Source: The Crooked Moon (2024)_

The region containing the Grinning Sinner's lair is changed by its presence, creating the following effects:

- **Fate's Wheel.** Within 1 mile of the lair, if the number rolled on a die to restore <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Hit Points</span> is even, increase the number rolled by 1. If the number rolled is odd, reduce it by 1.  
- **Run of Bad Luck.** Within 1 mile of the lair, a creature that rolls a 1 on a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">D20 Test</span> must subtract `dice:1d4|noform|noparens|avg` (`1d4`) from its next <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">D20 Test</span>.  

If the Grinning Sinner dies or moves his lair elsewhere, these effects end immediately.
