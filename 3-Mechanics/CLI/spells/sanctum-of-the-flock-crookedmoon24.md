# Sanctum of the Flock
*3rd-level, Transmutation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 minute
- **Range:** 5 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (powdered silver worth 25+ GP, which the spell consumes)
- **Duration:** 24 hours

You create a 20-foot-radius <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Sphere</span> of faint, shimmering light centered on a point within range. You and a number of creatures of your choice up to your spellcasting ability modifier (minimum of 1 other creature) can absorb empowering magic from the <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Sphere</span>. When any of the chosen creatures finish a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Long Rest</span> in the area, the <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Sphere</span> vanishes, and those creatures gain `dice:2d10|noform|noparens|avg` (`2d10`) <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Temporary Hit Points</span>, and for the remainder of the duration, they have <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Advantage</span> on Charisma checks that use one of their skill proficiencies. Chosen creatures who don't finish a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Long Rest</span> in the <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Sphere</span> gain no benefit.
