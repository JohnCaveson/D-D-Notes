Dani was born to [Walter](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/npc/walter-jenkin-crookedmoon24.md) and [Gilly](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/npc/gilly-jenkin-crookedmoon24.md) within a couple of weeks of their move to the mayoral manor to serve [Mayor Somerton's](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/npc/wendel-somerton-crookedmoon24.md) family. Though she cannot yet control her abilities, her bond with the powers of the Old Ways grows.
