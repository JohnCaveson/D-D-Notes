Type: Level 4 one-shot
Setting: Casino

Players awake on first floor of a casino. They must do a mix of combat and games to progress to the sub-bosses and ultimately the Casino Manager. Death is only a misstep in this casino. Any adventurer who dies will return and be rewelcomed to the establishment like nothing happened. The only way out is to defeat the Manager and break the psychic lock.

The heroes awaken by hearing the voice of the Manager in their heads.
