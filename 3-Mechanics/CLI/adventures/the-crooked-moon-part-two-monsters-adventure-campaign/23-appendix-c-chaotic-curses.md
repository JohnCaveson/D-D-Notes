# Appendix C: Chaotic Curses
*Source: The Crooked Moon Part Two: Monsters & Adventure Campaign, p. 618* 

![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheGiddyLimit/homebrew-img/refs/heads/main/img/CrookedMoon24/Adventure/486-22-001.chaotic-curses-opener.webp#center)

This section presents 156 Chaotic Curses, which can be optionally used to add a bit of unexpected chaos and humor to your game. Normally, curses are lasting or permanent negative conditions that require magic or significant effort to end. Chaotic Curses deviate from such curses in several ways:

## Fleeting

These curses are designed to last a brief amount of real-world time (15-30 minutes or less) and should fade before they become tedious.

## Roleplay-Focused

They add whimsy, surprise, or mayhem to the game without heavily impacting mechanics or combat.

## Unpredictable

These effects alter the characters' beliefs, appearance, and actions in humorous or unexpected ways.

Chaotic Curses are entirely optional and should be used with discretion. Groups that prefer less active roleplay or serious folk horror atmosphere should avoid these curses. For those that favor sillier games, even while confronting the many horrors described in this book, these curses add levity and entertainment.

## Using Chaotic Curses

To distribute Chaotic Curses, use one of the following methods:

![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheGiddyLimit/homebrew-img/refs/heads/main/img/CrookedMoon24/Adventure/487-22-002.weird-pangolin.webp#center)

### Tarot Deck

Shuffle a tarot deck and draw a card. Each card is associated with a curse (see the table below), and the card's position—upright or reversed—determines which curse takes effect. The card can then be removed from the deck permanently or reshuffled into the deck each time.

### Rolling Dice

Alternatively, you can roll a `dice:d100|noform|noparens|avg` (`d100`) and check the corresponding curse on the table below.

### Starting and Ending Curses

Chaotic Curses are best left up to the GM's discretion, so they occur at unexpected times rather than during intense or pivotal moments. However, you can use mechanical triggers to determine when a curse begins and ends. Here are some ideas that might trigger Chaotic Curses:

- Rolling a natural 1 on a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">D20 Test</span>  
- Whenever combat ends  
- Finishing a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Long Rest</span>  
- Touching a cursed object  
- Entering a specific location  

Ending curses can also be handled in a variety of ways:

- Expending <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Heroic Inspiration</span>  
- Remove Curse or similar magic  
- Healing magic above a certain threshold  
- Finishing a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Short Rest</span>  
- GM pity

> [!note] Tarot Interpretations
> 
> The tarot deck is simply used for randomization. There is no intended connection between the card's traditional meaning and its curse effect.
