# Sword of the Crimson Abbot (Very Rare)
*Weapon (longsword), very rare (requires attunement)*  
![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheGiddyLimit/homebrew-img/refs/heads/main/img/CrookedMoon24/Items/Sword%20of%20the%20Crimson%20Abbot.webp#right)

- **Damage**:
  - One-handed: 1d8 slashing
  - Two-handed: 1d10 slashing
- **Properties**: [Fabled Heirloom](/3-Mechanics/CLI/item-properties.md#Fabled%20Heirloom), [Versatile](/3-Mechanics/CLI/item-properties.md#Versatile)
- **Weight**: 3.0 lbs.

This powerful, fiendish sword was once wielded by the Crimson Abbot. While it was created as a gift that brought evil and malice to the corrupted former knight, it has lost its connections to the Crimson Rose. However, its evil origins still linger within. The sword is sleek and polished, with a blade that absorbs light. A blood-red ruby on the hilt is fitted within a setting of thorns and roses.

You gain a +2 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls with this magic weapon determined by the sword's rarity. While holding the sword, you can use the following properties.

## Blood Drain (Rare+)

When you hit a creature with the sword, it takes an extra `dice:1d6|noform|noparens|avg` (`1d6`) Necrotic damage.

## Irresistible Hunger (Very Rare+)

Necrotic damage you deal ignores <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Resistance</span>.

## Vampiric Charm (Very Rare+)

You can cast Charm Person (save DC = 16 + the sword's bonus, no Concentration required) from the sword. Once this property is used, it can't be used again until the next dusk.

*Source: The Crooked Moon (2024) p. 605*
