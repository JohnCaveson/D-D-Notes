# Revelry Suit
*Medium armor (hide armor), rare (requires attunement)*  
![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheGiddyLimit/homebrew-img/refs/heads/main/img/CrookedMoon24/Items/Revelry%20Suit.webp#right)

- **Armor Class**: 12 + Dex modifier (max of +2)
- **Properties**: [Cursed Curio](/3-Mechanics/CLI/item-properties.md#Cursed%20Curio)
- **Weight**: 12.0 lbs.

This bulky suit is made from natural materials, including fur, canvas, and straw. Its mask is exceptionally oversized and shaped into a sun, smiling in an unsettling way. While you wear this armor, you gain a +2 bonus to AC, and it grants the following properties.

## Dancing Dodge

When you fail a Dexterity saving throw, if your <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Speed</span> isn't 0, you can use this property to succeed instead. Once used, this property can't be used again until the next dusk.

## Wild Steps

As a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Bonus Action</span>, you can move up to your <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Speed</span> without provoking Opportunity Attacks.

## Dancing Curse

When you roll <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Initiative</span>, you must make a DC 0 Wisdom saving throw. On a failed save, you gain the Incapacitated condition, and your <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Speed</span> is 0 until the start of your next turn as you perform a folk dance. When you use the Dancing Dodge or Wild Steps properties, the DC of the saving throw increases by 2. The DC of this curse can't increase again until you finish a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Long Rest</span>.

*Source: The Crooked Moon (2024) p. 611*
