# Bell of the Dusk Mother (Rare)
*Wondrous item, rare (requires attunement)*  
![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheGiddyLimit/homebrew-img/refs/heads/main/img/CrookedMoon24/Items/Bell%20of%20the%20Dusk%20Mother.webp#right)

- **Properties**: [Fabled Heirloom](/3-Mechanics/CLI/item-properties.md#Fabled%20Heirloom)

This heavy iron handbell is elaborately decorated with filigree. Its handle is a large ring, and its clapper hangs from a chain within. Its only sound is ever-mournful tolling.

While holding the bell, you can use it as a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Spellcasting Focus</span> for your spells, you gain a +1 bonus to spell attack rolls and your spell save DC determined by its rarity, and you can use the following properties.

## Dolorous Tolling (Rare+)

As a Magic action, you can ring the bell, producing an ominous tolling. Creatures of your choice in a 30-foot <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Emanation</span> originating from you must make a Wisdom saving throw (DC = 16 + the bell's bonus). On a failed save, a target takes `dice:3d10|noform|noparens|avg` (`3d10`) Psychic damage, and for 1 minute, when it makes an attack roll or saving throw, it must subtract `dice:1d4|noform|noparens|avg` (`1d4`) from the total. Once this property is used, it can't be used again until the next dusk.

*Source: The Crooked Moon (2024) p. 596*
