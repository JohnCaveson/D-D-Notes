# Appendix A: Bestiary
*Source: The Crooked Moon Part One: Player Options & Campaign Setting, p. 485* 

![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheGiddyLimit/homebrew-img/refs/heads/main/img/CrookedMoon24/Book/128-20-081.familiars.webp#center)

The creatures presented in this section are special beings who can form bonds with another and serve as a familiar—not just for a spellcaster, but for anyone. This bond creates a deep, magical connection and isn't entered into lightly by these familiars. They demand respectful treatment, but in return offer loyal companionship and service. If the familiar is mistreated or wronged, it can sever the bond and may become a thorn in the side of their former companion.

Learning to form a familiar bond with one of these creatures isn't necessarily common knowledge, and you can treat discovering how to perform the ritual as a secret, as discussed in the "Monster Secrets" section in the in Appendix A of The Crooked Moon Part Two.

## Familiar Bond

When a creature performs a 10-minute ritual using materials worth 100+ GP (noted in each familiar's subtitle), the familiar can form a magical bond with that creature to serve as its familiar. The bond ends when the creature dies, or when the familiar decides to end it (no action required). While the bond lasts, the creature and the familiar gain the following benefits:

## Bonded Sympathy

The familiar can choose to exclude the bonded creature from any area of effect it creates, such as a damaging <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Sphere</span> or an <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Emanation</span> like the [goatstealer's](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/monstrosity/goatstealer-crookedmoon24.md) Stench trait. Additionally, if the familiar has another feature that creates a bond (such as the [devil's glove's](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/plant/devils-glove-crookedmoon24.md) Symbiosis Bonus Action), that bond forms as part of the familiar bond.

## Channel Magic

When the bonded creature casts a spell with a range of touch while within 100 feet of the familiar, the familiar can take a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Reaction</span> to deliver the touch when the spell is cast.

## Telepathic Connection

While the bonded creature and the familiar are within 100 feet of each other, they can communicate telepathically. Additionally, as a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Bonus Action</span>, the creature can see and hear through the familiar's senses until the start of its next turn, gaining the benefit of any special senses the familiar has.

## Revival

If the familiar dies while the bond exists, the bonded creature can restore the familiar to life with all its <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Hit Points</span> by performing a 1-hour version of the Familiar Bond ritual (no further research required to learn this version).
