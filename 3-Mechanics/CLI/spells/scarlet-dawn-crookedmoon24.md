# Scarlet Dawn
*3rd-level, Evocation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 120 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

Crimson light shines down in a 20-foot-radius, 60-foot-high <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Cylinder</span> centered on a point within range. Each creature in that area that isn't a Construct or Undead must make a Constitution saving throw, taking `dice:4d10|noform|noparens|avg` (`4d10`) Necrotic damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one. Constructs and Undead in the area regain `dice:4d10|noform|noparens|avg` (`4d10`) <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Hit Points</span>.

If any of this spell's area overlaps with an area of <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Darkness</span> created by a spell of level 3 or lower, that other spell is dispelled.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** The damage increases by `dice:1d10|noform|noparens|avg` (`1d10`) and the level of spell that can be dispelled increases by 1 for each spell slot level above 3.
