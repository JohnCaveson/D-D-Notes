# Hide of the Brimstone Behemoth (Rare)
*Wondrous item, rare (requires attunement)*  
![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheGiddyLimit/homebrew-img/refs/heads/main/img/CrookedMoon24/Items/Hide%20of%20the%20Brimstone%20Behemoth.webp#right)

- **Properties**: [Fabled Heirloom](/3-Mechanics/CLI/item-properties.md#Fabled%20Heirloom)

This cloak was made from the hide of a reptilian magma Fiend, incomparable in its destructive power. Flame now shies away from it, for one who has mastered such a titanic creature can master its element.

While wearing this cloak, you gain a +1 bonus to <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Armor Class</span> and Constitution saving throws determined by the cloak's rarity, and you can use the following properties.

## Deep-Dweller (Rare+)

You have <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Resistance</span> to Fire damage and a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Burrow Speed</span> equal to your <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Speed</span>. Once during your turn when you emerge from burrowing, each creature in a 10-foot <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Emanation</span> originating from you must make a Dexterity saving throw (DC = 16 + the hide's bonus), taking `dice:2d8|noform|noparens|avg` (`2d8`) Fire damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.

## Infernal Roar (Rare+)

You can cast Fear (save DC = 16 + the hide's bonus) from the cloak. This property can be used three times, regaining all expended uses daily at dusk.

*Source: The Crooked Moon (2024) p. 599*
