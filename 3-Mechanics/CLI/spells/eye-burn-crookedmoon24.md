# Eye Burn
*cantrip, Transmutation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Reaction
- **Range:** 30 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (a smear of camphor)
- **Duration:** 1 round

You sting the eyes of a creature attacking you. The target must make a Constitution saving throw (a creature with Blindsight or <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Immunity</span> to the Blinded condition succeeds automatically). On a failed save, the target has the Blinded condition until the end of the turn. On a successful save, it must subtract `dice:1d4|noform|noparens|avg` (`1d4`) from the triggering attack roll. The target automatically succeeds on its saves against your subsequent casting of this spell for 24 hours.
